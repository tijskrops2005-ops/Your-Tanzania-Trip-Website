Deep Sea Fishing – Full day

Board an engined boat to enjoy a full-day deep sea fishing in the turquoise waters of the Indian Ocean, right off the shore of Zanzibar. With a captain who knows the area well, you’ll have the opportunity to fish for black and blue marlin, acrobatic sailfish, dorado, yellowfin tuna, wahoo, barracuda, or kingfish, depending on the season. An unforgettable experience!

Important details

  • Boat: Engined, accommodates up to 5 guests.
