Two-day hike from Ngorongoro to Lake Natron

In this two-day hike, you walk from Ngorongoro’s Empakaai Crater to the mesmerizing Lake Natron, guided by local Maasai, a park ranger and some donkeys for your luggage. The first day takes you through a wonderful deserted landscape and some very remote Maasai villages, after which you spend the night in a tent your guides set up for you. The next day, you walk past the volcano Ol Doinyo Lengai to the flocks of flamingos at Lake Natron. An extraordinary experience!

Important details

  • Ol Doinyo Lengai is a sacred site for the Maasai
