Two-day hike on Mount Kilimanjaro (Marangu route)

This two-day hike up the famous Mount Kilimanjaro is perfect for those wanting to experience Mount Kili without going to the top. Following the Marangu route this hike takes you 4 – 6 hours up to the Mandara Huts on the first day and 3 – 4 hours up to Horombo on day 2. You pass through jungles and rocky landscapes, see Kibo on clear days and enjoy amazing views in general. From Horombo back down to the Marangu gate takes another 5 – 6 hours, so make sure you wear proper hiking boots!

Important details

  • Duration: 2 days
  • Price on request
  • Walking shoes and warm clothes required
  • Accompanied by experienced guide
