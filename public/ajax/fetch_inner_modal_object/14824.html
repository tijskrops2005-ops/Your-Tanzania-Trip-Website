Sunset Cruise Mafia Island

After a wonderful day on mesmerizing Mafia Island, there is nothing better than to jump aboard a traditional dhow for an impressive Mafian sunset at sea. The dhow departs at 5:30 pm with a waiter and drinks on board, after which the captain gently sails you through Mafia’s aquamarine waters. Once he has found a spot to settle, he drops the anchor and you can raise your glass to a sky filled with striking red, pink, purple and orange just above an endless ocean. An amazing sight!

Important details

  • Duration: 1.5 hours
  • Minimum 2 pax
