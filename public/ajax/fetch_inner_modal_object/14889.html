Zanzibar cooking lesson

Bring the taste of Zanzibar home with a Swahili cooking lesson! This is a great way to learn more about the Zanzibar way of life, as you first go shopping for ingredients at the local market, after which you learn to cook various Zanzibar-style dishes like chapatis, a curry with fresh coconut or yummy pilau rice. Along the way, you learn all about the exotic spices Zanzibar is famous for, and how to integrate them in your cooking. And of course you get to taste afterwards!

Important details

  • Duration: 2 hours
  • Join a daily cooking class for a maximum of 4 people
  • Price on request
