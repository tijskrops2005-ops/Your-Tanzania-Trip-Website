Bush dinner in the Serengeti

A bush dinner right on the plains of Serengeti National Park is the ultimate romantic dining experience! During a bush dinner, a delicious meal is served on the savannah plains under a sky glittering with stars. Enjoy the crackling of a campfire while listening to the animals scurrying in the dark around you. Usually, tables are set up just outside the camp, but still close enough to be safe.

important details
