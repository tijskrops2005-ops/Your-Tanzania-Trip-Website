Stone Town Tour – Half day

Get to know the rich history of Zanzibar by following the trail of the sultans, slaves, and explorers through the winding streets of Stone Town, accompanied by a guide. Visit iconic landmarks such as the former slave market, the House of Wonders and the Sultans’ Palace Museum and learn about the events that have shaped Zanzibar. The tour also gives a glimpse of modern life, with visits to lively spice markets, a vibrant fish market, and bustling fruit and vegetable stalls.
