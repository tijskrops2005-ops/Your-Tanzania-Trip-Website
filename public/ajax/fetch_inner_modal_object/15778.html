Semi-Submarine Experience – the Classic Reef Tour to corals & reef

Board a semi-submarine and experience an immersive underwater adventure around the beautiful Bawe Island, in the middle of the Indian Ocean on the west of Zanzibar’s capital Stone Town. A semi-submarine vessel stays afloat but has space below the waterline featuring underwater windows that allow you to explore the reef from the comfort of your seat!

Important details

  • Duration: 2 hours
  • Minimum age: 5 years
  • Price on request
