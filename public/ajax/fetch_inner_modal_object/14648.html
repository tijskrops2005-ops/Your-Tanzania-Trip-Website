Night game drive in Lake Manyara National Park

A night game drive in Lake Manyara National Park gives your safari a completely different twist – animals that hide during the day, now become visible in the headlights of your jeep. Mind you, there are no street lights in a national park, so the lights of your jeep are the only ones you’ll see. Experience the thrill of the night, while your guide tells you all about the porcupines, civet cats, bush babies, hippos, lions and leopards that can be spotted during this time.

Important details

  • Duration: 2 hrs (8pm – 10pm)
  • Price on request
  • Accommodation: preferable in the park or Mto Wa Mbu
