Tanzanite Museum Arusha

Tanzanite is one of the world’s rarest gemstones, mined only in Tanzania. If you have an interest in shiny gemstones, then a visit to the Tanzanite Museum Arusha is for you. During a visit, you can see and touch this beautiful violet-blue gemstone, and learn all about how the stone is discovered, hewed, and processed in an imitation mine shaft. You can even buy your very own specimen to bring home!

Important details

  • Duration: Half day
  • Price: Free
  • Transport: price on request
