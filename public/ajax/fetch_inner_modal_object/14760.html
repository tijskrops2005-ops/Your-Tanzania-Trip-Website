Chumbe Island – day trip

Chumbe Island is an award-winning private nature reserve just outside the west coast of Zanzibar. The island has an eco-lodge, some historical monuments, a fully protected coral reef sanctuary, and a forest reserve. During this full-day visit, you go snorkelling over one of the most diverse coral reefs of East Africa, take a guided walk through the forest, climb the historical lighthouse and get a tour of an eco-bungalow. A fresh lunch and boat transport are of course included!
