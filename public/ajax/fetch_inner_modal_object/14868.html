Climb Mount Longido

Mount Longido is located on the edge of the town of Longido near the border with Kenya. It is possible to climb Mount Longido in one day, but it is definitely worth spending the night on the mountain and then reaching the summit on the second day. The summit is at an altitude of 2637m above sea level. The hike goes through a wooded area that leads to a rain forest higher up, and buffaloes are often seen here. Maasai warriors are your guide during the hike.
