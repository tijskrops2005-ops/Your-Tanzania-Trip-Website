Visit a coffee plantation in Arusha

The area around Moshi and Arusha is known for its many coffee plantations, and you can find many lodges connected to one. These plantations make for an interesting visit, as you can see how coffee beans grow, are harvested and processed into your favourite drink of the day. Outside the harvest season, you learn all about the different types of beans, what exactly makes the delicious coffee taste and how the beans are processed. And of course, you get to taste some of the most delicious, freshly brewed kahawa.
