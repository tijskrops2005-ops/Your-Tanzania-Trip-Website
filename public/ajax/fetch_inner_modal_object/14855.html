Hot air balloon flight in Serengeti

A balloon safari in the iconic Serengeti National Park will make your Tanzania safari a memorable experience. You rise at sunrise and gently fly over the endless plains of the Serengeti. The hot air balloon floats at treetop height above the baobabs and acacias so that you can see the animals nearby. Watching the wildlife below from your balloon is a fascinating and peaceful experience that comes with breathtaking panoramic views.

Important details

  • Duration of the balloon flight: 45-60 mins
  • Total duration: 3-4 hours (incl. pickup, flight, bush breakfast and drop off)
  • Price on request
  • Minimum 4 passengers
  • Clothes: preferably warmer clothes for the early morning
  • Weight limit: typically 120 kg per person; extra seat may be required if exceeded
