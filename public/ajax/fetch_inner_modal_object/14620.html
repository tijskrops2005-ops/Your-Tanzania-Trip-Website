Hike to the Mount Meru Waterfalls

A hike to the Mount Meru Waterfalls takes 1.5 to 2 hours each way, covering roughly 4 kilometres through forested trails, bubbling streams, and rocky paths. Along the way, an English-speaking guide shares stories about the mountain’s plants, wildlife, and cultural significance. The reward? A waterfall cascading into a quiet pool – cool mist and all. Sturdy hiking shoes are a must, as the climb can be challenging at times.

Important details

  • Duration: Half day
  • Price on request
  • Travel time: about 30 mins drive from Arusha
