Safari Blue Experience

The Safari Blue Experience is an amazing full-day sailing trip on a traditional wooden dhow to the stunning Menai Bay Conservation Area near Zanzibar where two snorkel spots filled with the most colourful corals and fish await you. During the day, you also visit a sandbank for a refreshing dip, satisfy your tastebuds with a delicious Swahili seafood lunch, and take a refreshing dive between mangroves. And, as a bonus, you might even spot some dolphins!

Important details

  • Price on request
  • Including snorkeling equipment, lunch and soft drinks
