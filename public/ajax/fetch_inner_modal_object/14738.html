Mountain bike tour outside Arusha

A mountain bike tour in Arusha is the perfect way to get a feel of local life.This tour takes you on your mountain bike over beautiful narrow paths and flat terrain that is relatively easy. The paths lead along local farms and extensive fields of bananas and coffee. You also cycle through a number of villages, which shows beautifully how people live here. Along the way, trees provide the necessary shade, which is especially nice in the warm season.

Important details

  • Duration: 2 hrs (morning or afternoon)
  • Price on request
  • Including mountain bikes and helmets
