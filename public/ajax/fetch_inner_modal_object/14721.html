Deep Sea Fishing – Half day

In Zanzibar, you can go deep-sea fishing on a boat with professional anglers. The Indian Ocean is home to blue marlin, dorado, yellowfin tuna, kingfish and many more large species that make for an excellent fishing day. The local captain knows the area well and can tell you what fish can be found at a specific time of the year. Zanzibar’s deep turquoise waters, the blue skies, tropical temperatures and the waves lapping against the boat’s prow make this a perfect (half) day out.

Important details

  • Duration: 5 hrs
  • Price on request
  • Boat: SuliSuli boat for a maximum of 5 passengers
