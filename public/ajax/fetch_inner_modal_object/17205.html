Night game drive in Mikumi National Park

As the sun sets over Mikumi National Park, the bush transforms into a different world entirely. Nocturnal creatures emerge from their hiding places – leopards begin to hunt, hyenas roam, and bush babies dart between the trees. Your guide will help you spot and understand the animals that only come alive after dark, from 7 PM to 11:30 PM. It is an experience that stays with you long after you leave.

Important details

  • Duration: 4.5 hours
  • Pick-up at 7 PM
  • Price on request
