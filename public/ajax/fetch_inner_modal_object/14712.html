Sunset Dinner Cruise

Relax and enjoy the romance of Stone Town from your ‘own’ private traditional but luxurious dhow. Take in the sites of the beautiful old town, local culture, and life at sea. Indulge in succulent seafood freshly grilled on board and sail on to a beautiful sunset. This two-hour sunset dinner cruise is for groups of a maximum of 8 people. It departs from Stone Town at 5 pm and returns at 7 pm.
