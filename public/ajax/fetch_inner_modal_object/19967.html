Hot air balloon flight in Tarangire

This 45-minute to an-hour hot air balloon flight gives you panoramic views of Tarangire National Park while drifting peacefully through the air. At the crack of dawn, you rise up into mid-air, soaring high enough for breathtaking views of the sunrise and the Tarangire River and low enough to watch the baobabs, elephants, lions and other animals in their natural habitat. Watching Africa’s wilderness from a bird’s eye view in the quiet stillness of a hot air balloon is an unmatched experience.

Important details

  • Total duration: 3-4 hours (incl. pickup, flight, bush breakfast and drop off)
  • Price on request
  • Minimum of 4 passengers
  • Clothes: preferably warmer clothes for the early morning
  • Weight limit: typically 120 kg per person; extra seat may be required if exceeded
