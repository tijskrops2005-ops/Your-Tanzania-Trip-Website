Singita Sasakwa Lodge

The Singita Sasakwa Lodge is different than what you expect to find in a place like Serengeti National Park. Instead of your standard camp with luxury safari tents, this lodge offers an Edwardian manor house and thus lets old-world elegance meet with untamed nature. Located in the middle of the Singita Grumeti Reserve in Serengeti, this lodge offers cottages decorated in a colonial style, with African artifacts, silver candelabras, and coveted antiques in a beautifully designed unit. The old-fashioned stand-alone bathtub has a view, the beds are sumptuous, and the views all around are to die for. Smoke your cigars in the salon, nurse your drink on the thatched sunbeds near the infinity pool, enjoy wellness treatments, or indulge yourselves with a game of tennis. For a night or so you find yourself in an era long gone, brushing off the Serengeti dust and wishing you brought your evening gown, hat, or cane for the occasion…!
