Golden Safari Camp

Close to Seronera airstrip, in Serengeti National Park, you find Golden Safari Camp among acacia trees. This solar-powered camp offers several en-suite safari tents with fluffy beds, hot showers, and flush toilets. A family tent with two bedrooms, two bathrooms, and a central living area is available for groups of up to 4 people. The camp features a restaurant tent that serves a variety of continental and local cuisine. Additional amenities include a communal lounge area, a fireplace (bonfire) for evening gatherings, and a Wi-Fi internet hotspot in the main tent.
