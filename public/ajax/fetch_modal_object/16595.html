Mambo View Point Eco Lodge

The Mambo View Point Eco Lodge lies in the remote village of Mambo in the Usambara Mountains. Perched high on a mountain top, the lodge offers spacious double cottages, a honeymoon suite and a family cottage with lots of privacy, all boasting the most amazing view over the surrounding mountains and plains. The stone cottages are set up with traditional thatched roofs, ensuite bathrooms, simple but cosy wooden furniture, free but slow WiFi (as you are high on this remote mountain!) and large windows to fully take in the view. The house restaurant takes care of hungry stomachs after exhilarating hikes through the Usambara Mountains, with the possibility of hiking for several days before reaching Mambo View. Cultural visits to local tribes are recommended as well, as the Dutch owners of the lodge support a variety of community projects.
