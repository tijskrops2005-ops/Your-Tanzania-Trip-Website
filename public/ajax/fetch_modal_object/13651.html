Baraza Resort and Spa

Located along the paradisical beach between Bwejuu and Paje on the east coast of Zanzibar, the Baraza Resort & Spa is a five-star boutique resort with 30 luxuriously appointed villas. This stunning all-inclusive resort evokes the heritage of Zanzibar dating back to the era of the Sultans, with a fusion of Arabic, Swahili and Indian design with Swahili arches, Arabic antiques, handmade furniture and Indian brass lanterns.

There are five types of villas at Baraza, with each one or two bedrooms, walk-in dressing rooms, freestanding bathtubs, and plunge pools. Spread over the spacious garden and along the beach, these villas make you feel like you are in a 1001-night fairytale. With the Dhahabu Bar & Lounge, the Livingstone Terrace Restaurant, the Ocean Lounge, a grand swimming pool and the beach at your bare feet, Baraza provides beautiful moments all throughout the day. All in all, this resort offers a little piece of heaven on one of the most beautiful beaches in the world.
