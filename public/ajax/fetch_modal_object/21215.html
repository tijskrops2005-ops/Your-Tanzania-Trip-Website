Shose Chalets

Shose Chalets offers a serene eco-retreat set in lush gardens just 5 kilometres from Moshi. The property features three chalets with spacious interiors, comfortable beds, and en-suite bathrooms – though you can also choose to stay in one of the farmhouse’s 17 rooms (each with private balconies and views of Mount Kilimanjaro) or camp within the compound. While here, you dine at the on-site restaurant serving fresh African and international cuisine, with most ingredients sourced directly from their own farms…a genuine farm-to-table experience! You can spend your days unwinding by the inviting pool and enjoy your evening at the cosy bar. With warm hospitality and a tranquil setting, Shose is the perfect base for relaxation or adventure.
