Chanya Lodge by Ardhi Africa

Chanya in Kiswahili translates to ‘positive’. Chanya Lodge is nestled on the outskirts of Moshi town, at the foothills of the majestic Mount Kilimanjaro and just a 50-minute drive from Kilimanjaro International Airport, making it an ideal stay for trekkers, safari seekers and families. Guests are warmly welcomed by the smiles of friendly, helpful staff and accommodated in one of 32 ensuite, well equipped and cozy rooms. The lodge is known for its abundant green gardens, multi-cuisine restaurant and its large swimming pool.
