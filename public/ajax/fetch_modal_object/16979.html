Fundu Lagoon

Fundu Lagoon is a boutique hotel on Pemba Island and a little paradise in itself. Located on the beach against a hill, it has beautiful tented rooms with thatched roofs on the beach, as well as a bit higher on the hill. The suites have beautiful little plunge pools on their terrace, besides there being a main pool near the restaurant. Fundu Lagoon is a great place for those who want to go diving or snorkelling, but also for those who just need a place to let go of all of their worldly worries…!
