The Manta Resort

The Manta Resort lies on the tropical island of Pemba. Rather than offering five-star luxury, the Manta presents the unique possibility of becoming one with nature. It invites you to switch off all your electronic devices and to become a person with simple needs; sunshine, fresh air, natural food and a comfortable bed. But if you are looking for something special, you can also book the unique underwater room: here you fall asleep surrounded by the underwater world and wake up with all sorts of tropical fish surrounding you to wish you a beautiful new day…!
