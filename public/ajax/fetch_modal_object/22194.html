Lake Masek Tented Lodge by Tanganyika Wilderness Camps

The Lake Masek Tented Lodge is a lodge with luxury safari tents right in the middle of Ndutu – the area just south of Serengeti National Park that is famous as the area where the calving season of the Great Migration takes place. Each of the 30 tents is set up on a wooden platform with an ensuite bathroom with both a bath and an outside shower. A private terrace allows you to observe a multitude of wildlife drinking at the lake. The beautiful main building of the lodge is made of wood and a thatched roof and hosts a lounge, a restaurant where meals are served, as well as a terrace with a pool and spectacular views of the lake.
