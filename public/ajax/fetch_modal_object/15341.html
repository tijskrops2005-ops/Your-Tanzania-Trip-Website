Ang’ata Tarangire Camp

Ang’ata Tarangire Camp lies in the heart of Tarangire National Park overlooking the Tarangire River. It offers 8 beautifully designed and spacious tents (one of which is an interconnected family tent), equipped with fluffy beds, a small sitting area, a cosy outside terrace, and a luxury appointed ensuite bathroom. A public lounge tent allows you to relax before a sumptuous dinner in the restaurant, after which the Bush TV (a campfire) awaits to end your night in style. An ideal place to stay in luxury while still living that true camping experience!
