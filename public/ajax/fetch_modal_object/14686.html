Horseback riding in Arusha

Go off the beaten track by going on a horseback safari with a guide through the beautiful untouched nature surrounding Mount Meru, at an estate located 45 minutes from Arusha. Less experienced riders will stay on wider, dusty paths, from where they can see wildebeests and antelopes grazing. More experienced riders can explore smaller meandering tracks, spotting a rich array of animals such as zebras, wildebeests, moose and gazelles.

Important details

  • Duration: 2 hrs (depending on pace)
  • Available 8 am or 2.30 pm
  • Price on request
  • Minimum age: 6 years
