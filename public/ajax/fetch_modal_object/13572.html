Fun Beach Resort

Fun Beach Resort on Zanzibar’s southeast coast combines beachfront living with plenty of space to swim, eat, and relax. The resort has rooms, bungalows, and suites, each fitted with air conditioning, Wi-Fi, hot water showers, private bathrooms, mosquito nets, and safes. Between ocean swims or dips in the two pools here, you can enjoy spa treatments, relax in the lounge under a makuti roof (dried coconut palm leaves), or dine on local seafood and international cuisine with vegetarian and vegan options available. And drinks flow from the bar throughout the day, while the beach (lined with coconut trees) offers space for relaxation and fun.
