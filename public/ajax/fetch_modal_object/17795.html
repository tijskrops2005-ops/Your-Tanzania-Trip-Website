Chem Chem Lodge

Chem Chem Lodge offers a cosy retreat with just eight tented suites, including one family suite, giving you plenty of space without the crowds. The lodge can accommodate up to 16 adults and 2 children, so it’s perfect for those seeking a more intimate experience. Each suite comes with both indoor and outdoor showers, and you’ll find the essentials like fans, mosquito nets, and even a “gym-in-a-basket” for some sneaky workouts. If you’re more of a relaxer, no worries – there’s a butler service on hand, ready to cater to your every need.

Families are well taken care of too! There is a family suite features two bedrooms and bathrooms, comfortably fitting up to 2 children and 2 adults, which means you get some extra room to stretch out while keeping your little ones close. WiFi is available in the suites, but the connection speed (which fluctuates) might remind you to slow down and enjoy the views – maybe that’s the lodge’s secret charm!
