Tarangire Safari Lodge

Tarangire Safari Lodge is a combined lodge and tented camp in the north of Tarangire National Park, with some of the most amazing views of the park. The safari tents, with thatched roofs and mesh-screened windows, welcome you to rest comfortably after your game drive. For more space, you can opt for a circular bungalow, built in a style similar to the Masai bomas. All tents and bungalows have their ensuite bathroom with solar-heated showers and 24-hour electricity. Whether you relax on your private terrace, jump in the pool, enjoy dinner in the open-air dining room or sip cocktails at the bar and lounge area, everything comes with stunning panoramic views.
