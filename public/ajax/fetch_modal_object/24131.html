The Craters Edge Lodge – Wilderness Collection

The Craters Edge Lodge boasts three safari camps, one of which is located straight on the edge of the famous Ngorongoro Crater. Relax in style before or after your descent into this wonder of nature, as the camp offers expansive suites adorned with beautiful teak floors, sliding glass windows and mesh screens. This allows you to keep insects out while letting the outside breeze in on warmer days, and to keep the cold at bay on chilly evenings. Fluffy beds and a beautifully designed bathroom complement the feeling of luxury. A restaurant with tantalising dishes, a well-filled bar, a cosy lounge area with a central fireplace, and of course the amazing views ensure that your afternoons, evenings and mornings are all about reminiscing about your adventures and enjoying the amazing nature surrounding you.
