Lemala Nanyukie Tented Lodge

Lemala Nanyukie Tented Lodge lies in central Serengeti, where it is superbly located on a grassy savanna dotted with rocky kopjes and shaded by ancient giant acacia trees. Here it offers outstanding views across the fascinating endless plains of the Serengeti.

The 15 spacious and luxurious canvas tents boast a lovely interior with an oversized bed and an indoor and outdoor shower in the ensuite bathroom. Take a warm bath on cold days in the freestanding bath tub with a view or dive in your own plunge pool in the corner of the outside terrace, for a view on warm days. The comfortable lounge and bar and outdoor viewing deck in the public area lead to a refreshing public swimming pool, and to the indoor and outdoor dining areas. An amazing place!
