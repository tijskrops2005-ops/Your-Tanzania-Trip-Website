andBeyond Lake Manyara Tree Lodge

The Lake Manyara Tree Lodge is one of the most special and luxurious accommodations in the Tanzanian safari circuit. It consists of nine deluxe stilted treehouse suites set amongst an enchanting mahogany forest, right in the middle of Lake Manyara National Park. The commodious suites are fitted with cozy sitting rooms and vast beds in the adjourning bedrooms, while enormous outside decks bring you closer to nature’s bosom, as you nestle yourself right among the treetops. Soak in a freestanding tub encircled by forest views or zone out while taking an exhilarating shower outdoors. Overhead fans silently whirl, creating cool comfort. Mosquito nets adorning every bed make for a restful night’s sleep. And when you need a balance between nature and the surrounding world, WIFI is at your fingertips.
