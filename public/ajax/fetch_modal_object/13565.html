Amariah Hotel – Mikocheni

The Amariah Hotel is a boutique hotel in Dar es Salaam. It’s located in the heart of the city and therefore perfect as a place of rest before or after a long flight. What’s more, from here it’s easy to venture into the bustling city. The rooms are comfortable and equipped with all the standard necessities, such as free Wi-Fi, air conditioning, TV and a kettle. Those who rather stay in, have a restaurant and a gym at their disposal.
