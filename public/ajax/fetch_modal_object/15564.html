Legendary Lodge by Legendary Expeditions

At the Legendary Lodge, you can experience peaceful luxury at the foot of the beautiful Mount Meru. The lodge is surrounded by lush tropical gardens and century-old coffee trees, and its tranquility and proximity to the airport make it a perfect starting point for your safari. It boasts twelve colonial-style cottages with an Afro-European touch, two of which are for families. All cottages provide large beds, mosquito nets, and a free Wi-Fi connection. Whenever you feel hungry, you find delicious food waiting for you in the restaurant and refreshing drinks in the bar. And if you need a proper cooling down, the amazing swimming pool awaits. In short, a legendary start of your safari!
