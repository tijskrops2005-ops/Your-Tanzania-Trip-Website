Dar Es Salaam Serena Hotel

The Dar es Salaam Serena Hotel provides its guests with 230 stylishly designed, beautifully appointed rooms and suites. Here, you enjoy a classic decor featuring large beds, ensuite bathrooms, plush carpets and windows with an impressive view of the Indian Ocean or the glittering Dar es Salaam cityscape. With culinary offerings ranging from freshly caught seafood to Indian and African specialities, Dar es Salaam Serena Hotel offers a choice between two distinctive restaurants – Serengeti and Jahazi. Besides, the Kibo Bar, the Pool Bar and the charming Bakers Basket Patisserie and Coffee Shop meet any taste and occasion one might have.
