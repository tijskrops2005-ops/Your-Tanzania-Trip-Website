Mafia Kivulini Lodge

The Mafia Kivulini Lodge is perfectly located inside Mafia’s marine park boundaries, which makes it a great place to start your snorkeling, diving, fishing, or whale shark expeditions. The lodge sits between ancient trees (kivulini means in the shadow in Swahili) are offers only 4 rooms with a maximum capacity of 8 guests, ensuring a private and tranquil atmosphere. The rooms are fitted with an ensuite bathroom, electric fans and air-conditioning, a private terrace, mosquito nets, a desk, a safe and free Wi-Fi. Breakfast and dinner are served in the restaurant, with breakfast consisting of seasonal fresh fruits, muffins, pancakes and more. Dinner is a 3-course set menu, mostly consisting of tropical dishes and fresh seafood, with the fishermen right outside on the ocean. The pool and the bicycle rent complete your beach stay.
