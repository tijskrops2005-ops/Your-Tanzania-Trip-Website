Camp Bastian

Camp Bastian is a good place for an overnight during a visit to Mikumi National Park. The camp has a campground and 7 ensuite cottages in a very green environment, ensuring you always wake up to the sounds of nature around you. There is a swimming pool where you can cool down with a drink after a dusty safari. The food is all freshly prepared and made from local products, and the friendly staff knows how to create a relaxing atmosphere. A lovely place!
