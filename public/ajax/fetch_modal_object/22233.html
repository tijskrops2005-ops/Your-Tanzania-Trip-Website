Manyara Best View Lodge and Spa – Upgrade

The Manyara Best View Lodge & Spa is an astonishing lodge in one of Tanzania’s most beautiful locations, surrounded by lush nature and an abundance of tropical birds and other wildlife. Here, you are welcomed in luxuriously appointed rooms, comfortably furnished with fluffy kingsize beds, a beautiful bathroom with an outside shower and bathtub, and a private veranda that allows you to relax in complete privacy and take in the unique view. If you want to indulge even more, you can book a wonderful massage at the luxury in-house spa (or in your room if you want!), that offers all kinds of the most relaxing treatments – easily paid by card, as the lodge is fully cashless.

Then imagine a sunset, sitting on your veranda on a 350-meter high top of the famous Rift Valley Escarpment with your aperitive in hand, overlooking the lodge’s infinity pool, and enjoying the vast views… See the magnificent escarpment wall on the left curving slightly forward and leading up to Lake Natron, and the glittering Lake Manyara in all her glory on the right. At the bottom of the escarpment, you find a lot of banana farms (which you can visit), and in the distance the vast Mbuyuni hillsides are visible, and behind those the Manyara Ranch (a significant wildlife migration zone). In short, this is the perfect place to relax in complete luxury with the most exquisite views you can imagine. Come and enjoy the moment in the Manyara Best View Lodge!
