Serengeti Sound of Silence Tented Camp

Serengeti Sound of Silence is a tented camp where every kind of luxury is present. It offers an amazing view from the tent over the savannas of Serengeti National Park. In addition, this tented camp has an à la carte restaurant where high-quality food is served.

The luxurious tents are not only more spacious than those in other tented camps but also have a unique African ambience. They are equipped with a large king-size bed and a comfortable shower. Your Tanzania Trip has been involved in the development of this tented camp since Day One because we noticed that there is a demand for such a luxurious and distinctive tented camp. If you want to get the most out of your Serengeti safari experience we recommend staying at this unique camp. You will have an outstanding and wonderful experience.
