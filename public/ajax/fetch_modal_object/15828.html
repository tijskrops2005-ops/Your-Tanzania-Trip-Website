Asanja Ndembo – Ruaha

Directly on the Great Ruaha River in Ruaha National Park, Asanja Ruaha lies between magnificent old baobabs. With eight luxury designer guest suites, this is an intimate camp that pampers your soul. Each room is 140 square meters in size, boasting stone walls and floors but canvas walls to the front and side to allow both privacy and amazing views over the impressive Ruaha plains. Luxuriously appointed, the bedroom, sitting area and bathroom ooze a sense of well-being and pampering, topped off by a lone standing bathtub inside and a plunge pool just outside on the terrace. An extraordinary place!
