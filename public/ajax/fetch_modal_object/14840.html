Serengeti Explorer by Elewana

The Serengeti Explorer is a luxurious lodge within the renowned Serengeti National Park. It lies in the south-central part of the park, on top of the Nyaburo Hills, which makes it the perfect balcony for admiring the Great Migration when it passes here between March and June.

It boasts 70 en-suite rooms and 5 suites with elegant décor in ochre tones, all equipped with comfortable beds, a bathroom with a rain shower, and a balcony with amazing views of the endless plains of the Serengeti. If you are looking for body and mind relaxation, it’s possible to get a massage in the wellness room or even in your room, and a large infinity pool awaits those wanting to take a dip after a long safari day. When it comes to dining, there are a variety of places to choose from; a main restaurant with live cooking stations, a boma restaurant serving Swahili and African dishes on its terrace, a poolside bistro and bar, and a lobby cafe and deli. You can even ask to have a private dinner in its extensive wine cellar!
