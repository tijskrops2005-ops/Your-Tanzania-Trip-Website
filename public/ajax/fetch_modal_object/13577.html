Qambani Luxury Resort

Be ready to rub your eyes, because Qambani Luxury Resort will make you feel like you’ve landed in paradise. It boasts six very stylish villas in different sizes that are completely in sync with the magical atmosphere of Zanzibar, each designed in its own style and offering a lot of privacy. Some of the villas boast private little pools on the ocean’s edge, while others offer outside showers or a standalone bathtub. All villas are beautifully decorated with spacious living areas and private verandahs (sometimes on a rooftop) with sea views. On the ocean’s front, you find an amazing restaurant, public sitting area and infinity pool, all together perfectly suited for leisurely afternoons or evenings.

What makes Qambani even more special, is that it’s an ecological resort where solar power and the newest environmentally friendly techniques are combined with luxury and comfort. The perfect place for those looking to enjoy the holiday of their lives in style!
