Ahadi Lodge

Ahadi Lodge is a small-scale lodge in an authentic residential area in the heart of Arusha, where you get an impression of everyday life in Tanzania while still enjoying the peaceful surroundings. The relatively small rooms are set in a lush tropical garden, surrounding the swimming pool. Every room has a different colour and theme, and is equipped with double or twin beds, an ensuite bathroom with a shower, a TV and a safe. In addition, there’s a small restaurant serving nice food – the friendly staff makes it a guest favourite. Ahadi is a cashless lodge, so meals and drinks are paid by card.
