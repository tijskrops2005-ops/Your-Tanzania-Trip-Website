Ngare Sero Mountain Lodge

The Ngare Sero Mountain Lodge is an exclusive lodge in Arusha, surrounded by unbelievably beautiful gardens and forests. In the huge garden, you can see Colobus monkeys jumping among the trees and hear all sorts of tropical birds sing. The tranquillity you find on these grounds makes you feel like you have landed in paradise.

The rooms are decorated in a Zanzibar style, with plush, comfortable beds, and a beautiful bathroom with a bathtub and a shower. In addition, rooms boast a small lounge area and a large terrace with views of the garden or sometimes even of Mount Kilimanjaro. Other rooms are located in the beautifully designed main house. The food is prepared by a team of chefs who will always do their utmost best to put a gourmet meal on the table, many times prepared with produce from the kitchen garden. In and around the lodge, you can entertain yourself in various ways – take a nice walk around the grounds, go canoeing, take a dip in the pool, or visit a local village with a guide. An ideal starter (or endpoint) of your trip!
