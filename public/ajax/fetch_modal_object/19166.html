Nimali Mara

Nimali Mara is a hidden oasis in a remote location in the northern part of Serengeti, where it ensures guests a peaceful and soothing stay. This cozy tented camp provides ten spacious and beautifully designed tents that have canvas walls with glass fronts to allow the best view over the Serengeti plains and the passing Great Migration.
Everything here is designed for you to marvel at the view and the wildebeest and zebra herds that frequently parade by. An infinity pool is set within a rocky natural cave, where it allows you to savor your drink while watching the wildlife stroll by. The tents afford you multiple views: from the comfort of your bed, from the swing bed on your terrace, and from the romantic copper bathtub that is perched in front of the glass windows that open up to the vast plains. Additionally, the two honeymoon/family tents also have their own plunge pools which also allow for a breathtaking view. The food served is simply mouthwatering, and the service is impeccable. A special place for your memorable Great Migration adventure!
