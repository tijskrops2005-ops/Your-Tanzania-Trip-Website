Inoga Lodge

Inoga Lodge is located near Karatu town, between Tarangire National Park and the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. The lodge is set up on a lovely, clean terrain boasting a swimming pool and communal restaurant, with rooms spread over several little houses. Each room boasts an ensuite bathroom (with bathtub or shower), cable television, a mini-bar, free Wi-Fi and a coffee machine. Meals are served at the restaurant, with dishes prepared based on seasonal ingredients. After your safari, the pool is available for your relaxing moment, and you can sip cocktails at the bar.
