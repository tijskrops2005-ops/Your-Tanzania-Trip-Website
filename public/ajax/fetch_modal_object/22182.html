Giraffe Manor Tanzania

Giraffe Manor Tanzania is a one-of-a-kind lodge set on the serene Dolly Estate just outside Arusha, where mornings often come with giraffes walking by as you sip your first cup of coffee! Surrounded by lush lawns, flowering gardens, and acacia woodland with birdlife, impala, elands, zebra, and wildebeest, the lodge offers a peaceful setting while remaining close to Kilimanjaro International Airport and Arusha National Park – an ideal starting point for the northern safari circuit. And with views stretching toward Mount Meru, you can watch stunning sunsets from the upstairs deck, relax by the infinity pool with sunken lounges, or spend time in the elegant lounges and bar. The restaurant here serves fresh, locally sourced dishes, while the accommodation features comfortable rooms – some with private plunge pools and terraces – overlooking the manicured grounds. It’s an experience shaped by calm comfort, open space, and the possibility of unexpected wildlife encounters!
