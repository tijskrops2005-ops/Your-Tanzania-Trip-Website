Tarangire Treetops by Elewana

Tarangire Treetops is a luxury safari lodge that offers a unique and unforgettable experience. The lodge features spacious treehouses built on stilts, allowing guests to immerse themselves in the stunning surroundings. Each treehouse has a private balcony overlooking the majestic baobab trees and the park’s abundant wildlife. Guests can enjoy game drives to spot elephants, lions, and other African wildlife, or unwind at the lodge’s pool and spa. With its exceptional service and breathtaking views, Tarangire Treetops promises an extraordinary adventure and luxury.
