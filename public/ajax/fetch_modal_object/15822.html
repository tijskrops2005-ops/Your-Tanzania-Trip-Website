Ruaha River Lodge by Foxes Safari Camps

The Ruaha River Lodge is set on one of the most stunning stretches of the Great Ruaha River. You can see the game throughout the day from the comfort of your veranda. The 24 stone chalets are stretched along the river bank, decorated with a feel of African wilderness and feature a sitting area, writing desk, comfy beds and large bathrooms. The Ruaha River restaurant is lovingly situated by the side of the river and offers delicious meals.

Wake up to the sounds of the nature surrounding you, and enjoy your morning coffee while you watch the game meandering down to the river for their morning drink.
