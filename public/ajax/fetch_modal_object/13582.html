Next Paradise Boutique Resort

The Next Paradise Boutique Resort sits on one of the most beautiful white sandy beaches of Zanzibar, directly facing the Indian Ocean with coral reefs at only half a mile away. The resort is well known for its sober elegance, excellent food, and intimate and quiet atmosphere, with sumptuous rooms that are fully furnished and equipped with both air conditioning and WiFi. The 25 rooms all lie close to the beach, while you also can choose from no less than four pools for your refreshing dip. Last but least, the chef will amaze you with his Mediterranean cuisine with a touch of Swahili flavours. Next Paradise is Zanzibar’s romantic getaway!
