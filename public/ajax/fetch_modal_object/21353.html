Signature Serengeti Luxury Tented Camp

The Signature Serengeti Luxury Tented Camp is nestled deep in the bush of central Serengeti National Park, along the Nyabogati River. This location provides year-round water for the animals and therefore offers great viewing opportunities. Set on wooden platforms, the luxury safari tents are all custom-made from the finest canvas and offer all the comfort you need. Wide king-size beds, a lounge area and an ensuite bathroom are part of the package. Outside, a covered veranda awaits you for game viewing, relaxing and admiring wonderful sunsets as all the tents are west-facing. In the dining and lounge tents, you can enjoy a cold drink and a healthy meal, or play one of their many board, chess, card or children’s games.
