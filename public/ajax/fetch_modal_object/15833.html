Laba Jongomero Camp – Tented Suite

The Laba Jongomero Camp lies beautifully in a remote part of Ruaha National Park, over the seasonal Jongomero sand river, a tributary of the great Ruaha River. The luxury permanent camp is only accessible by a light aircraft which makes it a very exclusive place. Here, you enjoy top-notch service and assistance at all times.
Eight large and well-appointed tented suites welcome you under magnificent outstretched trees. Raised on wooden platforms, each suite lies under enormous thatched roofs and comprises an ensuite bathroom, comfortable furniture made of reclaimed dhow wood and a private deck with a sunbed. The decoration makes you feel like the first explorers, blending adventure with a touch of luxury. Whether you want to stay fit at the gym, enjoy a plunge in the swimming pool or savour a tasty meal prepared by a local chef, you do it all in style with amazing views over the landscape and the surrounding wildlife.
