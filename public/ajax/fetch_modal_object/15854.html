Vuma Hills Tented Camp by Foxes Safari Camps

While doing a safari in Mikumi National Park, the Vuma Hills Tented Camp is a lovely choice for your overnight. This tented camp has a beautiful view of the Mkata Plains of the national park. All tents are comfortably furnished and have ensuite bathrooms. There is also a covered veranda at each tent from where you can enjoy the surroundings – including the bush babies who might pay a visit! In the evening you can relax at the campfire and in the cosy bar and dining area. And if you are looking to cool down after safari, a swimming pool is at your disposal.
