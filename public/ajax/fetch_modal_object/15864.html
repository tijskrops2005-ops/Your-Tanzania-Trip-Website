Hondo Hondo Udzungwa Forest Tented Camp

The Hondo Hondo Udzungwa Forest Tented Camp borders the Udzungwa Mountains National Park in the Kilombero Valley in southern Tanzania. Hondo Hondo means hornbill in Swahili, and the camp got its name thanks to the amazing number of these remarkable birds that make the area their home. Hondo Hondo’s lush green terrain is located on the edge of pristine rainforest and offers excellent views. The camp offers comfortable safari tents with ensuite bathrooms and private terraces, as well as thatched huts. Enjoy an outdoor shower under the bare sky and feel completely at one with nature. The restaurant caters to all tastes, and ice-cold drinks are available in the cosy bar.
