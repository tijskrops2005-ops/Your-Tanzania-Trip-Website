Ngorongoro Lodge by Melia

The Ngorongoro Lodge by Melia lies in the heart of the Ngorongoro Highlands, overlooking the famous UNESCO-protected conservation area of Ngorongoro crater.

With 28 rooms and 24 spacious suites boasting breathtaking views over the crater, this newly renovated lodge provides luxury services but also keeps sustainability at heart. It indeed generates a significant part of its electricity through solar panels and avoids plastic consumption by having a water bottling plant onsite.
The interiors are inspired by Masai traditions and art and all furniture is locally made. In the bedrooms, you find rain showers or bath tubes, elegant beddings, and wide windows and some even have a terrace to enjoy the view to its fullest.

In addition, the lodge features two gourmet restaurants using local and organic products, an infinity pool with unbeatable views, a barbeque area and a spa to pamper yourself. An unforgettable place in a unique area.
