Villa Maua

Villa Maua, or the House of Flowers, offers nine, spacious rooms, right in the center of Arusha in one of the historic residential areas of the city. All rooms are air-conditioned, ensuite (some with a bath tub), double- or twin-bedded, and offer satellite TV, WiFi, and private verandas overlooking the lush tropical garden full of flowers. Designed for the traveler of today, the clean contemporary decor is complemented by natural hardwood furniture and ‘touches of Africa’. The three dining areas of the hotel offer the opportunity to dine indoors as well as outdoors, with snacks and meals on offer throughout the day. What’s more, there’s a relatively small swimming pool with crystal-clear blue water, an ideal spot to relax, soak up the sun, and cool down!
