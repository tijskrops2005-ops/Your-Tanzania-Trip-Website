Saadani Safari Lodge

On the edge of Saadani National Park, where elephants roam and waves roll in from the Indian Ocean, Saadani Safari Lodge pairs safari adventure with barefoot beach living. The lodge offers Swahili-style suites with makuti roofs, private shaded decks, and direct access to the sandy beach. The suites feature en-suite bathrooms with showers, mosquito nets, fans, locally carved coconut wood furniture, and solar-powered amenities, as well as lockable pirate boxes for storing your valuables. You can spend time at the infinity swimming pool overlooking the ocean, the beachfront thatched restaurant, the Mikadi Bar for sundowners, or even the yoga deck tucked in tropical greenery. Meals are part of the stay, ranging from bush breakfasts to private dinners on the sand, so you are never far from good food (and a sea breeze!).
