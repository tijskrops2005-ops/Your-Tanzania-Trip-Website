Kigoma Hilltop Hotel

Situated on a secluded rocky outcrop amidst lush gardens overlooking Lake Tanganyika, the Kigoma Hilltop Hotel offers a lovely retreat close to the city of Kigoma in the west of Tanzania. All 30 hotel bungalows are equipped with large four-poster beds, ensuite bathrooms and private terraces overlooking the lake. Both the swimming pool and the restaurant offer the same magnificent view, with the Sangara Restaurant (sangara meaning perch, a fish frequently caught in the lake) boasting a terrace above the water, allowing you to enjoy the view while having breakfast, lunch or dinner.
