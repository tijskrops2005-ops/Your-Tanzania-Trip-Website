Nimali Tarangire

Nimali Tarangire is an intimate camp situated next to a dry riverbed, in between acacia and baobab trees. The accommodation lies quietly in its own concession area away from the big tourist stream. Each of the ten guest tents is large and stylishly furnished, offering a large, leather strapped travelling chest, private deck and en-suite facilities including a double vanity basin and revitalizing rain shower. The spacious decks are perfect for private dining and in-room spa treatments. Enjoy the pool overlooking a waterhole from which wild animals regularly drink.

The only ‘photographical hide’ in Tanzania
In the private concession of Nimali, they have made their own photographical hide next to the waterhole where the animals frequently gather to drink. In this hide, you can get close to the animals and capture the most beautiful pictures. This is a unique opportunity!
