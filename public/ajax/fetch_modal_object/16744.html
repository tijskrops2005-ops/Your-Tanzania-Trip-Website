Gibb’s Farm

Gibb’s Farm imparts a sense of well-being, tranquillity, and history, as it is deeply rooted in African culture and community. In a warm, rustic, and luxurious environment, this peaceful sanctuary offers 17 cosy, well-appointed cottages and 2 large farmhouses perfect for families or groups. The extremely spacious cottages are all designed in a unique style, referring to either the surroundings or the history, with for example the Mulberry cottage surrounded by Mulberry trees, or the Zamani (Swahili for a long time ago) cottage as a hommage to its founders Margaret and James Gibbs. Last but not least, the breathtaking scenery, scattering wildlife, and unique cultural activities allow you to fully experience the rhythms and beauty of the farm, its community, and the surrounding nature.
