Serena Mivumo River Lodge

The Serena Mivumo River Lodge provides a serene retreat amidst the unspoilt beauty of the stunning Julius Nyerere National Park. The 12 spacious chalets offer thatched roofs, wooden floors and an appealing blend of Edwardian elegance and contemporary comfort. From your terrace, you enjoy unparalleled views of the wilderness, as well as from your outside shower. Families or groups can choose to stay in the Mivumo Villa, which offers three double ensuite bedrooms, a private deck with sun loungers, a plunge pool and a rain shower. Perched on timber decks above the awe-inspiring Rufiji River, the spacious and light-filled dining room is an ideal spot to begin or end your day. Enjoy the comfortable sofas in the open bar and the uninterrupted views of hippos, elephants and crocodiles that congregate on the riverbank as the sun sets and you savour your drink after a splendid day of safari.
