Manyara’s Secret

Manyara’s Secret is a cosy boutique hotel with a view over Lake Manyara National Park, that offers beautifully designed villas for two, four, six or eight people each. Couples who value their privacy can stay in a smaller villa with lush beds, outside showers and a private plunge pool, while families can stay in larger villas with multiple ensuite bedrooms with outside showers and larger private pools for the family to share. Feeling more social? Meet other guests by heading to the communal pool near the restaurant, enjoy delicious food, and take your drink to the fire that gets lit in the night. A wonderful place!
