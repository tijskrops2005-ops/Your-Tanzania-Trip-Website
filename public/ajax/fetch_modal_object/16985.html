Pemba Paradise Lodge

Pemba Paradise Lodge is situated on the beautiful Pemba Island on the north-west coast in the village of Makangale. The hotel offers 8 ocean-view suites with a sleeping and a sitting area, and 8 garden-view double rooms, all set in a beautiful garden. The rooms are fitted with one big double bed, whereas the suites come with additional daybeds, which can be used for extra guests – great for small families. They also feature ensuite bathrooms and a private terrace. The restaurant serves delicious meals, all prepared with fresh fish straight out of the ocean and fruits and vegetables from the lodge’s garden or the local market. To top it off, you’ll find a great beach area and a swimming pool here, as well as two bars; the Pili Pili Bar situated next to the restaurant, and the Mapenzi Beach Bar where you can admire the sunset over the ocean.
