andBeyond – Grumeti Serengeti River Lodge

The luxurious andBeyond – Grumeti Serengeti River Lodge is located in the remote western foothills of the Serengeti National Park. There are few other lodges here and you rarely see other vehicles. This ensures an extraordinary experience with the feeling of ultimate exclusivity. Rustic yet refined, this camp has a warmth that makes you feel at home and part of the family. All ten tented suites have shaded private verandas overlooking the riverfront. Large day beds make this the perfect place to spend the afternoon. The interiors combine wildebeest grey tones with accents of natural stone and bone. Stone shower towers are open to the sun and stars. Intimate guest areas include seating and dining areas with open fronts and a vaulted bar, where you indulge in lovely sundowners and delicious food.
