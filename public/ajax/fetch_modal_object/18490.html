Ngorongoro Kuhama Camp by TNS Hospitality

The Ngorongoro Kuhama Camp is an eco-friendly camp in the savannah, surrounded by acacia trees. Located on the eastern side of the Ngorongoro Crater, just a 15-minute drive from the crater, the camp is a convenient starting point for a trip to the famous caldera. It offers 16 tents with wooden floors that can accommodate one to three people. The traditional African-style tents come with beds, en-suite bathrooms, mosquito nets, free Wi-Fi and private verandas overlooking the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. The camp serves international and African cuisine and can adapt dishes to suit special dietary needs. It is fully powered by solar energy, and next to the dining area is a lounge and bar with outlets for charging electronic devices. After dinner, a campfire is lit outside the dining tent, giving you a place to sit and reflect on your day at the crater and the sights you saw there.
