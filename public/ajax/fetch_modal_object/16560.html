Salinero Kilimanjaro Lodge

The Salinero Kilimanjaro Lodge lies in a leafy suburb, at a 3-kilometre drive from Moshi town. The rooms are well-ventilated and specially designed to make you comfortable after a hectic day, as they are equipped with a refrigerated mini-bar, tea and coffee-making facilities, an ensuite bathroom, wireless internet, and satellite television. The restaurant here serves dishes made with fresh, seasonal produce from its own kitchen garden. Besides, a pool is available for a dip, and a large outdoor area set in an oasis of lush gardens offers relaxing chairs and an atmosphere designed to make you feel right at home.
