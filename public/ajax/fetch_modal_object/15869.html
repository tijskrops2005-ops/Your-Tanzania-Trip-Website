Udzungwa Falls Eco-Cultural Lodge

The Udzungwa Falls Eco-Cultural Lodge is located on the slopes of Udzungwa Mountains National Park. From here, you can go on Udzungwa hikes, visit the waterfalls, and explore the flora and fauna of the national park. The lodge has an idyllic flair and offers 40 spacious and air-conditioned rooms with private balconies, alle ensuite with shower and fitted with satellite TV, coffee and tea facilities, a minibar, a safe, a desk and free Wi-Fi. Indulge in fine dining with an à la carte menu featuring a wide variety of soups, salads, steaks, poultry dishes, fine fish delicacies and mouth-watering desserts.
