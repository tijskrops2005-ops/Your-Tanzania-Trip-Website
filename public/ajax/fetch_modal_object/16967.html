Pole Pole Bungalows

The Pole Pole Bungalows are located on Chole Bay on Mafia and offer 7 well-appointed bungalows, all with private terraces (some even have their own massage room!) and built in an ecologically responsible manner with, for example, a system to reuse their wastewater. They are fitted with ensuite bathrooms, fans, air-conditioning, Wi-Fi, coffee and tea stations, safes and mosquito nets. The resort itself features a lovely swimming pool and many activities. Snorkeling and diving are highly recommended because Mafia is known for its beautiful coral reefs and as the place where you can swim with whale sharks (in season)!
