Mbali Mbali Gombe Lodge

The Mbali Mbali Gombe Lodge in Gombe Stream National Park features 7 luxury safari tents with corrugated roofs, situated in the shadow of ancient mangotrees, offering a view over Lake Tanganyika. Each tent boasts an en-suite bathroom, large beds, and a balcony in paradise, complete with a small table and safari chairs. Here, you can relax and enjoy the breathtaking sunsets over the lake after your amazing chimp trekking. The canvas tents are set wide apart to ensure complete privacy on the strip of forest between the coast and the mountain. A small reception room built on stilts on the beach has a bar, lounge, library, and dining room. A jetty leads you over the lake, and comfortable deck chairs are available on the beach.
