Ole Serai Luxury Camp – Turner Springs by Wellworth

Ole Serai Turner Springs lies in the heart of Serengeti National Park, 17 kilometres from the Seronera Airstrip. The spectacular and secluded location of this camp ensures sightings of wildlife year-round, choosing to graze around the camp due to its lagoon-type contoured landscape as water catchments border the camp on three sides. Turner Springs consists of 20 luxury tents that are set out and specially designed with maximum guest privacy in mind. The camp boasts fine dining experiences, luxurious and comfortable bedding and furnishings, and personalized butler services. Sundowners, aperitifs, and cocktails at the campfire will complete your safari experience here!
