Ngorongoro O’ldeani Mountain Lodge by Wellworth

Ngorongoro O’ldeani Mountain Lodge is a beautiful 5-star luxury lodge in Karatu. It’s a great place for a relaxing night en route to Serengeti National Park, or for when you come back from exploring the Ngorongoro crater. The lodge is furnished in a beautiful, colonial style and offers an incredible view all around – extending all the way to Lake Manyara and Lake Eyasi – that never fails to leave guests awestruck! All rooms of the hotel comprise big, comfortable beds, beautiful bathrooms, a luxury outside shower and a marvellous balcony. Last but not least, the lodge has a nice swimming pool and a staff that provides excellent service.
