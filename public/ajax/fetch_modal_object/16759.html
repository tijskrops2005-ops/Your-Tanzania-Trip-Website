Sanctuary Swala

Sanctuary Swala is located in an exclusive pocket of the Tarangire National Park. Set on stilts around towering, ancient baobab trees, the elevated dining area, lounge, and library are all sound-tracked by the sing-song of hundreds of birds. The 12 spacious tents themselves boast kingsize beds swathed in neutral linens mingling a fresh design with a new take on safari living. Indoor and outdoor bush-view showers ensure more magical moments of connecting with the surrounding wildlife, especially with the view of the waterhole close by. The elevated main deck is perfect for afternoon sundowners in soft lounge chairs, followed by scrumptious meals from the restaurant and a campfire in a beautiful circular setting under the outstretched night skies. At Sanctuary Swala you experience your safari nights in style!
