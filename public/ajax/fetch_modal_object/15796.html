Southern Sun Dar es Salaam

Southern Sun Dar es Salaam is a leading Dar es Salaam hotel renowned for comfort-boosting services and productivity-enhancing amenities. Neighbouring the historic Botanical Gardens, the Julius Nyerere ICC and the Zanzibar ferry; it’s easy for beach-adventures or business in the city. With modern meeting rooms, exceptional dining, a pool, gym, and great-sleep rooms in a tree-lined, location near the ocean, Southern Sun is really an inviting place to discover Dar es Salaam.
