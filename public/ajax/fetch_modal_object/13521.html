Lake Manyara Kilimamoja Lodge by Wellworth

The Lake Manyara Kilimamoja Lodge lies on an escarpment at the edge of Lake Manyara National Park, where it boasts astonishing views over the park. On days with clear skies, you can even see Mounts Meru and Kilimanjaro from this lodge. All rooms are luxuriously and comfortably appointed, with for example an outside & inside shower, a fireplace, free WiFi and a private balcony with a panoramic view. The main building has a spa, a TV room, a giant swimming pool and a spot where at night a fire is lit so you can watch the vast sky full of stars.
