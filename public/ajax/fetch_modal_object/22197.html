Lolkisale Tarangire Camp by Top Guides

Lolkisale Tarangire Camp is a serene and luxurious getaway in the Tarangire National Park. The camp has 8 custom-designed safari tents, on an elevated wooden platform. Located right by the wetlands, you see herds passing through in search of water from your private porch at the break of day. Thoughtfully selected interiors, with soft natural colours and textures, and a comfy bed give you just that extra touch of comfort you need on a safari. In the dining tent and lounge area you can enjoy great meals, great conversations, and a good nightcap from a well-stocked bar. You don’t need much more on safari in the African bush!
