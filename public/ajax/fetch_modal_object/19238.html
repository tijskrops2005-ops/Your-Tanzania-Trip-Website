One Nature Nyaruswiga Serengeti

The tented camp of One Nature Nyaruswiga in Serengeti National Park will leave you wanting nothing more. The tents are beautiful and very spacious, with lush furniture in a classic style, beautiful bathrooms and outside showers. The comfy big sofas, the fourposter beds filled with pillows, the wood fire inside the tent, the classic bathtub openly in the bedroom next to that fire… it all speaks of a luxury that you will rarely find somewhere else. The camp has 12 tents designed for 2 -3 guests and 2 family tents for up to 6 sleeping guests. Besides, it boasts a lush lounge area, a restaurant with white linen, beautiful china, crystal glasses and silver chandeliers, and an infinity pool with a jacuzzi. On top of that, there is an astral observation deck where you can have your dinner with the telescope right next to you for your stargazing.

Staying here feels like going back to colonial times, as you can play tennis on the grass of Serengeti, with your lunch and comfy chairs set up next to the court. The trading post sells all the safari clothes and necessities you might need. Enjoy your whiskey in the bar, play some vinyl on the old-fashioned gramophone, take a bath with a view before dinner or go for a massage in the spa. At One Nature all that and more is at your service!
