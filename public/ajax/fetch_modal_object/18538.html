Ngorongoro Serena Safari Lodge

Nestled high above the plains into the crater’s jagged rim, cloaked in river stone and indigenous vines, the Ngorongoro Serena Safari Lodge offers the most splendid views over the ancient Ngorongoro crater while barely being visible with the naked eye. The 75 rustic rooms are looped around the crater rim, boasting a lush almost old-fashioned decor with rock-enclosed balconies right on the crater’s edge, ensuite bathrooms and good beds, all donned in brown and ecru colours. The elegant yet relaxed dining space features a glowing fireplace, walls with prehistoric cave paintings, and – how can it not be – the same magical views next to good, hearty food. This special and luxurious lodge feels almost as ancient as the crater itself, yet free WiFi, digital TV and a spa are available to cater to your every need.
