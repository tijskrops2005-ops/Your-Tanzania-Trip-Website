Asanja Nyasirori

Asanja Nyasarori is a luxury tented camp in the Western Serengeti – a 20-minute drive from Grumeti Airstrip. The camp has seven ensuite huts and a two-bedroom family hut, all inspired by traditional Maasai Enkaji (home). Each of these rounded huts has a private plunge pool, a separate lounge area, and a bathroom with a hot shower. The camp lets you choose between two single beds or one double bed, with private dining available in each unit. The open woodlands (famous for large herds of elephants!) surrounding the camp add to the secluded and peaceful atmosphere.
