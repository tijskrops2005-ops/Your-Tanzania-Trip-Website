Mkomazi View Camp by African View

Mkomazi View Camp lies in a secluded valley called Watershed inside Mkomazi National Park, where it overlooks one of the biggest waterholes in the park. The camp lies close to the Mbula Rhino Sanctuary, where you can watch the endangered rhino at close range, and the Dindira Dam, a popular spot where many wild animals come to drink. The camp houses only eight ensuite tents, running on solar power and equipped with outside bucket showers. Its stunning location in combination with more than just a touch of comfort and privacy ensures a lovely evening under the endless night sky of Mkomazi.
