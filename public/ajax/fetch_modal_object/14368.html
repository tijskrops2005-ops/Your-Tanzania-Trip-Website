Into Wild Africa – Tented Camp

Into Wild Africa is a tented camp in a completely remote and unique place. Hidden deep in the central Serengeti along the Nyabogati River, it lies 8 kilometres from the nearest road on an amazingly beautiful savannah plain. As such, it’s the perfect place to observe animals as you are surrounded by wildlife at any time of the day… and night!

The camp is composed of 10 extra large luxury tents (including 2 family tents). Inside each tent, you enjoy the comfort of queen-sized beds, a nice sitting area and an ensuite bathroom with hot water. In the evening, you have dinner in the restaurant tent where a chef and his team prepare delicious and varied food, catering to special preferences and dietary wishes if needed. And, being so remote, after dinner there is nothing better than sitting by the campfire with the vast open sky above you and bush noises all around you.
