Mbali Mbali Mahale lodge

Mbali Mbali Mahale is located deep in the heart of Tanzania on a secluded golden sand beach. Here, at the edge of the Mahale Mountains National Park this lodge offers 10 modernly furnished beach tents strategically placed on the edge of the cooling forest of Mahale and Lake Tanganyika. All rooms are located on raised platforms under thatched roofs and feature a private terrace overlooking Lake Tanganyika and the beach, close to the main lounge and dining areas. They are equipped with comfy beds, luxurious bedding and a full bathroom with an indoor rain shower and double vanity. The surroundings of the lodge are impressive with birds, monkeys, small carnivores and butterflies that allow for an impressive walk through the woods.
