Baobab Mara Luxury Camp

The Baobab Mara Luxury Camp is a convenient starting point for safaris in the northern part of the Serengeti National Park. Since this is a migrating camp, it has the unique opportunity to find the perfect spot for migration adventures. Here, the Great Migration is usually close and wildlife can be found easily. The camp consists of 11 well-appointed safari tents, all with ensuite bathrooms. The king-size bed, luggage rack, bedside tables and reading lamps are designed to make you comfortable while still experiencing a touch of Africa. The camp is located in one of the most complex ecosystems in the world, which is the main reason for setting up an eco-camp, with both electricity and hot water being solar-powered only.
