Halisi Natron Camp

Halisi Natron Camp is located on the southern shore of Lake Natron in a remote, private area – a birder’s paradise! The camp offers 10 spacious walk-in tents for single, double, triple or family occupancy. The tents are furnished with traditional East African décor, king-size beds with comfortable mattresses, and private en suite bathrooms with a bush shower, sink, and toilet. The camp has a lounge, dining and bar tent, and you can enjoy a campfire under the stars in the evenings. All food is sourced locally, which supports the economy and creates jobs and educational opportunities to empower members of the local villages. The camp utilizes solar energy, and minimal water usage, and measures designed to ensure the smallest carbon footprint possible, without compromising on service and comfort.
