Lake Eyasi Safari Lodge

Located on a gentle slope, Lake Eyasi Safari Lodge offers stunning views over the seasonal soda lake, all the way up to the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. All 16 tastefully furnished rooms are fitted with an ensuite bathroom and private terrace overlooking the lake. The decor blends elements from the surrounding landscape with modern solar-powered comfort. Key amenities include a swimming pool, a restaurant serving delicious continental cuisine, a bar/lounge, a gift shop, free Wi-Fi in public areas, and guided cultural tours to the Hadzabe and Datoga tribes.
