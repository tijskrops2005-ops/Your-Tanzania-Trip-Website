Nimali Central Serengeti

Located in the heart of the Serengeti near Makoma Hill, Nimali Central Serengeti is the epitome of a safari experience. The camp offers fourteen opulent tents – two of which are connected by a walkway to accommodate families or couples travelling together. The tented suites are beautifully furnished in a lush and modern take on the old safari days. They each have a bathtub, indoor and outdoor shower, and a private veranda where you can immerse yourself in nature and enjoy breathtaking panoramic views. Views of the vast Serengeti plains are also available from the main lounge and dining area. Indulge in exquisite dining at the restaurant, experience some cosy hours by the fire pit, and take a refreshing dip in the infinity pool. A Serengeti experience doesn’t get much better than this!
