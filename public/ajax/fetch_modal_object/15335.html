Mawe Mawe Manyara Lodge

The Mawe Mawe Manyara Lodge is a boutique lodge on the edge of the Rift Valley overlooking Lake Manyara National Park. It serves as a gateway to Ngorongoro Crater, Serengeti and Tarangire. The lodge features a main house and villas (some with plunge pools and outdoor showers) that blend modern comfort with local materials, offering privacy, spacious interiors, and breathtaking views across the valley. The main building has an infinity pool, a large entertainment area, a spa, a restaurant, and a bar – great for winding down after a safari day. And when you sit by the fire after a meal of freshly prepared food and fine wines and admire the African sky, you know you found a special place in the middle of the wilderness.
