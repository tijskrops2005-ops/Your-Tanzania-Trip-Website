Ngorongoro Lion’s Paw Camp by Karibu Camps and Lodges

The Lion’s Paw Camp nestles in between a grove of acacia trees on the eastern rim of the Ngorongoro crater. The camp has 8 spacious ensuite tents on wooden platforms with fixed roofs, all boasting a classic decor with lush beds and fancy bathrooms. The bottom of the crater is just a 10-minute drive away, but from the camp, you already get a taste of it due to the marvellous views. The scenic wood-and-glass restaurant offers the possibility to eat inside or on the large terrace with a view, but if you feel romantic you can also choose to dine in your room. Lion’s Paw is a great place to stay before or after your descent into the crater, where you enjoy all the luxury you need while you are surrounded by some of the most ancient nature one can find on this earth.
