The Kilimanjaro Luxury Camp

The Kilimanjaro Luxury Camp is an exclusive retreat, located on the lush slopes of Mount Kilimanjaro in Moshi, just 14 kilometres from Kilimanjaro National Park. The camp features beautifully appointed tented suites, designed to blend modern comfort with traditional African safari charm. They are fitted with ensuite bathrooms (including premium toiletries, bathrobes, slippers and hairdryers), complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi, air-conditioning, desks, in-room safes and private terraces. 2 presidential tents also feature a private pool, perfect for night swims. The general amenities include a bar, a swimming pool, a wellness centre and a restaurant.
