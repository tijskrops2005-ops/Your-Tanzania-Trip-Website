Arusha Coffee Lodge by Elewana

The Arusha Coffee Lodge is an extraordinary accommodation on the outskirts of Arusha, where it’s surrounded by lush green coffee plantations. The wooden, charming, and elegant aesthetic in the 18 rooms and 12 suites will make you feel right at home. Rooms are luxurious and spacious, with a sumptuous bathroom in a classic design. The very hospitable staff makes you feel even more at home, by helping you with everything you need.
This is an ideal lodge for people who want to learn more about the process of coffee making, as you can do a tour of the plantations. But there is also a spa with massages on offer, a swimming pool, free Wi-Fi, and special activities for children. Regarding food, you have several restaurants to choose from. In the Garden Buffet, you find succulent meats, vegetarian dishes, and delicious desserts, whereas the Grill Room offers an elegant place with a gourmet menu and a long list of excellent wines. In addition, the lodge boasts a bistro that is open all day and night. And of course, you cannot leave this lodge without trying their most wonderful coffees…!
