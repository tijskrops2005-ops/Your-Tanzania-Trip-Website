Rufiji River Camp by Foxes Safari Camps

The Rufiji River Camp lies beautifully on a riverbank of the Rufiji River in Julius Nyerere National Park. With 11 luxury double tents under thatched roofs and 3 tented suites with two tents each for families, it offers true relaxation in the middle of the jungle. Tents are spacious with wooden floors, beautiful bathrooms and large terraces with views over the nearby river. The shaded pool is a great way to cool off after an active day and the restaurant and bar overlook the river and allow for al fresco dining under the stars.
