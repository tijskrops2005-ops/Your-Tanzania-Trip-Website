Pazuri Inn – Arusha

The Pazuri Inn is located on Arusha’s outskirts, one hour from Kilimanjaro International Airport and en route to Tarangire National Park. It has a swimming pool, a restaurant with lounge, a bar, an outdoor terrace, a fireplace, and a TV room for watching football matches with other guests. Rooms are available for up to four people, furnished with king-size and/or twin beds, mosquito nets, and an ensuite bathroom. The Pazuri Inn is suitable for stays before or after a flight, and before or after a safari in the northern parks.
