How to get your visa for Tanzania

Once you have booked one of our safaris in Tanzania or holidays on Zanzibar (great news!), your next step is applying for a tourist visa. Many people find this worrying and feel unsure about it. How do you apply? Where? Which documents are required? Fortunately, a Tanzanian visa is fairly simple to arrange, particularly if you book with us. Keep reading to find out more.

For a trip to Tanzania you can obtain your visa either online or on arrival*. Both options are valid and acceptable, although the government of Tanzania would rather you applied online, because it wants to present Tanzania as a modern country. Further down we have set out the advantages and disadvantages of each route. Let's start with the costs, though.

TANZANIA VISA: HOW MUCH DOES IT COST?

What you pay for a Tanzanian visa varies a little depending on your nationality. Travelling from Europe? Then you pay 50 USD. If you hold US citizenship, you need a multi-entry visa, which costs 100 USD. Each is valid for three months, but with the multi-entry visa you can leave Tanzania and return on the same visa within a year.

These prices are for visas bought online. On arrival, the fee is 50 USD or 50 EUR (or 100 USD for a multi-entry visa). That is slightly odd, since 50 EUR is obviously worth more than 50 USD. You can also pay by credit card, in which case you pay 50 or 100 USD plus the card charges. It’s your choice.

ONLINE APPLICATION

Applying online is what the Tanzanian government would like you to do, and to be honest, there are some real advantages:

  • It is a fairly straightforward process, all the more so with the explanatory PDF we send you when you book with us.
  • When you get on the plane, you know your visa has been sorted and paid.
  • After landing, you only need to wait at a single counter for your visa stamp.

An online application is made through the official portal of the government of Tanzania. Please do NOT use any other website, as these are either pricier or fraudulent. We know a number of travellers who lost money to such scams, so it pays to be careful. You will find the official portal here:

https://visa.immigration.go.tz/

If you book with us, you receive a special PDF that walks you through each step, and our travel consultants are always on hand to advise you. We are not able to submit the visa application on your behalf, but with this help it is hard to make a mistake. Once you have sent your application, processing and approval can take as long as ten days. Be sure to bring a printed copy or a download of it on your trip. When you land, you simply join the queue at the immigration counter to have the visa stamped into your passport. After that you pick up your luggage and you are ready to go.

GETTING YOUR VISA ON ARRIVAL IN TANZANIA

Can't or don't want to apply online? Then, as of January 2026, travellers from most countries* can also get their visa on arrival. Be ready for a little African bureaucracy; apart from that, it is a fairly simple procedure:

  • When you land at the airport, you complete an application form (have a pen with you and keep the address of Your Tanzania Trip and of your first lodge handy).
  • Apply at the visa counter.
  • Then head to a second counter to pay the visa fee, in cash or by credit card.
  • At a third counter, your passport receives the visa stamp.
  • Get your bags, and you’re good to go.

So with a visa on arrival you stand in line three times, while an online application means just one queue when you land. The traditional route may hold you up a little, but remember, you are now in the land of ‘pole pole’ – there's no rush in Africa! Better get used to it straight away. And between ourselves: as most travellers now apply online, the single queue for online visas is often far longer than the three queues for visas on arrival. You may even get through faster!

THE CHOICE IS YOURS

These days most travellers choose to apply online, simply so they know they have the visa in hand before they leave. That makes sense. If you go for the online option, be well prepared:

  • Submit your application three to four weeks before your trip, in case it is rejected. That leaves you time to fix any errors and reapply.
  • Keep a credit card at hand to pay, or make sure you know how to send an international bank transfer.
  • Prepare a digital passport photo, a photo of the biodata page of your passport (max 300 kB) and a PDF of your plane ticket (max 1 MB).
  • Check that your passport will still be valid for six months after you return from Tanzania.
  • When children are travelling with just one parent, you need a written letter in English in which the other parent consents to the trip, which you then upload into the system.
  • Bring a pen for your arrival in Tanzania, as you are sometimes still asked to complete a form.

Feeling a little adventurous and planning to get your visa on arrival? Then make sure you are prepared before you fly as well.

  • Bring the fee in cash (USD/EUR), or pay with your credit card.
  • No passport-size picture is needed.
  • Keep a pen ready for the application form, along with the addresses of Your Tanzania Trip and of your first hotel.
  • When children are travelling with just one parent, you need a written letter in English in which the other parent consents to the trip.

How you apply for your visa is entirely your choice – go with whatever feels least stressful. Just keep the aim in sight: getting the visa. Only once you have it can you collect your luggage and begin your safari adventure with us.

 

*Citizens of certain countries require a Referral Visa and cannot apply on arrival. Please check this before choosing how to apply. Visas on arrival may also be discontinued for everyone at some point, so before you travel, look at the immigration guidelines to make sure the option still exists.
