Responsible Travel

When you travel with Your Tanzania Trip, you are choosing a responsible, sustainable and safe way to see Tanzania. Contributing to the country's social and economic development matters a great deal to us as a company, and we take part in a number of initiatives that do exactly that.

Because we are a trusted Tanzanian travel company offering exceptional encounters with wildlife and nature, we know how much Tanzania's great wild places need to be protected and preserved. For that reason we are committed to running our business sustainably and to playing an active part in environmental projects across the country.

Prioritising carbon-neutral activities

  • We select our luxury lodges and other Tanzanian accommodation partners against a strict environmental checklist. High on that checklist is how sustainably they operate, from cutting waste to running on green energy. Solar panels power 75% of the places to stay that Your Tanzania Trip offers.
  • Low-impact experiences are at the heart of the tailor-made Tanzania vacations we put together. Whether it is hiking, biking, canoeing or camping, a lot of what our guests love most leaves nothing behind but actual footprints.

Tree planting

Alongside building sustainable choices into our guests' trips, we work to shrink our own carbon footprint through the Manyara Tree Planting Project. We were proud to start it in 2020 on a 30-acre plot in the Manyara region, and have since added a further 18 acres close to Arusha National Park. So far that has allowed us to put thousands of indigenous trees in the ground.

Training vulnerable street kids

Youth unemployment is a serious problem in Tanzania, with many children and young adults unable to go to school or get an education. A large number of kids also end up living on the streets of the country's big cities. At Your Tanzania Trip this is a cause close to our hearts, and we are determined to do our part. In 2022 we founded the Hakuna Matata Academy, with the goal of giving vulnerable young people a chance while also raising the standard of hospitality in Tanzania. It got off to a good start with six trainers and a range of courses, and today:

  • Each year 200 street children take part in our life-skills programme, the long-term goal being to prepare them for work in hospitality.
  • We arrange hospitality courses for the teams at the hotels we work with, helping to raise standards across Tanzanian tourism.
  • We teach guides about wildlife and about why professional service to clients matters.

Supporting our employees

Our dedicated employees are our most important capital. From the head office in Arusha to the expert safari guides out in the bush, every guide and driver in our partner's crew is selected by Baraka personally, and the crew is paid above the local average. We believe in investing in our staff and providing everyone with the opportunity to thrive.

Fair practices on Kilimanjaro

Every climb is run by our partner Kilimanjaro Nature Tours in Arusha, founded by Baraka Dalali, who started on the mountain as a porter himself. Porters are hired properly, with fair pay, proper gear and weighed loads; guides are licensed by the Kilimanjaro National Park authority and trained in Wilderness First Response, first aid and CPR; and everyone on the mountain gets a daily health check. Anyone with serious symptoms descends.

On Kilimanjaro the crew works to Leave No Trace principles so that trekking disturbs the mountain's ecosystem as little as possible, and roughly 5% of every trip goes towards schooling in a village close to the mountain. Travelling with Your Tanzania Trip means travelling with a crew that is well cared for, on a mountain that is well cared for.
