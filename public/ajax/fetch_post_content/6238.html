Hakuna Matata Academy

At the Hakuna Matata Academy, the conviction is that knowledge is power. Set up in 2022 by the founders of Your Tanzania Trip, the HMA is a hands-on initiative based right next to their office in Arusha. It runs specialised hospitality training programmes for two groups: vulnerable street kids and professionals already working in hospitality and tourism. Nothing like the Hakuna Matata Academy existed in Tanzania before.

Knowledge is power

The Hakuna Matata Academy:

  • Offers training and opportunities to vulnerable street kids
  • Opens up rewarding careers for Tanzanians in the expanding tourism sector
  • Provides guides with additional training in wildlife and professional customer service
  • Encourages sustainable business practices across Tanzania, for a healthy and happy country
  • Drives economic growth through better-quality services and more professionalism in travel and tourism.

“The Hakuna Matata Academy (HMA) grew out of our wish to make a positive difference in Tanzania. HMA creates opportunities for vulnerable street kids and for people employed in tourism alike. In the long run, Your Tanzania Trip wants to raise both the standard of service and the sustainability of the tourism sector. Making an impact in a sustainable way benefits people and the environment at the same time!”

Our founder, founder Your Tanzania Trip

A blueprint for success

Six trainers currently work at the Hakuna Matata Academy. Each of the 80 lodges that partner with Your Tanzania Trip can send a professional to the academy's training and mentoring programmes. After a month of theoretical and practical instruction by local and international experts from the sector, HMA graduates leave with a broad, professional skill set that raises the standard of service in the lodges and opens up real opportunities for their own careers. Alongside this, 200 young adults from vulnerable groups are currently being trained to take up internships at the 80 participating lodges. That training lasts a year, and this year 100 participants have already been able to start their internships.

Funding for the Hakuna Matata Academy comes with support from the DeveloPPP programme of DEG Impulse, a German investment organisation for developing countries, which in turn is funded by the German Federal for Economic Cooperation and Development.
