Our Guarantees

No worries… or… Hakuna Matata!

A well-known phrase to anyone who has ever travelled to Africa, and exactly how we want you to feel when you book with us. So here is, in plain words, what Your Tanzania Trip guarantees and how your money and your trip are protected.

Who you book with

You book with Your Tanzania Trip (Tijs Krops, Chamber of Commerce 97223344, VAT NL005256557B94) in the Netherlands. Your trip is operated in Tanzania by Kilimanjaro Nature Tours, a licensed Tanzanian tour operator and member of the Tanzania Association of Tour Operators, founded by Baraka Dalali. We confirm every booking with them in writing before we send you an invoice.

How your payment works

  • You pay a deposit when you confirm your trip and the balance before departure; the exact amounts and dates are on your proposal and invoice.
  • Park fees, permits and lodges are reserved in your name as soon as your deposit is in, so your dates are fixed.
  • We do not use a travel guarantee fund. We tell you that up front, and we keep the money trail short: one invoice, one bank account (Knab, IBAN NL74 KNAB 0776 8630 37), no third parties in between.

What we guarantee

  • A clear price. The price on your proposal is the price you pay. Park fees, accommodation, transport, guide, meals as listed and the permits are in it; what is not included is written down.
  • A licensed crew. Guides are licensed by the Kilimanjaro National Park authority and trained in Wilderness First Response, first aid and CPR; porters are hired fairly, with proper gear and weighed loads.
  • One contact, two time zones. Tijs Krops in the Netherlands before and during your trip, and Baraka's team in Arusha on the ground, by phone, e-mail and WhatsApp.
  • Flexibility. Change your dates, route or lodges before departure and we rework the proposal; on the ground the crew adapts the day to the weather and the wildlife.
  • Honest reviews. We show our partner's score with the date we checked it and link to the source, and we ask you to review your trip where the next traveller can read it.

Travel insurance

Take out travel insurance that covers cancellation, medical costs and, if you climb Kilimanjaro, high-altitude evacuation. We are happy to tell you what to look for; see travel insurance.
