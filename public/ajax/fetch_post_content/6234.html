Flying Doctors Services

When you are planning a safari in East Africa, the thought of a medical emergency far from anywhere may cross your mind. Fortunately, the AMREF Flying Doctors can come to the rescue: they run an air ambulance service across all of East Africa, with doctors and pilots ready around the clock. For 40 USD per person, covering 30 days, you can head to even the most remote spots without worry in one or more of these countries: Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda, Burundi and Rwanda.

Trodden on a snake?
Should a medical emergency occur, Flying Doctors can offer expert advice or evacuate you straight away to a hospital in Nairobi, Kenya. Say you are unlucky enough to tread on a snake in Kenya’s Masai Mara: a single phone call puts you in touch with professional advice, which may mean you can carry on with your trip.
Or suppose a freak accident leaves you with a badly broken leg as you board a boat in Uganda’s Queen Elizabeth National Park, or an existing medical condition flares up in the middle of Tanzania’s Serengeti National Park (touch wood!). Within a short time, a plane carrying a full team of medical professionals can be ready for you at the closest airstrip. From there the team flies you directly to the hospital in Nairobi, where you can receive further care.

What is not covered
Bear in mind that Flying Doctors always judges which help is required, and covers only emergency assistance and transport. You pay yourself for a visa (if you arrive from outside Kenya and are flown to Nairobi), as well as for the hospital and your treatment. It is no substitute for health and/ or travel insurance. That said, AMREF Flying Doctors can make the visa process easier, so that you get to the hospital in time.

In a life-threatening situation on Mount Kilimanjaro, Flying Doctors can organise a helicopter evacuation to a hospital in Moshi. Be aware that the highest altitude at which they can land depends on the weather, the terrain and approximate weight-lift limits. You can read more about this in their FAQs. Climbs of Mount Meru are NOT covered in any way by the Flying Doctors Insurance. So please check that your travel insurance includes this kind of evacuation.

Travel with peace of mind
We put your safety first, so we are happy to help you sign up for the Flying Doctors Insurance. While you are on safari we do everything we can to make sure all goes smoothly, and 99 per cent of the time our clients return home having had the best time of their life.

Still, it is wise to remember that you are heading into wild, untamed nature, where the animals are in charge and the unexpected can happen. That is why Flying Doctors have been in the air since 1957, letting safari lovers travel with complete peace of mind. After all, we all enjoy some adventure – just not too much!

Travel Insurance Tanzania

We ask all our clients to take out proper travel insurance. Besides medical costs, travel insurance also covers things such as damaged or lost luggage.

Cancellation Insurance Tanzania

Taking out cancellation insurance is a good idea. For long trips, cancelling sometimes means that only part of the amount is refunded. Check this with your insurer so you know you are properly covered.
