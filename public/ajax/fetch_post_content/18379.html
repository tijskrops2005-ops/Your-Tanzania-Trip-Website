Choose your payment method

Our clients come from many different countries, so we have to stay as flexible as we can. For that reason we hold a bank account in the Netherlands for bank transfers, and we also take credit/ American Express/ debit card payments (through iDeal, Bancontact, EPS, Giropay and Sofort, though not Paypal).

For some people a bank transfer is the easier option, while others would rather use a (credit) card for international payments. The choice is entirely yours.

How to make a bank transfer

In some countries bank transfers are the norm and in others they are rare, yet we receive them from every corner of the world. Provided you have the details required for an (international) transfer, the process is fairly simple and can normally be completed online or in your banking app.

Two things are essential for an international transfer. The first is the recipient’s IBAN, their bank account number in the international format. The second is the BIC/SWIFT code, the international identifier of your bank.
