Zanzibar vacation

Picture a dream holiday on the tropical island of Zanzibar. This bounty island is a true paradise, with some of the loveliest beaches and coral reefs anywhere. Endless stretches of white sand dotted with palm trees, lapped by the clear azure water of the Indian Ocean. Besides relaxed beach life, the island has a colourful history and culture too. A visit to Stone Town is well worthwhile. The captivating old town is full of pleasant markets, restaurants and historic sights.

Together with Pemba, Mafia and lots of smaller islands, Zanzibar Island forms the Zanzibar archipelago. People also call them the Spice Islands, after their long history in the spice trade and their numerous spice plantations. They make wonderful beach destinations for washing off the safari dust.

Zanzibar beaches

Some of the loveliest beaches in the world are found on Zanzibar. Think soft white sand, bordered by colourful coral reefs and the crystal-clear Indian Ocean. The island counts around 25 beaches, with fishing villages scattered along them. Will you stay in the north, or would the south or east coast of Zanzibar suit you better?

North:
The finest beaches on Zanzibar lie along the north and east coasts, each with brilliant white sand and colourful coral reefs. Nungwi and Kendwa are among the most popular beaches, as the tides there are not an issue. The mood is relaxed, and there are plenty of restaurants, bars and places to stay. If real peace and privacy is what you want, you are better off in another area of the island (such as the east or south). Villages in the north include Uroa, Pongwe, Kiwengwa, Nungwi and Kendwa.

East:
The east coast is where the most idyllic beaches are. A lovely coral reef stretches along this coastline, one of the world's top places for snorkelling and diving. Here you can dive into rich marine life, including sea horses, turtles and many tropical fish. Tides on the east coast are strong. At low tide you can stroll along the beach and take in the views of the ocean. For fans of windsurfing or kitesurfing, this is the place to be. On this side of the island, Matemwe beach is the longest and one of the loveliest. As in the north, you can choose from a wide range of accommodation.

South-East:
This area is famous for its long stretches of soft sand. Thanks to the constant winds, it is the best part of the island for sailing, kitesurfing and windsurfing. Board a traditional wooden sailing boat (dhow) to head out to sea, or swim with dolphins at Kizimkazi (south). This is where you find small villages such as Jambiani, Paje and Bwejuu. These little fishing villages have a pleasant, timeless feel and friendly residents. Jambiani is one of the villages we like best. Find out why we believe Jambiani is the place to be on Zanzibar.

South-West:
On the south-west coast things are a little more remote, with fewer lodges and empty beaches. The west and south of Zanzibar draw fewer visitors, although you will find some lovely beaches with mild tides. If you head south, make sure you visit the Menia Bay Conservation Area, a spectacular underwater world for divers. The west coast is where you find the captivating old Stone Town, the historical and cultural heart of Zanzibar.

Exursions during your Zanzibar holiday

Zanzibar, the tropical island, is famous for its lovely white beaches and coral reefs. Yet besides relaxed beach life and water sports, it also has a colourful history and culture. There is no end to the possibilities, and everyone will find plenty to do on Zanzibar. Below is our selection of the best excursions on Zanzibar.

  • Spice tour
    Head to a spice farm and find out how spices, herbs and fruit are grown and used as medicine.
  • Kitesurfing
    With its constant sideshore winds, Zanzibar is perfect for windsurfing or kitesurfing, above all on the beaches of Paje, Kiwengwa and Nungwi.
  • Mountain bike tour
    Fancy a bit of exercise and adventure? Explore the island on a bike tour. You get a good look at everyday life and at the lovely natural surroundings.
  • Stone Town walking tour
    Zanzibar's historical heart is the old Stone Town, a truly amazing mix. Food lovers and culture fans alike can explore fascinating cultural spots such as the Sultan’s Palace, the House of Wonders, the Slave Market and the Darajani or Forodhani food markets. You can even have dinner in the former home of Freddie Mercury, who was born on Zanzibar.
  • Prison Island
    Pay a visit to this uninhabited island, where slaves were once held. Today, Prison Island is a spot for relaxing on its pretty white beach, perfect for snorkelling, swimming or sunbathing. You can also see the ruins of the old prison and around a hundred giant land tortoises.
  • Safari Blue
    Take in the natural beauty of Zanzibar aboard a traditional wooden sailing boat (dhow). You stop at 3 lovely islands to swim, snorkel and tuck into a tasty barbecue. Mnemba Atoll, one of the finest snorkelling spots on Zanzibar, is another popular snorkelling trip.
  • Jozani Forest
    Walk through the last native forest and mangroves of Zanzibar. The enormous palms here are a striking sight, and you will see many monkeys, including the rare red colobus monkey.
  • The Rock restaurant
    Set on a rock in the sea, this restaurant is a great spot to have a meal and take in the view.
  • Chumbe Island
    A small, uninhabited coral island that is a real paradise for snorkellers. It was the first private marine park in the world and has just 7 lovely eco-friendly bungalows, set in untouched nature.

Best time to visit Zanzibar

The dry season, from June to October and from December to February, is the busiest time to visit Zanzibar. That said, the island makes a great beach destination throughout the year. The wet season (mid-March, April, May, November) is lovely too, as showers rarely last more than 30 minutes and temperatures stay at around 28 C. The hottest month on Zanzibar is February (average 28 C) and the coolest is July (average 24 C). Thanks to the tropical climate, any time is a good time to go.

Zanzibar Hotels

On Zanzibar you can choose from a huge range of lodges, hotels and resorts, from simple places to stay right up to ultra-luxury. As Your Tanzania Trip knows most of the accommodation on Zanzibar, you are always welcome to ask us for advice. Our own preference is the personal feel of the island's small bed & breakfast lodges. The staff are very friendly and go out of their way to make sure you enjoy your stay. A further advantage of bed & breakfasts is that you are free to try other restaurants and bars during the day and in the evening. There are lots of great places to eat and drink, with lovely ocean views and a great atmosphere.
