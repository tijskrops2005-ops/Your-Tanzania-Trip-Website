Day 1

Arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport for your climb

Silver

Our Your Tanzania Trip guide is waiting for you when you land at Kilimanjaro International Airport. He takes you to your hotel so you can rest after your flight.

Getting ready for your climb: Before the climb starts, you meet your guide for a briefing, with advice on clothing, packing and other useful tips to make the climb as comfortable as possible for you.

Day 2

Umbwe Route (1/6) | Umbwe Gate (1,600 m/5,249 ft) - Umbwe Cave Camp (2,900 m/9,514 ft)

Today your driver collects you from the hotel and drives you to the Umbwe Gate, where you complete the registration formalities. Your Kilimanjaro climb starts in dense rainforest, where various animals can be heard and seen. Closer to the Umbwe River the path gets steeper, with views of Kibo Peak in the distance and the deep valleys all around you. After 5-7 hours the climb ends at Umbwe Cave Camp.

Day 3

Umbwe Route (2/6) | Umbwe Cave Camp (2,900 m/9,514 ft) - Barranco Camp (3,950 m/12,959 ft)

You leave the rainforest behind and enter the heather zone, surrounded by rocks and valleys. At the Umbwe Route Ridge the climb gets steeper, then levels out again close to the Barranco Valley. After hiking for 4 to 5 hours, you have dinner and spend the night at the Barranco Camp, in the valley beneath the Barranco wall.

Day 4

Umbwe Route (3/6) | Barranco Camp (3,950 m/12,959 ft) - Barranco Wall (3,977 m/13,047 ft) - Karanga Camp (4,040 m/13,254 ft)

This morning you walk to the Barranco Wall, which offers a spectacular view of the Kibo volcano on your left and Mount Meru on your right. After that, a more demanding stretch to Karanga Camp starts, with plenty of ups and downs. At the camp you have lunch and a few hours of rest. You can then do a 300-metre acclimatisation hike towards Barafu Camp before heading back down to Karanga Camp to sleep.

Day 5

Umbwe Route (4/6) | Karanga Camp (4,040 m/13,254 ft) - Barafu Camp (4,645 m/15,239 ft)

Your fourth day on the mountain takes you to Barafu Camp, from where you make your summit push to Uhuru Peak. You reach the camp around midday, have lunch and rest for a while. Later on, you do one more acclimatisation walk to Kosovo Summit Camp (4,870 m) before going back to Barafu Camp. A hot dinner warms you up, and you relax for the rest of the day before setting off for the top of Kili at midnight.

Day 6

Umbwe Route (5/6) | Barafu Camp (4,645 m/15,239 ft) - Uhuru Peak (5,895 m/19,340 ft) - Millenium Camp (3,820 m/12,532 ft)

At around midnight your final push to Uhuru Peak begins. You climb on good trails while your guide watches over you, checking how you are coping physically and mentally with the altitude. When you reach the top, take in a beautiful sunrise from the roof of Africa! After celebrating at the summit, you go down to the nearby glacier, then return via Barafu Camp and, after a two-hour break, carry on descending to Millennium Camp.

Day 7

Umbwe Route (6/6) | Millenium Camp (3,820 m/12,532 ft) - Mweka Gate (1,640 m/5,380 ft) - Hotel

Silver

Millenium Camp lies in a tropical rainforest. Here you feel relief at the lower altitude and satisfaction at having reached the summit the previous day. After breakfast you walk to the exit of the national park, and your driver brings you back to the hotel to recover from the demands of the climb.
