Family safaris in Tanzania

A family safari in Tanzania is a rare chance to meet astonishing wild animals your children may only know from television or books. Watch mighty lions, tall giraffes and enormous elephants wander freely. Travelling in 4×4 safari vehicles, which we can adjust to suit your kids’ needs, you will explore national parks such as the renowned Serengeti, the vast Ngorongoro Crater and smaller treasures like Arusha National Park. Do your children have a favourite African animal? Let your guides know and they will go looking for it!

Should your visit to Tanzania fall at the right moment, seeing the Great Wildebeest Migration is something you cannot skip. It ranks among the greatest natural spectacles on the planet: more than 1.5 million wildebeests, joined by hundreds of thousands of zebras and gazelles, on the move in search of fresh grazing. Standing among those coordinated masses of animals thundering across rivers and plains is an incredible sight… one that will leave your whole family speechless.

Private, tailor-made family tours in Tanzania

Our private Tanzania family tours let you shape your dream itinerary around your family’s interests and way of travelling. Would you like the kids to have a lie-in? Stay up late to admire the Milky Way? Or head back to camp early for a swim? No problem – just tell us in good time.

With your own child-friendly, well-informed English-speaking guide and your own safari vehicle, you set the pace of your Tanzania safari. The freedom of a private tour also lets you change the route when your family spots an animal they want to keep following across the savannah. You can stop whenever you like for a toilet break or a photo, so you can take in Tanzania’s wonderful landscapes and exotic wildlife entirely on your family’s terms.

Savannah plus paradise: a winning combination

Tearing the kids away from the daily excitement of safari, with lions, elephants and other wildlife up close, might sound impossible. But the promise of unspoilt beaches, turquoise sea and lazy days of swimming, snorkelling and building sandcastles could well do the trick.

Give your family the best of both worlds in Tanzania: start with an unrivalled safari adventure and follow it with a dreamy tropical beach break.

Family-friendly Zanzibar, or quieter options such as Mafia Island and Pemba, make a wonderful second act. Fill your days chasing waves, tucking into fresh seafood barbecues on the beach and watching the kids make new friends while you savour a little island romance. It may well be the best 2-in-1 deal you will ever get.

Adventures island-style

Naturally, you can also keep the explorer mode switched on and fill your days on these lovely islands with more exciting activities. Do your kids want even more animal memories? Then watch hundreds of baby turtles hatch on Mafia Island, or look out for dolphins and whales in the ocean. Or discover ancient ruins, caves made for Instagram and forests with a Jungle Book feel. There is far more to these islands than sandy beaches and sparkling water.

Things to do with your family in Tanzania

Adventurous, fun or cultural: Tanzania and its islands have plenty to offer. These are our carefully chosen top three excursions for a Tanzania family holiday you will never forget.

  1. Hot air balloon safari: Drift above Tanzania’s dramatic landscapes in a hot air balloon and enjoy a bird’s-eye view that delights children and grown-ups alike. Guided by experienced pilots, you float quietly over salt flats and grassy savannah while wildlife roams underneath. With these superb views over Tanzania’s Eden, our hot air balloon rides give your family memories to treasure together.
  2. Semi-submarine experience: Step aboard a semi-submarine and let your family discover the remarkable underwater world and marine life of Zanzibar. After the magical tour below the surface, a speedboat carries you to a castaway-style picnic on a sandbank, where white sand and aquamarine water invite you to swim and snorkel among tropical fish in the sun.
  3. Visit a Maasai village: Get to know Maasai traditions by calling on this fascinating tribe where they live. Have a go at spear-throwing alongside the warriors, ride a donkey and dance the Maasai way! Experiencing the authentic Maasai way of life at first hand gives your family a real bond with this proud and hospitable people.

Family-friendly accommodation

When travelling as a family, choosing the right Tanzania accommodation makes a real difference. Safari lodge, treehouse with monkeys in view or a safe, comfortable tented camp out in the wild – every place to stay has been personally tested and chosen by us. You’re welcome.

A few hotels even have a waterhole of their own. So if you or the children would rather not spend a whole day out on safari, you can simply unwind at the lodge and let the safari come to you.

When the adventure moves from land to sea, the beach resorts of Zanzibar pamper you with swimming pools, calm, child-friendly beaches and candlelit dinners at sunset… something to keep every member of the family happy.
