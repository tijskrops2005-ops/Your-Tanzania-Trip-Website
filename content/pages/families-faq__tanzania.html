Treat your family to the adventure of a lifetime – during an unforgettable family holiday in Tanzania. Your kids can see how the animals from their picture books come alive, and you can relax knowing that Tanzania is a safe and family-friendly holiday destination.

Yes, of course. In Tanzania, your kids can see how the animals from their picture books come alive, and you can relax knowing that Tanzania is a safe and family-friendly holiday destination. Our guides love children and have fun telling them gripping stories about nature, the Big Five and lion cubs – just as in the Lion King.

Upon creating your trip, we make sure that we organize it for your kids AND for you so that you can all enjoy it to the fullest. Depending on your situation, it means: shorter safari days and more time to enjoy the pool, children seats in the car, with extra snacks and Wi-Fi. Also, we carefully select accommodations with special facilities for your little ones, like pools, activities, or even nannies.

There is no minimum age to enter Tanzanian national parks. As long as your kids are under the age of 15, you even pay a lower entry fee for them. But just to give you an idea: most families who book a family-friendly safari with us have kids who are at least six years old. If you think that your kid is still a little too young for a long safari, a few safari days followed by a trip to Zanzibar could be the best option. Or you simply wait one, two, or three more years – Tanzania will be waiting for you.

If you plan a family holiday in Tanzania with kids, best check with your doctor or health office regarding recommended vaccinations and medication. It’s also advisable to take out additional Flying Doctors insurance. If something happens (knock on wood it won’t), a helicopter can transport you to the nearest hospital straight away.

Yes, babies are welcome! With us, you travel in your own, private 4×4 landcruiser which allows you to be flexible and have some privacy. Your little one can rest in their baby seat (ask your travel consultant to book one for you!) while you are enjoying the views. Our safari jeeps are often also equipped with a fridge for milk etc. and if you let us know special dietary wishes of your little ones beforehand, we can accommodate them as well. Also, let us know if you need a baby bed in the lodges… we’ll arrange that for you.

Of course, you can rent a car seat simply by contacting your travel consultant. We want to make sure that you can travel comfortably with your kids, with baby or booster seats that can be rented for the safari cars. Please make sure to reserve your baby seat before your arrival, for your kids’ safety and comfort during the safari (price on request).

In general, lodges love children, but not all lodges offer the same services for the little ones – some only have a few. In some lodges, you might find supervised playgrounds, animation teams or nanny services, while others just offer a swimming pool and a kids’ menu. Some of these services are complimentary, for others you have to pay extra. Your consultant is happy to inform you about the options and help you to make the best choice!

Best tips from our consultants!

Because it’s all about the right preparation…

  • Book accommodation with a swimming pool: kids have a lot of energy and need to have fun after a safari day.
  • Check with your doctor which vaccinations or medications they might need, and don’t forget to take an insurance as well.
  • Packing only needs a good system! Pick out the outfits at home and pack them into the suitcase as a set – underwear, socks, t-shirt, trousers, skirt, jumper, etc. If you stack all of these clothes on top of each other and then roll them up, you can save a lot of space. Each morning, take out the next outfit, wrinkle-free! Put the dirty clothes in a separate bag, done!
  • Bring snacks and drinks, or order special snacks and drinks with us, so that no one of you gets hangry during the adventure.
  • Bring some toys or order Wi-Fi in the car in case your kids get a little bored.
  • Always have some baby wipes at hand.
  • Diaper bags are handy if you have to change nappies but don’t have a bin somewhere close.
  • Don’t forget sunscreen and insect repellant – that’s always important, no matter if you travel with or without kids.
