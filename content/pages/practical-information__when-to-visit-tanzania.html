When is the Best Time To Visit Tanzania?

When you should travel to Tanzania depends on what you hope to see, where you want to go, your budget and your interests. Each period of the year has its advantages and drawbacks. This guide will help you work out the best time for your Tanzanian adventure. Oh, and one thing before we forget…Everyone is different, so there is no wrong answer. What is certain, though, is that a Tanzania Travel is beautiful at any time of year!

The finest months to go: June to October & December to February

For a trip to Tanzania, these months are the crème de la crème! That is why we rated them ‘Excellent’. See June to October as nature’s blockbuster season, with sunny skies, wildlife in abundance and the epic wildebeest migration. December to February feels like winning the safari jackpot: green, lush landscapes and adorable baby animals wherever you look. And March, April, May and November are perfectly good too—if you enjoy vivid greenery, bird-watching and having the park nearly to yourself, those months are a hidden gem. There may be rain, yes, but still!

June to October (Dry Season)

In this period the wildlife viewing is at its best, as animals gather around the waterholes; July and August are the peak months for seeing the dramatic wildebeest migration and the river crossings, and the weather is sunny and pleasant.

December to February (Dry Season)

The dry season carries on, bringing excellent game viewing and agreeable weather; January and February are especially memorable for the calving season in the southern Serengeti, which draws in plenty of predators…👀

March (Rainy Season)

The rains arrive and turn the landscape green and vibrant, with superb bird watching and fewer tourists, which makes for a more intimate safari. And lower prices too, of course!

April to May (Rainy Season)

Heavy rainfall makes some roads tricky, but the scenery is spectacular and the parks are quiet, with lower rates that make for a peaceful and affordable safari.

November (Rainy Season)

The short rains begin and bring the landscape back to life with flowers in bloom and green forests, while the northern parks still offer good wildlife viewing. It is also usually less crowded.

Best time to visit Tanzania: month by month

Best time to visit Tanzania: highlights month by month. The highlights are based on climate, wildlife, the safari experience and feedback from our local guides.

Dry season: probably the best season to visit Tanzania

The dry season is the most popular period for a safari in Tanzania. Why is that?

At this time of year, animals gather at rivers and waterholes, so they are easier to spot. The pleasant temperatures of 25-27 °C with sunny, rain-free days are ideal for game drives and outdoor activities. Picture yourself in the game drive car under a shining sun, while the wildlife heads to the waterholes for a well-needed drink.

Fewer mosquitoes and better roads make your safari more enjoyable. The dry season is also the best moment to see the wildebeest migration, in particular the dramatic river crossings in the northern Serengeti. And if you plan to climb Kilimanjaro, the clear blue skies and the cooler temperatures from evening until early morning create perfect climbing conditions.

When is the dry season?

  • January
  • February
  • June
  • July
  • August
  • September
  • October
  • late December

Pros of traveling during the dry season

  • Animals gather at rivers and waterholes, so they are easier to spot
  • Pleasant temperatures of 25-27 °C and sunny, rain-free days
  • July and August are ideal for seeing the wildebeest migration and the epic river crossings
  • Fewer mosquitoes
  • Better roads and easier game drives
  • The best time for the southern parks; the northern parks can be visited all year round
  • Cool temperatures from the evening until early morning
  • Clear blue skies
  • Ideal conditions for climbing Kilimanjaro

There are, however, a few drawbacks to bear in mind:

Cons of traveling during the dry season

  • Accommodation and tours are more expensive
  • Unique accommodation tends to sell out fast!
  • More tourists in this popular period, so parks and lodges are busier

Would you rather avoid the busier parks and higher prices? Read on!

 

Rainy season: for an intimate safari experience

The rainy season, also called the wet or green season, delivers a different yet just as captivating experience. Why? Nature is lush and vibrant, with flowers in bloom and green landscapes. Bird watching is at its peak, and the parks are quieter, which makes your safari more intimate. On top of that, lower rates make it a more affordable time to travel.

When is the rainy season?

  • March
  • April
  • May
  • November
  • Beginning December

Advantages of travelling in the rainy season

  • Nature is colourful and vibrant, with flowers in bloom and green forests and grasslands
  • Wildlife viewing in the northern Tanzanian parks is good all year
  • Excellent bird watching
  • Wildlife is in good condition
  • Lower rates make the safari cheaper
  • The parks are quieter, which makes the safari more intimate

There are, however, a few drawbacks to remember:

Disadvantages of travelling in the rainy season

  • Rain can make some roads hard to drive
  • A greater chance of running into mosquitoes
  • Afternoon showers can disrupt plans

Is there anything else to consider besides the dry and rainy seasons? Tanzania’s tourism falls into three distinct seasons: high, mid and low. These run parallel to Tanzania’s rainy and dry seasons. Every season brings its own particular benefits and challenges, which helps you choose the best time for your visit according to your preferences and priorities.

Low, Mid, and High Season in Tanzania

High season: January, February, June to October and December.

Travelling to Tanzania in the high season means ideal weather and the best chances of seeing wildlife. With sunny skies, pleasant temperatures and the incredible wildebeest migration, it’s the ideal time for a safari adventure you will remember.

Pros:

  • Ideal weather for both safaris and beach stays
  • Best wildlife viewing opportunities

Cons:

  • Parks and attractions can be busy
  • Highest hotel and tour rates
  • Unique hotels are fully booked
  • Most tourist services and activities are available

Mid season: March, 1 November to 21 December.

The mid-season strikes a balance between high and low season, which makes it a good choice for travellers who want moderate prices and fewer crowds without giving up too much in terms of weather and wildlife sightings.

Pros:

  • Moderate hotel rates
  • Fewer tourists than in the high season

Cons:

  • Some attractions may be available only to a limited extent
  • Variable weather conditions

Low Season: April and May

The low season suits budget-conscious travellers who don’t mind some rain and are after a calmer, more peaceful experience of Tanzania’s stunning landscapes.

Pros:

  • Lowest hotel and tour rates
  • Least busy parks and attractions

Cons:

  • Some lodges and camps may be closed
  • The most rainfall, which may affect your travel plans

Whatever the season, Tanzania offers a unique and beautiful experience, so your trip will be memorable whenever you come!

See the Great Migration in Tanzania all year round

Good news: the awe-inspiring Great Migration can be seen throughout the year as it moves between the Maasai Mara in Kenya and the Serengeti in Tanzania. Whenever you travel, this incredible spectacle will not pass you by. Below we set out when and where the highlights take place, so you can decide what matters most to you!

December – March

See the breathtaking Great Migration in the southern Serengeti, including the magical sight of newborn animals in February.

July – September

Witness the thrilling river crossings in the northern Serengeti, one of the most spectacular highlights of the Great Migration.
