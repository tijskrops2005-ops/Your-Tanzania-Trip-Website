Manyara Best View Lodge & Spa is a unique lodge in one of the most beautiful places in Africa! Enjoy the surrounding nature, the exotic birds, the spacious rooms, the good restaurant, and the full-service spa with its relaxing massages and beauty treatments. Rooms in this lodge are comfortably furnished with good beds, a private bathroom, and a veranda with a unique view as the lodge lies at the top of the famous Rift Valley Escarpment. The views over the endless African savannah stretching hundreds of kilometres before you are truly mesmerising.

Imagine sitting on the veranda with a drink, overlooking the infinity pool, and enjoying the vast view during sunset: On the left, you look at the magnificent 350-meter-high escarpment wall that curves slightly forward and goes all the way to Lake Natron, at the bottom of the escarpment are a lot of local banana farms (which you can also visit), in the distance you look up against the vast Mbuyuni hillsides that turn into the Manyara Ranch (a significant wildlife migration zone), and on the right Lake Manyara in its full glory. Relax here and enjoy the moment in the Manyara Best View Lodge! Good to know: the lodge is cashless, so bring a card for any extras.
