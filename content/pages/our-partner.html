Our local partner: Kilimanjaro Nature Tours

Every trip you book with Your Tanzania Trip is operated in Tanzania by Kilimanjaro Nature Tours. This page tells you who they are, who you will meet, and how the roles are divided, so you know exactly who you are dealing with before and during your trip.

Meet Baraka Dalali

Baraka grew up in the Udzungwa Mountains, came to Kilimanjaro as a porter and worked his way up to licensed guide (KINAPA) before founding Kilimanjaro Nature Tours in Arusha. He is certified in Wilderness First Response, teaches first aid and CPR to his crew, and still answers every message himself.

Baraka was also where Your Tanzania Trip began: Tijs Krops and Edrian Waterman did their internship with him in Arusha, helped him set up his company for international guests, and decided to keep working together. You can read that story on our About page.

The crew you will meet

Guides, cooks, drivers and porters, most of them from Arusha and the villages around the mountain. Guides are licensed by KINAPA and trained in Wilderness First Response, first aid and CPR. Porters are hired properly, with fair pay, proper gear and weighed loads, and about 5% of each trip funds schooling in a local village.

On safari you travel in a private 4×4 with your own driver-guide; on Kilimanjaro you climb with a guide, assistant guides, a cook and porters, with daily health checks on the mountain. On Zanzibar the team hands you over to trusted local hosts and picks you up again for the flight home.

Who does what

  • Your Tanzania Trip (Tijs Krops, Nagele, the Netherlands): Your Tanzania Trip plans your trip with you, sends the proposal and the invoice, and is your contact before departure.
  • Kilimanjaro Nature Tours (Baraka Dalali, Arusha, Tanzania): Kilimanjaro Nature Tours provides the guides, drivers, vehicles, mountain crew, park permits and bookings, and looks after you during the trip.

How a booking works

  1. Plan with us. Send us your dates and wishes; you get a day-by-day proposal and a price from Your Tanzania Trip, free and without obligation.
  2. We confirm with Baraka. Once you say yes, we fix the itinerary, lodges and permits with Kilimanjaro Nature Tours and send you the invoice and the travel documents.
  3. You travel with his crew. From the airport in Kilimanjaro or Arusha onwards, Baraka's team takes care of you. We stay reachable in the Netherlands throughout.

Recognised by

Kilimanjaro Nature Tours is a member of the Tanzania Association of Tour Operators, works under Tanzania National Parks and the Tanzania Tourist Board, and has received the Tripadvisor Travellers' Choice award in 2023 and 2024.
