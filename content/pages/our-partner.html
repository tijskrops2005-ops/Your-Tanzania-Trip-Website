Our local partner: Kilimanjaro Nature Tours

Every trip you book with Your Tanzania Trip is operated in Tanzania by Kilimanjaro Nature Tours. This page explains who they are and how the roles are divided, so you know who you are dealing with before and during your trip.

Who is Kilimanjaro Nature Tours?

Kilimanjaro Nature Tours is a tour operator based in Arusha, Tanzania, in northern Tanzania, led by Baraka Dalali. From Arusha they run safaris on the northern and southern circuits, trips around the Great Migration, Kilimanjaro climbs and Mount Meru climbs, and combinations with Zanzibar.

When you travel with us, their guides, drivers, cooks and porters are the people you see every day. They know the parks, the roads and the lodges, and they make the decisions on the ground, for example when the weather changes or a route can be improved.

Who does what

  • Your Tanzania Trip: Your Tanzania Trip plans your trip with you, sends the proposal and the invoice, and is your contact before departure.
  • Kilimanjaro Nature Tours: Kilimanjaro Nature Tours provides the guides, drivers, vehicles, mountain crew, park permits and bookings, and looks after you during the trip.

Contact during your trip

Before departure everything goes through Your Tanzania Trip. During the trip you also have the direct contact details of Kilimanjaro Nature Tours:
