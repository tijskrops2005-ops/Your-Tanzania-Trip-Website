Located in central Tanzania, Ruaha National Park is the second-largest park you can visit on a Tanzania holiday, surpassed only by Nyerere National Park. Because it is relatively hard to reach, visitor numbers are far lower than in any park of similar size on the Northern circuit. The Great Ruaha River runs through the park and is its most important waterway.

Plant life in Ruaha is remarkably varied, yet the landscape is defined above all by acacias and the many baobab trees. More than 1,650 plant species have been recorded here.

Wildlife in Ruaha National Park

Ruaha holds an enormous number of African elephants; some people regard it as the densest elephant population anywhere in Africa! The park probably shelters more elephants than any other park in East Africa. It is also home to large mammals such as kudu, sable and roan antelope, which you won't find in any other national park, and close to 10% of all of Africa's lions live here as well! Leopards, cheetahs and giraffes can be spotted too, while zebras, elands, impala, bat-eared foxes and jackals roam the park in large numbers. No world-famous great migration passes through Ruaha, but it remains a wonderful park to explore. If luck is on your side, you might even catch sight of the endangered wild dogs in Ruaha National Park!

Reptiles and amphibians in the park

Reptiles and amphibians have their place here too. Crocodiles, venomous and harmless snakes, monitor and agama lizards and frogs all live in and along the river. The ecosystem of Ruaha National Park is still largely untouched and unexplored, which gives your safari a character all of its own!

Birds in Ruaha

Ruaha is not only the perfect spot for watching elephants; birders will feel in heaven here as well! Some 571 bird species have been counted in Ruaha National Park, among them the Crested barbet, Dickinson's kestrel, Pale-billed hornbill, Violet-crested turaco, Racket-tailed roller, Plovers, Kingfishers, Hornbills, Green-wood hoopoes, Sunbirds, Bee-eaters and Egrets.

Activities and excursions in Ruaha National Park

For a quiet yet spectacular safari, Ruaha is a superb choice.

Ruaha pairs beautifully with other parks on the Southern circuit, including Udzungwa National Park, Mikumi National Park and Nyerere National Park.

  • Ruaha to Udzungwa: 5-6 hrs drive

Best time to visit Ruaha

The road into Ruaha can be used all year round. Ruaha National Park has two rainy seasons: the short rains start in November (February is the top month for birdwatching!), while the long rains fall from April to May. The dry season in the park runs from June to October.
