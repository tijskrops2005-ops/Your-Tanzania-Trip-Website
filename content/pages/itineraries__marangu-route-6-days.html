Kilimanjaro in 6 days on the Marangu Route, sleeping in huts

It may be among the quickest and cheapest ways to the summit, yet the Marangu route still holds lots of memorable moments for you. You get to explore the mountain’s nature along varied trails and take in the view from the top.

The drawbacks: it can get fairly crowded, above all in peak season, there is less time for your body to adjust to the altitude, and because you come down along the same path, you see less of the stunning scenery.

On the other hand, if camping is not really your thing, you’ll be glad to know that you sleep in huts on this route. One final fun fact: people also call it the “Coca-Cola” route… in case you would like to impress your guides and fellow hikers.

Day 1

Arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport for your climb

Our Your Tanzania Trip guide is waiting for you on arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport. He takes you to your hotel, where you can rest after the flight.

Getting ready for your climb: Before the climb begins you meet your guide, who gives you a briefing and advice on clothing, packing and other useful tips to make the climb as comfortable as possible for you.

Day 2

Marangu Route (1/6) | Marangu Gate (1,860 m/6,102 ft) - Mandara Huts (2,720 m/8,923 ft)

Early in the morning your guide drives you from the hotel to Marangu Gate at 1,860 m. The trek begins among coffee and banana plantations and continues through untouched rainforest. Make the most of the chance to enjoy the wonderful views of green vegetation and varied wildlife on the way. A seven-hour hike brings you to the Mandara Huts. In the afternoon you can go to the Maundi Crater, a volcanic crater close to the huts, or just rest and take in the lovely setting. It is your first day at this altitude, so be sure to drink lots of water!

Day 3

Marangu Route (2/6) | Mandara Huts (2,720 m/8,923 ft) - Horombo Huts (3,720 m/12,204 ft)

After Mandara Huts the scenery gradually changes. Your trek passes through beautiful landscapes with unusual plants and animals, and you are surrounded by the impressive beauty of Mount Kilimanjaro. As you climb higher, there is less and less vegetation, and the land begins to resemble a lunar landscape. The higher you get, the thinner the air becomes. Enjoy the excitement of gaining height as you head for the cosy, comfortable Horombo Huts, where you have dinner and spend the night.

Day 4

Marangu Route (3/6) | Horombo Huts (3,720 m/12,204 ft) – Kibo Huts Camp (4,720 m/15,485 ft) – Horombo Huts (3,720 m/12,204 ft)

Today is an extra day to acclimatise. After breakfast you hike in the direction of Kibo Huts and then return. This gives your body plenty of time to adjust to the altitude. In the afternoon, after lunch, you can photograph the Mawenzi Volcano, explore the surroundings or simply rest. Sleep well at the Horombo Huts once more so that you are ready for tomorrow.

Day 5

Marangu Route (4/6) | Horombo Huts (3,720 m/12,204 ft) - Kibo Huts Camp (4,720 m/15,485 ft)

See the landscape change dramatically as the hike takes you across a type of Alpine desert to a spot between Kibo and Mawenzi, the two famous mountain peaks. Here the terrain is more open and bare, with less shelter. It is a tough hike, but the excitement of nearing the impressive summit of Mount Kilimanjaro keeps you moving. You reach Kibo Huts Camp in time for lunch. Remember to drink lots of water and make sure you rest well before the big day!

Day 6

Marangu Route (5/6) | Kibo Huts Camp (4,720 m/15,485 ft) - Uhuru Peak (5,895 m/19,340 ft) - Horombo Huts (3,720 m/12,204 ft)

During the night you set off to conquer the impressive Uhuru Peak, Africa's highest point! The hardest stretch is the first section of the climb, up to Gilman's Point. From there Uhuru Peak is close by and fairly easy to reach. At the summit you can enjoy a beautiful sunrise, which is very impressive at this altitude! You then descend via the Kibo Huts Camp to the Horombo Huts for dinner and the night. Today you are on your feet for around twelve hours, so be ready for a demanding yet rewarding day!

Day 7

Marangu Route (6/6) | Horombo Huts (3,720 m/12,204 ft) - Marangu Gate (1,860 m/6,102 ft) - hotel

Your remarkable journey ends as you walk down to Marangu Gate, where you leave Kilimanjaro National Park. This lovely walk of five to six hours leads you through beautiful forests. At Marangu Gate your guide is waiting to drive you back to the hotel, where you can enjoy a well-earned rest and celebrate what you have achieved. Unforgettable memories, stunning views and a real feeling of accomplishment lie ahead, and they will stay with you forever!

Day 8

Flight home from Kilimanjaro International Airport after the climb

Unfortunately your holiday is over and it is time to travel home. Make the most of your final hours in Tanzania until our driver collects you and brings you to Kilimanjaro International Airport for your flight back. Would you like more? You can also combine your Kilimanjaro adventure with a few extra days of safari or a beach holiday!
