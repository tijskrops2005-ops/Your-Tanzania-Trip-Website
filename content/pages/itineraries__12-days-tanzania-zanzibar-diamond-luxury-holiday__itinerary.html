Itinerary

For many people, discovering everything Tanzania has to offer is a dream. Whether it is an epic Tanzania safari in search of Africa’s Big Five, a Kilimanjaro climb or time on the paradise beaches of Zanzibar: you will find it all in Tanzania. The good news is that Your Tanzania Trip can put together a custom made itinerary around all your wishes.

Looking for inspiration? Our most popular itineraries are a good place to start. Most of our clients like to round off their exciting safari with a few relaxing days on one of Zanzibar’s most stunning beaches. Does that appeal to you too?

Whether you pick one of our popular Tanzania itineraries or would rather shape the journey yourself, we are happy to help. Join us and get ready to discover Tanzania at your own pace, just the way you want!

Day 1

Arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport

Diamond Luxury

When you step out of Kilimanjaro International Airport, your own Your Tanzania Trip guide is there to meet you with a broad smile: 'Karibu Tanzania - welcome to Tanzania!' Your guide drives you directly to the hotel, so you can unwind and soak up the African atmosphere. The following day you set off on an exclusive private safari where you set the pace yourself. You have your own safari jeep, and you are free to stop and watch the animals for as long as you wish!

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Arusha Coffee Lodge by Elewana
Diamond Luxury

Day 2 - 3

Tarangire National Park - luxury style!

Diamond Luxury

Grandeur is the theme in majestic Tarangire National Park - you pass countless giant baobab trees while you look for large herds of elephants. These giants are not the only big game here, because the Tarangire River and Lake Burunge draw countless lions, zebras, giraffes, leopards, cheetahs, pythons and monkeys to the water. For the smaller species, the grandeur is in their numbers, as Tarangire is also a true haven for birdwatchers!

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Tarangire Treetops by Elewana
Diamond Luxury

Day 4

A taste of culture - Mto wa Mbu: Mountain bike ride & Maasai village

Diamond Luxury

Close to Lake Manyara National Park lies the village of Mto wa Mbu, a green oasis that stands out among the Maasai plains around it. Hop on a mountainbike for a taste of the local culture and explore this charming little village, the lush green countryside around it and the banana plantations. Afterwards you leave your bike to visit a Maasai village nearby, where Maasai tribesmen explain their way of life to you.

Activities:

On the slopes of the Ngorongoro Highlands, Gibb's Farm keeps to the age-old rhythm of an African farm and coffee plantation. Stroll around this peaceful, tranquil sanctuary and see vegetables being picked, coffee being roasted, cows being milked and pigs being fed. The farm's chefs draw inspiration from the vegetable gardens, fruit trees and herbs, and they then treat visitors to a delicious lunch. A small slice of heaven!

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury The Manor At Ngorongoro by Elewana
Diamond Luxury

Day 5

Ngorongoro Crater - luxury style!

Diamond Luxury

Today you go down into the wondrous caldera of the famous Ngorongoro Crater. This is THE day for an early start to your game drive, when there are plenty of animals and few tourists! The hairs on your arms will stand up as you descend, amazed by the clouds hanging over the crater and the many animals in view. You can find jackals, hyenas, buffaloes, wildebeests, gazelles and many more animals here, and you are almost guaranteed to see them all.

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury The Manor At Ngorongoro by Elewana
Diamond Luxury

Day 6

Serengeti National Park luxury style - by plane

Diamond Luxury

Fly in to Serengeti National Park, stunningly beautiful and the very essence of safari. Here you can admire the infamous Big Five at their best, and during certain months watch thousands of wildebeests stream past on the Great Migration. Even outside those months this is a paradise for big cats; lions rest in prides on the kopjes, cheetahs hide in the tall yellow grass and leopards lounge up in the trees. Your arrival leaves time for a wonderful game drive in the afternoon, heading into the sunset.

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Lemala Nanyukie Tented Lodge
Diamond Luxury

Day 7

Serengeti National Park - luxury style!

Diamond Luxury

Whatever you imagine when you think of a luxurious safari, Serengeti National Park is sure to have it. Not just the Big Five, the famous Great Migration and many big cats, but also rarer species like the gerenuk or the wild dog, and did we mention more than 500 species of birds? Along the way you will naturally see antelopes, giraffes, elephants, zebras, hippos and many other animals. Be sure to get up at sunrise for an epic balloon safari...!

Activities:

A balloon safari over the iconic Serengeti National Park turns your Tanzania safari into an experience to remember. You go up at sunrise and drift gently across the endless Serengeti plains. The hot air balloon glides at treetop height over the baobabs and acacias, so you can see the animals close by. Looking down on the wildlife from the balloon is a fascinating, peaceful experience, with breathtaking panoramic views to go with it.

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Lemala Nanyukie Tented Lodge
Diamond Luxury

Day 8

Flight to Zanzibar (included in the price)

Diamond Luxury

Today you take a flight to Zanzibar, the spice island. At the airport our guide meets you and drives you to your hotel. In the days ahead you can explore white sandy beaches, clear turquoise water and colourful coral reefs. Interested in culture? Make your way to Stone Town and immerse yourself in the history of this intriguing little town. If you love food, you can visit the spice plantations and get to know the varied flavours of the tasty local cuisine. A tropical paradise is waiting for you!

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Baraza Resort and Spa
Diamond Luxury

Day 9 - 11

Zanzibar - luxury style!

Diamond Luxury

After the beautiful yellow hues of the savannah in Tanzania, your flight takes you to the fabulous white beaches of... Zanzibar! At a luxurious five-star all-inclusive resort on the coast, a fantastic team takes care of your every need, while you swim, surf or simply relax with a cocktail in a hammock. Picture your own slice of paradise: kilometers of beaches fringed with palms, traditional wooden dhows far out on the horizon, and an ocean of such a deep blue that you will want sunglasses just to look at it…!

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Baraza Resort and Spa
Diamond Luxury

Day 12

Outbound international flight home from Zanzibar Airport (ZNZ)

Your final day! Depending on when your flight leaves, you can unwind on the beach or see a little more of the island. After that, you head to Zanzibar International Airport in good time for your flight home. Not quite ready to leave? You can also add another paradise island to your holiday, such as Mnemba, Pemba or Mafia!
