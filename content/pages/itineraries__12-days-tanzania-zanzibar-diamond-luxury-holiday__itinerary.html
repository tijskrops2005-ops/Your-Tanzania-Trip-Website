Itinerary

Exploring all that Tanzania has to offer is a dream for many. From epic Tanzania safari adventures spotting Africa’s Big Five, to climbing Kilimanjaro or relaxing on the paradise beaches of Zanzibar: Tanzania has it all. Good news: Your Tanzania Trip can build a custom made itinerary that suits all your wishes.

Our most popular itineraries can help you find some inspiration. Most of our clients love to end their exciting safari with a few days of relaxation on one of Zanzibar’s most stunning beaches. Would you like that as well?

Whether you choose one of our popular Tanzania itineraries or prefer to customize your journey, we are here to assist you. Come aboard and get ready to explore Tanzania at your own pace, exactly the way you like it!

Day 1

Arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport

Diamond Luxury

As you walk out of Kilimanjaro International Airport, your personal Your Tanzania Trip guide greets you with a big smile: 'Karibu Tanzania - welcome to Tanzania!' The guide takes you straight to your hotel, where you can relax and take in the African atmosphere. The next day your guide takes you on an exclusive, private safari that allows you to travel at your own pace. Enjoy the luxury of your own safari jeep and take the liberty to stop and watch the animals as long as you like!

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Arusha Coffee Lodge by Elewana
Diamond Luxury

Day 2 - 3

Tarangire National Park - luxury style!

Diamond Luxury

In the majestic Tarangire National Park, the focus lies on grandeur - cross paths with the countless gigantic baobab trees, in search of vast herds of elephants. And these giants are not the only large game you find here, since the Tarangire River and Lake Burunge attract countless lions, zebras, giraffes, leopards, cheetahs, pythons, and monkeys to their waters. For smaller species, the grandeur lies in the numbers, as Tarangire is also an absolute haven for birdwatchers!

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Tarangire Treetops by Elewana
Diamond Luxury

Day 4

A taste of culture - Mto wa Mbu: Mountain bike ride & Maasai village

Diamond Luxury

Mto wa Mbu is a village close to Lake Manyara National Park, where it stands out as a green oasis in the middle of the surrounding Maasai plains. Get a taste of culture and discover this lovely little village, the surrounding lush green nature, and the banana plantations by hopping on a mountainbike. Then get off your bike to visit a nearby Maasai village, where some Maasai tribesmen tell you all about their way of life.

Activities:

Gibb's Farm offers the old-age rhythm of an African farm and coffee plantation, right on the slopes of Ngorongoro Highlands. Walk around on this sanctuary of peace and tranquility, and watch vegetables being harvested, coffee roasted, cows milked, and pigs fed. The vegetable gardens, fruit trees, and herbs provide inspiration for the farm's chefs, who in their turn amaze visitors with a delicious lunch. A little piece of heaven!

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury The Manor At Ngorongoro by Elewana
Diamond Luxury

Day 5

Ngorongoro Crater - luxury style!

Diamond Luxury

Today you descend into the wonderous caldera of the famous Ngorongoro Crater. This is THE day to start your game drive early when animals are plenty and tourists few! Feel the little hairs on your arms rise as you descend into the crater, awed by the sight of clouds hovering and the numerous animals in sight. Coyotes, hyenas, buffaloes, wildebeests, gazelles, and many more animals can be found here and you are almost guaranteed to see them all.

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury The Manor At Ngorongoro by Elewana
Diamond Luxury

Day 6

Serengeti National Park luxury style - by plane

Diamond Luxury

Fly into the mind-blowing beautiful Serengeti National Park for the epitome of safari. See the infamous Big Five in all their glory, and, in certain months, wildebeests running by the thousands in the Great Migration. And even without that; this is big-cat-paradise with prides of lions on kopjes, cheetahs in the long, yellow grass, and leopards in trees. You arrive in time for an amazing afternoon game drive into the sunset.

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Lemala Nanyukie Tented Lodge
Diamond Luxury

Day 7

Serengeti National Park - luxury style!

Diamond Luxury

Anything you can think of when you picture a luxurious safari, you will surely find in Serengeti National Park. Not only the Big Five, the famous Great Migration, and a multitude of big cats, but also rarer species such as the gerenuk or the wild dog, and did we mention the plus 500 species of birds? In between, you naturally spot antelopes, giraffes, elephants, zebras, hippos, and many more animals. Make sure to wake up at sunrise for an epic balloon safari...!

Activities:

A balloon safari in the iconic Serengeti National Park will make your Tanzania safari a memorable experience. You rise at sunrise and gently fly over the endless plains of the Serengeti. The hot air balloon floats at treetop height above the baobabs and acacias so that you can see the animals nearby. Watching the wildlife below from your balloon is a fascinating and peaceful experience that comes with breathtaking panoramic views.

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Lemala Nanyukie Tented Lodge
Diamond Luxury

Day 8

Flight to Zanzibar (included in the price)

Diamond Luxury

Today you fly to the spice island of Zanzibar. Our guide picks you up at the airport and takes you to your hotel. Over the next few days, you can discover white sandy beaches, crystal-clear turquoise waters and vibrant coral reefs. Into culture? Then head to Stone Town and dive into the history of this fascinating little town. Food lovers can also visit the spice plantations and discover the diverse flavours of the delicious local cuisine. A tropical paradise awaits you!

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Baraza Resort and Spa
Diamond Luxury

Day 9 - 11

Zanzibar - luxury style!

Diamond Luxury

From the beautiful yellows of the Tanzanian savannah, you fly to the most fabulous white beaches of... Zanzibar! Get pampered by a fantastic staff in a luxurious five-star all-inclusive resort on the coast, and spend your days swimming, surfing, or just chillin' in a hammock with a cocktail in hand. Expect your own piece of paradise with kilometers of palm-fringed beaches, wooden dhows on the horizon, and an ocean so blue you need sunglasses to even look at it…!

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Baraza Resort and Spa

Outbound international flight home from Zanzibar Airport (ZNZ)

The last day! Depending on your flight time, you can relax on the beach or explore the island just a little bit more.
