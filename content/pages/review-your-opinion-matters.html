5 stars doesn’t do this experience justice! 🌟

Rita S / July 2025

5 stars doesn’t do this company or our INCREDIBLE guide justice. The honeymoon of our dreams thanks to this team and our driver and guide to all things Tanzania safari - Hussein Abdullah! The planning process was so easy, and the communication was so fast. Brighton helped us to plan our dream honeymoon in advance so we had nothing to worry about once we arrived. Every single one of our accommodations exceeded our expectations and the tour itself was completely tailored to our requests/ dreams for the trip. We were greeted at the airport by Hussein in a brand new Land Cruiser all to ourselves stocked with cold beverages, and some TS swag. We visited Villa Maua to rest up then headed to Tarangire Lodge for our first stop! Our time in Tarangire was some of the best, with an early morning visit with a male and female lion right by the road after some rain. We were the only car out so far and Hussein made sure it was a special moment by explaining the lions were on their “honeymoon” just like us! Then gave us a gift to celebrate the special occasion. From Tarangire we went to a Maasai village - Hussein chose one he frequents with guests for its true to culture experience and it was incredible. We have no words for our time in Serengeti- it was pure magic with all the animals and baby animals! After Serengeti we went through the Ngorogoro crater to complete the “big 5” with the black rhino. Every day we had extravagant picnic box lunches and Hussein always offered to stop for coffee or breaks or lunch whenever we wanted ! We felt so spoiled. After our incredible safari experience, we were taken to the airport for the later half of our trip to Zanzibar ! We were sad for our first half of our journey to end, but feel we made a lifelong friendship with Hussein and a connection the Your Tanzania Trip we’ll treasure for a lifetime. Thank you SO MUCH YOUR TANZANIA TRIP & of course our new friend and amazing guide Hussein !
