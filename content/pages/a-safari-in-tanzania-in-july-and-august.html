How is the weather in Tanzania during July and August?

Are you asking yourself, “Is July a good time to visit Tanzania?“—the answer is a clear yes! And August is every bit as good. The weather in Tanzania in July and August is cooler and drier, with the temperature sitting between 15°C and 25°C (59°F to 77°F). Such a pleasant climate suits game drives, safaris and any other outdoor adventure perfectly. No more sweltering heat—this is the time to discover Tanzania in comfort!

Why Visit Tanzania in July and August?

Discover the appeal of Tanzania in July and August, the months when nature puts on its most dramatic show. This is the famous season of the Great Migration, when thousands of wildebeest and zebras brave dangerous river crossings in the Serengeti – wildlife drama you will never forget.

The cooler temperatures make the adventure all the better, so it’s an ideal moment to discover Tanzania’s magnificent landscapes and lively ecosystems. Once the safari excitement is over, wind down on the dreamy beaches of Zanzibar, where rest and cultural discovery go hand in hand.

Whether you’re after close encounters with wildlife or quiet days on the beach, Tanzania in July and August gives you a rare mix of adventure and calm. So what exactly are the main reasons to visit Tanzania in summer?

The Great Migration in central and northern Tanzania

Between June and October you can watch one of the greatest spectacles in nature—the river crossing of the Great Migration. This is the season known for the dramatic crossings of the Mara River, where wildebeest and zebras put everything on the line as they travel through Northern Tanzania’s Serengeti. The cooler weather at this time also creates the best conditions for game drives and watching wildlife. It really is one of the Seven Natural Wonders of the World!

Cooler Summer Climate

In contrast to the hot, dry months earlier in the year, June to September brings cooler temperatures and a refreshing climate, not unlike a European summer. On safari or on an outdoor excursion, the mild weather lets you make the most of every moment. It is the ideal time to explore Tanzania’s national parks, and you will be glad of the comfortable climate while you track wildlife 😉. Do pack layers for your daily outings so you are prepared for “chillier” evenings on safari or on the beach in Zanzibar. Not a fan of safaris in sweltering heat? Then this is the moment to go on safari in Tanzania. Temperatures are lower than usual, so bring a sweater for the cooler evenings and you are all set!

Beach Relaxation in Zanzibar

Once the safari excitement is behind you, make your way to Zanzibar for some well-earned rest. Dig your toes into the soft, powdery sand at Nungwi or Kendwa, cool off in the crystal-clear sea or dive into the rich history and lively culture of Stone Town. Our favourite beach, Jambiani, will keep you in awe for your entire holiday. Paje is another beach we can warmly recommend for relaxing! With its ideal balance of relaxation and adventure, Zanzibar is the perfect place to retreat to after a safari.

Walking Safaris

If you would like a more personal wildlife experience, join a walking safari in Tarangire National Park, Manyara National Park or Arusha National Park (to name just a few). The cooler weather makes these walks all the more pleasant, while expert guides take you through the bush and point out small details you would easily overlook from a vehicle. Few other activities let you connect with nature as closely as this.
