Day 1

Arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport for your climb

Our Your Tanzania Trip guide meets you when you land at Kilimanjaro International Airport. He drives you to your hotel, where you can relax after your flight.

Preparing for your climb: You get together with your guide before the climb starts. He briefs you and advises you on what to wear and pack, and shares other important tips so that the climb is as comfortable as possible.

Day 2

Marangu Route (1/5) | Marangu Gate (1,860 m/6,102 ft) - Mandara Huts (2,720 m/8,923 ft)

On the first morning your guide drives you from the hotel to Marangu Gate. You first walk through coffee and banana plantations, after which you pass through untouched rainforest. A seven-hour hike brings you to the Mandara huts. In the afternoon you can walk to the Maundi crater, a volcanic crater near the Mandara Huts, or simply rest and enjoy the lovely surroundings. This is your first day at this altitude, so remember to drink lots of water!

Day 3

Marangu Route (2/5) | Mandara Huts (2,720 m/8,923 ft) - Horombo Huts (3,720 m/12,204 ft)

After Mandara Huts the scenery gradually turns from cloud forest into grass plains with heather and other plants. The higher you climb, the less vegetation you see, and the more it looks like a lunar landscape. By now the air is getting thinner as well. A few hours after lunch you can go on an acclimatisation hike that climbs 200 meters, before returning to Horombo Huts for dinner and the night.

Day 4

Marangu Route (3/5) | Horombo Huts (3,720 m/12,204 ft) - Kibo Huts Camp (4,720 m/15,485 ft)

Today the hike takes you through a sort of Alpine desert to a spot between Kibo and Mawenzi, the two famous mountain peaks. It is a tough walk through open terrain that offers less shelter. You reach Kibo camp for lunch and can then rest to prepare for the big day!

Day 5

Marangu Route (4/5) | Kibo Huts (4,720 m/15,485 ft) - Uhuru Peak (5,895 m/19,340 ft) - Horombo Huts (3,720 m/12,204 ft)

In the middle of the night you begin the climb to Uhuru Peak, by the light of your flashlight. The first part of the climb, up to Gilman's Point, is the hardest. From there Uhuru Peak is close and fairly easy to reach. At the summit you can enjoy a beautiful sunrise, which is very impressive at this altitude! Once you have celebrated your success at the top, you start the descent via the Kibo Huts Camp to the Horombo huts for dinner and the night. Altogether you are on your feet for around twelve hours today, so be prepared for a tough but rewarding day!

Day 6

Marangu Route (5/5) | Horombo Huts (3,720 m/12,204 ft) - Marangu Gate (1,860 m/6,102 ft) - hotel

On your last day on the mountain you walk down to Marangu gate, where you exit Kilimanjaro National Park. It is a lovely walk of five to six hours through Kilimanjaro's beautiful forests. Afterwards your guide drives you to the hotel for a well-earned rest.

Day 7

Flight home from Kilimanjaro International Airport after the climb

Unfortunately your holiday has reached its end, and it is time to go back home. Enjoy your final hours in Tanzania before our driver collects you and takes you to Kilimanjaro International Airport for your flight. Still want more? It is also possible to extend your Kilimanjaro adventure with a few days of safari or a beach holiday!
