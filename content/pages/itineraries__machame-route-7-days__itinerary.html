Day 1

Arrival at Kilimanjaro Airport and gear check

Silver

Your driver from Kilimanjaro Nature Tours meets you at Kilimanjaro International Airport and takes you to your hotel near Moshi or Arusha. In the afternoon or evening your head guide comes by for the briefing: he checks your gear piece by piece, explains the daily rhythm and answers your questions. Anything missing can be rented.

Day 2

Day 1 of 7: Machame Gate (1,800 m / 5,905 ft) to Machame Camp (2,835 m / 9,301 ft)

After breakfast you drive to Machame Gate, register with the park and meet the full crew. The first day is a five- to six-hour walk through rainforest, with the trees thinning out to heather as you gain height. The camp lies at the edge of the forest. Drink more than you think you need; the altitude starts to count from today.

Day 3

Day 2 of 7: Machame Camp (2,835 m / 9,301 ft) to Shira Camp (3,839 m / 12,595 ft)

A shorter but steeper day. You climb out of the heather onto a rocky ridge with the first views of Kibo, the summit cone, and the Shira peaks. Lunch and the afternoon are at Shira Camp on the plateau. Those who feel good can take a short acclimatisation walk towards the Lava Tower and back.

Day 4

Day 3 of 7: Shira Camp (3,839 m / 12,595 ft) via Lava Tower (4,630 m / 15,190 ft) to Barranco Camp (3,950 m / 12,959 ft)

Climb high, sleep low. The trail rises steadily to the Lava Tower, where you eat lunch at 4,630 metres; most people feel the altitude here. After lunch you descend into the Barranco valley, past the giant groundsels, to camp at the foot of the Barranco Wall. The drop in height overnight is exactly what your body needs.

Day 5

Day 4 of 7: Barranco Camp (3,950 m / 12,959 ft) to Karanga Camp (4,040 m / 13,254 ft)

The morning is the Barranco Wall: a scramble rather than a climb, with hands on the rock in a few places and a guide in front of you. From the top you look across to Mount Meru. The rest of the day crosses ridges and valleys to Karanga Camp, where you arrive for lunch and rest. An afternoon walk towards Barafu helps acclimatisation.

Day 6

Day 5 of 7: Karanga Camp (4,040 m / 13,254 ft) to Barafu Camp (4,645 m / 15,239 ft)

A short walk to Barafu Camp, the base for the summit. You arrive around midday, eat, and rest. Your guide may take you up towards Kosovo Camp (4,870 m) for a last acclimatisation walk. Dinner is early. You sleep in your clothes, because the summit attempt starts around midnight.

Day 7

Day 6 of 7: Barafu Camp (4,645 m / 15,239 ft) to Uhuru Peak (5,895 m / 19,340 ft) and down to Millennium Camp (3,820 m / 12,532 ft)

Summit night. You leave Barafu around midnight by headlamp, walking slowly in a line behind your guide. The trail is good; the altitude is the hard part. Stella Point on the crater rim comes with the first light, and Uhuru Peak about an hour later, with the glaciers glowing pink at sunrise. After photos and a short rest you descend, past Barafu for a meal, down to Millennium Camp in the moorland. A long day, and the one you will remember.

Day 8

Day 7 of 7: Millennium Camp (3,820 m / 12,532 ft) to Mweka Gate (1,640 m / 5,380 ft) and your hotel

Silver

You wake up with thick air and a summit certificate to collect. The last walk goes down through the rainforest to Mweka Gate, where you sign out of the park and say goodbye to the crew. A driver takes you back to your hotel for a shower and a proper bed.
