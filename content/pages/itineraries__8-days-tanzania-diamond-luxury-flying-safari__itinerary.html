Itinerary

For many people, discovering everything Tanzania offers is a long-held dream. Whether it is an epic Tanzania safari in search of Africa’s Big Five, a climb of Kilimanjaro or lazy days on Zanzibar’s paradise beaches, Tanzania covers it all. The good news: Your Tanzania Trip can put together a custom made itinerary that meets all your wishes.

For ideas, take a look at our most popular itineraries. Many of our clients like to round off an exciting safari with a few relaxing days on one of Zanzibar’s most beautiful beaches. Does that appeal to you too?

You can pick one of our popular Tanzania itineraries or have your journey tailored to you; either way, we are happy to help. Join us and get ready to discover Tanzania at your own pace, just as you want it!

Day 1

Arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport

Diamond Luxury

When you step out of Kilimanjaro International Airport, your own Your Tanzania Trip guide is there with a broad smile: 'Karibu Tanzania - welcome to Tanzania!' You are driven directly to your hotel, where you can unwind and soak up the African atmosphere. On the following day your guide sets off with you on an exclusive private safari, so you can travel at whatever pace suits you. You have the luxury of your own safari jeep and are free to stop and watch the animals for as long as you wish!

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Legendary Lodge by Legendary Expeditions
Diamond Luxury

Day 2

Discover Mto Wa Mbu in a Tuktuk

Diamond Luxury

Close to Lake Manyara National Park, the village of Mto wa Mbu forms a green oasis among the dry savannah plains. Each tuktuk holds no more than 3 people, and touring by tuktuk gives you as much flexibility as possible: they fit through narrow streets and can pull over wherever you want. The route takes you past the village, into the forest and along several farms and plantations. Within one or two hours you stop at a brewery, a local artist and a local school.

Activities:

Get to know Tanzanian cooking right in the heart of a banana farm. Choose three local dishes and watch and learn how they are made. You can learn to prepare pilau (spiced rice), makande (a tasty mix of corn and brown beans) or ndizi nyama (plaintains with meat), and get a real taste of Tanzania!

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury andBeyond Lake Manyara Tree Lodge
Diamond Luxury

Day 3

Lake Manyara National Park - luxury style!

Diamond Luxury

With scenery ranging from acacia woodlands and jungle forests to mountain cliffs and grassland, Lake Manyara National Park is perfect for a scenic safari. Thanks to its biodiversity, it is home to the remarkable tree-climbing lions as well as many other species, including baboons, elephants, buffalos, warthogs, giraffes, wildebeest, zebras and plenty more.

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury andBeyond Lake Manyara Tree Lodge
Diamond Luxury

Day 4

Gibb's Farm walk & lunch

Diamond Luxury

On the slopes of the Ngorongoro Highlands, Gibb's Farm keeps the age-old rhythm of an African farm and coffee plantation alive. Stroll around this peaceful, tranquil sanctuary and see vegetables being picked, coffee being roasted, cows being milked and pigs being fed. The farm's chefs draw inspiration from the vegetable gardens, fruit trees and herbs, and in turn they delight visitors with a delicious lunch. A small slice of paradise!

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Gibb's Farm
Diamond Luxury

Day 5

Ngorongoro Crater - luxury style!

Today you head down into the wonderful caldera of the well-known Ngorongoro Crater. On this day above all, an early start to your game drive pays off, while there are lots of animals and few tourists! As you drive down into the crater you will feel the hairs on your arms stand up, amazed by the clouds hanging overhead and the many animals around you. Jackals, hyenas, buffaloes, wildebeests, gazelles and many other animals live here, and you are almost certain to see all of them.

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury The Craters Edge Lodge – Wilderness Collection

Day 6

Serengeti National Park luxury style - by plane

Diamond Luxury

Take a flight into the stunningly beautiful Serengeti National Park for safari at its very essence. Watch the famous Big Five in full splendour and, in certain months, thousands of wildebeests racing past during the Great Migration. Even without the migration, this is a paradise for big cats, with prides of lions on kopjes, cheetahs in the tall yellow grass and leopards up in the trees. You get there in time for a wonderful afternoon game drive into the sunset.

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Lemala Nanyukie Tented Lodge
Diamond Luxury

Day 7

Serengeti National Park - luxury style!

Diamond Luxury

Whatever you imagine when you think of a luxurious safari, Serengeti National Park is sure to have it. Besides the Big Five, the well-known Great Migration and an abundance of big cats, you can find rarer animals such as the gerenuk or the wild dog, and did we mention more than 500 bird species? Along the way you will of course see antelopes, giraffes, elephants, zebras, hippos and many other animals. Be sure to get up at sunrise for an epic balloon safari...!

Activities:

Floating over the iconic Serengeti National Park in a balloon turns your Tanzania safari into an experience to remember. You take off at sunrise and drift gently above the endless Serengeti plains. The hot air balloon glides at treetop level over the baobabs and acacias, so you can see the animals up close. Observing the wildlife beneath you from the balloon is a fascinating, peaceful experience, with breathtaking panoramic views thrown in.

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Lemala Nanyukie Tented Lodge
Diamond Luxury

At the end of a wonderful safari adventure you fly back to Arusha, and from there you are transferred to Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) for your international flight home.
