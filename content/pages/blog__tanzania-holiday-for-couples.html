Explore Romantic Get-Aways

Romantic Adventures

Hot Air Balloon Safari

Drifting high above the world-famous Serengeti in a hot air balloon is a memory you will treasure for the rest of your life. Shortly after sunrise, when wildlife is at its liveliest, you float over the plains with no sound but the occasional burst of the burner. Normally the basket is shared with other passengers, but if your budget stretches that far, you can reserve it for just the two of you. Whatever shape this adventure takes for you, it is sure to carry you up to cloud nine 😉

Private Sunset Cruise

Set sail into the sunset over the Indian Ocean. A sunset cruise could be the ideal moment to pop the question, and at the very least it will leave you with a special memory. With snacks and drinks (or even a delicious dinner) as the sun slips below the horizon, romance does not get much better than this.

Night Safaris

A night safari adds an extra thrill, taking you into parks such as Arusha National Park or Lake Manyara National Park after dark. Fortunately your safari jeep comes with a searchlight, so you can pick out all the nocturnal creatures. And if the animal noises make your partner jump, simply shuffle a little closer together and everything will be fine.

Wedding Ceremony with the Maasai

Carry on the wedding you celebrated at home, or renew your vows, with a traditional Maasai wedding ceremony out on the savannah. The women of the tribe dress the bride in traditional clothing and jewellery and lead her into a wedding boma. The warriors then bring the groom to the bride, after which an elder gives you his blessing. The whole tribe receives you as a married couple, with singing, dancing and a meal of goat meat as the closing part of a Maasai wedding. Tie the knot (again): right here, and almost right now.

Thrilling adventures for couples in Tanzania

Walk from Empakai Crater to Lake Natron

Only you two, a few donkeys and a handful of Maasai, far from anywhere. The Empakai Crater is a smaller crater within the Ngorongoro Conservation Area, a few hours' drive from the well-known Ngorongoro Crater. The road ends at the crater rim, and from there you set off on foot together. Two nights under the stars later, you arrive at the bare yet magical landscape of Lake Natron, where it feels as though you are the last few people on earth. That is, until you get to your lodge and a well-earned massage and delicious dinner await.

Walking Safaris

To get even closer to Tanzania's plants and animals, join a walking safari. With an armed ranger leading the way, you meet the country's famous wildlife at close quarters and notice the small creatures, such as insects, birds and rodents, that you would normally overlook from a safari jeep. The thrill of walking through open savannah or a jungle teeming with wild animals is bound to bring you closer together.

Climb the Kilimanjaro

Admittedly, climbing Kilimanjaro is neither private nor especially romantic, since guides, porters and cooks come along too. Yet a climb shared with your partner can genuinely deepen your connection. Being worn out, urging each other on and finally standing on Uhuru Peak together makes a memory that lasts a lifetime. And if you still like the way each other smells after four or five days without a shower, you may well be made for each other.

Where to sleep? 4 romantic suggestions for couples in Tanzania

Lake Manyara Tree Lodge

This beautifully designed lodge perches in the treetops of Lake Manyara, deep in the jungle. Every treehouse offers a snug sitting room, a comfortable bed, a huge outdoor deck, a freestanding bath and a shower wrapped in tree branches. You have total privacy, and the lodge's Swarovski binoculars (crystals not included) let you watch the wildlife in the trees around you.

Emerson Spice Hotel in Stone Town

Stone Town's Emerson Spice Hotel feels as if it has stepped out of the tales of 1001 Arabian Nights. Rooms are furnished in Arabic style with sumptuous fabrics and warm colours, while the sounds of Stone Town drift in on the sea breeze through open windows. A night here feels like travelling back through time. Celebrate your love in the L’amour Toujours suite, or treat yourselves to Violetta, a courtesan's boudoir. Romantic dinners can be enjoyed in the Secret Garden or up on the rooftop terrace, where one of the city's finest restaurants looks out over Stone Town's corrugated rooftops to the blue Indian Ocean beyond.

Mnemba Island Lodge

When it comes to romantic escapes, Mnemba Island is THE place. A handful of private bandas on a tiny island make you feel like a Robinson Crusoe couple who got lucky: stranded, contented and thriving. Straight from your bed you can slip into the crystal-clear Indian Ocean and its magnificent coral reefs. Make the most of your outdoor shower, your double sunbed and each other. Dinner is served at a table set into the beach sand, with the sea lapping at your feet and endless stars overhead.

Fanjove Island

Just south of Mafia Island, this lovely little island is another ideal romantic hideaway. You can only get there by small plane followed by a boat, and it offers ten beautifully designed beach villas with complete privacy. Luxurious though the villas are, life here is a fairly simple beach existence, as your accommodation stands right on the white sand. Dive from your bed straight into the blue Indian Ocean and join the tropical fish and turtles for their morning swim. After a lazy day in paradise, a tasty dinner awaits in an old, restored lighthouse.
