The Legendary Zanzibar Beach Resort lies on the Michamvi Peninsula on one of Zanzibar’s gorgeous east-coast beaches. It boasts 48 rooms (partly interconnected) with a tropical yet modern twist, featuring air conditioning, a private bathroom, and a relaxing patio overlooking either the garden or the sea. Legendary won’t let you get bored, as you can plunge into one of the two outdoor pools, step out onto the private beach, dive into the ocean, rent bikes to explore the surrounding village, stay active in the fully equipped gym, unwind with a book in the comfy lounge area, or play snooker, table tennis, and other games in the recreation area. When hunger strikes, you’ll find the restaurant under a beautiful, high, thatched ceiling, serving a menu with dishes from tropical Zanzibar to European to other flavours of the world. The resort is fully cashless, so a card is all you need for meals, drinks and bike rentals. A great place for those long, easy Zanzibar beach days…!
