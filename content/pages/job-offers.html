Travel consultant

French, Swedish, Danish, Spanish, Norwegian en Italian

The days of a travel consultant are never the same – new clients, new routes, new wishes. A few things though will pop up on your daily to-do list.

That’s what you do:

  • You handle new customer requests, make follow-up calls and adapt travel routes.
  • You work with customers in their native language.
  • Together with your customers you create travel programs according to their wishes – our software system will support you in doing so.
  • You work with our teams in Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda as well as the reservations team for hotel bookings.
  • You work remote but you live in Africa, in the same or a similar time zone as East Africa (GMT +3).

 

That’s what you bring:

  • You can speak and write well in English and perfectly in your native language.
  • You have strong and friendly communication skills.
  • You have general computer skills and you are a fast typer with your 10 fingers.
  • You have good time and task management skills.
  • You have a great sense of responsibility and a commercial attitude.
  • You can work well on your own but also within the team.
  • You are flexible – sometimes a client call needs to be made in the evening for example.

 

That’s what you get:

  • You work within an international team and an informal culture.
  • You get regular comprehensive trainings.
  • You get a full-time contract.
  • You work in a flexible environment – during peak seasons it will be busier, but you can work less during quiet periods.
  • You get a good salary.
  • You make people happy.
