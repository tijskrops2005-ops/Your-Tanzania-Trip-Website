The 5 Best Locations For Your Honeymoon in Tanzania

1. Manyara Best View Lodge

This lodge is located on top of the Rift Valley escarpment, which gives you a unique view that stretches across East Africa, Lake Manyara, the mountains of Mount Kilimanjaro and Mount Meru. And the best thing: you can enjoy all of this from the comfort of your open accommodation.

You’ll find amenities such as an infinity pool, hiking, and bicycling tours, room service, and a lovely dining room that is sure going to remind you of the comforts of home in this pleasant spot.

You’ll find the setting sun scenes from your room are a special and illuminating way to spend time with your partner and set a cozy and romantic atmosphere that you’re sure to appreciate here.

2. Sound Of Silence Serengeti

If you want your Tanzania honeymoon to be that much more special, you could try this extraordinary wildlife and safari resort experience. Here you can enjoy a view over the endless plains of the Serengeti, which is an excellent way to connect with nature on your honeymoon.

The accommodation consists of tents that have a warm feeling about them and can give you the privacy you need while being spacious and comfortable.

You’ll likely want to spend more time viewing the animals that walk right outside your tent, and you’ll find the staff are very accommodating and can make adjustments to make your stay even better.

3. Ngorongoro Crater Lodge

Craving something unique during your holiday in Tanzania and you want to explore various aspects of the cultures of Tanzania? This lodge boasts views that give you a unique lovely view over the Ngorongoro Crater, which can take your mind to a place of pure fantasy.

Within the rim, you’ll be able to spot endangered black rhinos, giant tusked elephants, lions, and flamingoes that brighten the landscape, as you’ll want to get your walking shoes on to experience this wonder.

In your downtime, you can visit the local tribes, with the possibility of experiencing a crater floor banquet that has dining that is on par with regular boutique hotel services.

4. Jabali Ridge

This camp is set in the heart of the Ruaha National Park and forms a point between the vast landscapes of East and Southern Africa. It makes for a great spot among giant granite boulders, so you get great views of the plains and suites that are airy, yet provide a private and relaxing experience.

You have access to many amenities such as an infinity pool, spa, and many opportunities to go exploring out in the bush to view the area. With one-tenth of the entire world’s wild lion population, and the chance to see elephants, giraffes, antelope, and over 500 bird species, this is a must-do.

You can unwind after a day of exploring with local cuisine and even a nice drink as you and your partner gaze up at the stars, giving you a very romantic honeymoon setting.

5. Nimali Mara

This tented camp is a hidden oasis in a remote location that gives you all the luxury and opportunity to see the annual migration river crossing if you book at the right time of year – a fascinating and unforgettable experience if you’re looking for something different.

You will find this nurturing place of solitude which has beautifully designed rooms that blend in well with the surrounding area and is a very revitalizing experience if you choose this camp.

Of course, you want to see some wildlife, so take advantage of the game drives and see the big five, and maybe even some lion cubs if you’re lucky enough.

What If I Want More Variety On My Tanzania Honeymoon?

We understand that these locations aren’t going to be for everyone. Some people prefer to see the various towns and villages, while others may want a more chilled honeymoon location that doesn’t require a lot of moving around.

If you sit in any of these groups, you’ll find the country of Tanzania to be plentiful in honeymoon experiences, tours, and destinations that you’ll want to make an effort to arrange or see.

If you’re the sightseeing type, you could wander to historical areas such as Stone Town. You’ll find spots like Nungwi beach amazing if you want to simply take in the beautiful ocean view. So there’s bound to be something that appeals to you.

If you’re looking for something more active, you could go climbing Kilimanjaro by the Lemosho route, a 7-day journey. During the climb, you’ll see a range of climatic zones, such as the Arctic desert, which is an unforgettable view. Also with you are guides, chefs, and porters who will guide you through the terrain and ensure that you’re comfortable and fun by providing you with insights and trivia. Of course, you’ll need a moderate to good activity level for this climb, so consider this as well as any preparations you need to make.

What Customs Do I Need To Be Aware Of?

We want to ensure that your honeymoon in Tanzania is fun but also safe. The first thing you want to consider is your dress options, as women are discouraged from wearing sleeveless shirts and shorts away from the tourist resorts.

You might also want to avoid being too intimate with your partner in public areas, as the tradition believes that public displays of affection should be limited to the privacy of the couple’s dwelling.

These might sound limiting in some ways, but respecting religious and cultural traditions during your honeymoon is a good way for you to be respectful and avoid any scrutiny or issues that might occur if you’re not aware of these customs.

Conclusion

With many options available, you might be wondering what location works for you. But as a guide, it’s a good idea to see what surrounding amenities or experiences are available to suit the needs of your new husband or wife.

You have a chance to go somewhere that is different from the honeymoon-centered resorts that might not offer you this much freedom. You also get an opportunity to learn about other cultures that can give you great insights to liven your outlook.
