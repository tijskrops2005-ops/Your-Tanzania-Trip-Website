In this article we’ll walk you through the top 5 locations for a honeymoon in Tanzania, places that make your stay extra special and leave you with memories that last.

Your special day – the sequel

Before we get to what this article promises, we want to introduce the high point of Tanzanian romance: a traditional Maasai wedding! Yes, you can marry again, or renew your vows, in a ceremony that’s normally reserved for true Maasai. You dress in traditional clothing, receive a blessing from a tribal elder and are celebrated by the whole community, complete with food, dancing and singing!

The 5 best places for your honeymoon in Tanzania

1. Manyara Best View Lodge

This lodge sits high on the escarpment of the Rift Valley, giving you a one-of-a-kind view across East Africa, Lake Manyara, the peaks of Mount Kilimanjaro and Mount Meru. Best of all, you can take it all in from the comfort of your open-fronted room.

At this pleasant spot you’ll find facilities such as an infinity pool, hiking and cycling tours, room service and a lovely dining room that is bound to remind you of the comforts of home.

Watching the sun go down from your room is a special, glowing way to spend time with your partner; it creates a cosy, romantic mood that you’re certain to enjoy, and you’ll want to make the most of it.

2. Sound Of Silence Serengeti

To make your Tanzania honeymoon even more memorable, try this remarkable wildlife and safari resort experience. From here you look out over the endless plains of the Serengeti, a wonderful way to feel close to nature on your honeymoon.

You stay in tents that feel warm and welcoming; they offer the privacy you want while remaining roomy and comfortable.

You’ll probably want to spend extra time watching the animals that wander past right outside your tent, and you’ll discover that the staff are very obliging and happy to adjust things to make your stay even better.

3. Ngorongoro Crater Lodge

Looking for something out of the ordinary during your Tanzania holiday, with the chance to explore different sides of Tanzanian culture? This lodge enjoys a wonderful, one-of-a-kind outlook over the Ngorongoro Crater, a view that can carry your thoughts into pure fantasy.

Inside the rim you’ll be able to see endangered black rhinos, elephants with giant tusks, lions and flamingoes that add colour to the landscape, and you’ll want to put on your walking shoes to take in this wonder.

When you have time to spare, you can call on the local tribes, and there is even the possibility of a banquet on the crater floor, with dining that matches what you would expect from a boutique hotel.

4. Jabali Ridge

Set in the centre of Ruaha National Park, this camp marks a meeting point between the vast landscapes of East and Southern Africa. It occupies a superb spot among giant granite boulders, so you get wonderful views over the plains, and the suites are airy yet private and relaxing.

A wide range of facilities is on hand, including an infinity pool, a spa and plenty of chances to head out into the bush and explore the area. With one-tenth of the world's wild lion population, plus the chance of seeing elephants, giraffes, antelope and over 500 bird species, this is not to be missed.

After a day out exploring, you can relax over local cuisine and perhaps a nice drink while you and your partner look up at the stars, which makes for a truly romantic honeymoon setting.

5. Nimali Mara

This tented camp is a hidden oasis in a remote spot, offering every luxury along with the chance to witness the annual migration river crossing if you book at the right time of year: a fascinating, unforgettable experience if you’re after something different.

Choose this camp and you will discover a calm, nurturing place of solitude, with beautifully designed rooms that sit naturally in their surroundings and a stay that leaves you thoroughly refreshed.

Naturally you want to see wildlife too, so make the most of the game drives to spot the big five, and perhaps even a few lion cubs if you’re fortunate.

What if I want more variety on my Tanzania honeymoon?

We realise these locations won’t suit everyone. Some people would rather visit the various towns and villages, while others prefer a more relaxed honeymoon spot that doesn’t involve much travelling around.

Whichever of these groups you belong to, you’ll find Tanzania full of honeymoon experiences, tours and destinations that you’ll want to go out of your way to arrange or see.

If you’re the sightseeing type, you could head for historic places such as Stone Town. And if you simply want to soak up a beautiful ocean view, you’ll love spots like Nungwi beach. So there’s sure to be something for you.

If you’re after something more energetic, you could try climbing Kilimanjaro via the Lemosho route, a 7-day trek. On the way up you’ll pass through a series of climate zones, including the Arctic desert, a sight you will never forget. Guides, chefs and porters accompany you, leading you across the terrain and making sure you’re comfortable and having fun by sharing insights and trivia along the way. Naturally you’ll need a moderate to good fitness level for this climb, so bear that in mind along with any preparation you have to do.

What Customs Do I Need To Be Aware Of?

We want your honeymoon in Tanzania to be enjoyable but also safe. The first thing to think about is what you wear: away from the tourist resorts, women are discouraged from wearing sleeveless tops and shorts.

It is also wise to avoid being overly affectionate with your partner in public, since local tradition holds that displays of affection belong in the privacy of the couple’s own home.

This may feel restrictive in some ways, but respecting religious and cultural traditions during your honeymoon is a good way to show courtesy and to avoid any awkwardness or problems that could arise if you’re unaware of these customs.

Conclusion

With so many options on offer, you may be wondering which location suits you best. As a rule of thumb, it’s sensible to check what nearby facilities or experiences are available to meet the wishes of your new husband or wife.

Here is your chance to go somewhere quite unlike the honeymoon-focused resorts, which may not give you this much freedom. You also get to learn about other cultures, which can offer valuable insights and broaden your outlook.
