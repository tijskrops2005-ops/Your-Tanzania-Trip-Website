Why Jambiani is the place to be in Zanzibar

Jambiani is an authentic and beautiful village on the southeast coast of Zanzibar, and our favourite place on the island. Curious about the best places to eat, stay, and explore while there? Read on to find out.

With its cultural variety, lovely nature and welcoming people, Zanzibar makes an ideal tropical escape, and Jambiani is where you experience all of it at its best. If beautiful beaches, exciting activities, tasty meals in local restaurants and real experiences appeal to you, you may find this is your place as well.

Fishing village

Jambiani, an old fishing village, has kept much of its authentic character, with charming lodges and boutique hotels, an easy-going atmosphere and friendly residents who are happy to mix with visitors. Most people here earn their living from fishing and seaweed farming.

Choose one of the many good beach restaurants, tuck into seafood or other local specialities, then take a short walk along the sand. Yes, Jambiani really is that lovely! A further plus is that Paje next door is within easy reach, with more beach life, a laid-back feel, kitesurf spots and shops. Paje is busier and more geared to tourists than Jambiani, though, and that is why Jambiani remains our favourite place to stay.

Jambiani beach

Back to the beach! Lined with swaying palms and powdery white sand, the 7-kilometre-long beach has barely any rocks or sea urchins – unlike some beaches in the north. A few kilometres offshore lies the barrier reef, and between it and the beach is a beautiful, clear turquoise lagoon. It is ideal for wading, snorkelling and diving, and gives you excellent chances to see marine life. The steady side-shore winds also make Jambiani a great spot for kitesurfing. Swimming in the Indian Ocean is pleasant at high tide, while low tide is the moment for a lovely walk along the beach.

Restaurants in Jambiani

Trying out the restaurants in Jambiani is half the fun, and plenty of them sit right on the sand. Bamboo Zanzibar offers refined dining built around fresh, locally sourced ingredients – think lobster dinners or classic curries with a modern touch. For more choice, go to Fun Beach Hotel, whose menu covers international favourites such as burgers and pasta as well as Swahili-inspired dishes. If you prefer something relaxed but authentic, the restaurant at Blue Oyster Hotel is known for its fresh seafood and vegetarian options. For a change, Be Zanzibar has a strongly plant-based menu at its Meze restaurant, next to other locally inspired dishes with fresh seafood and seasonal specialities.

Because Jambiani has so many good restaurants, we suggest booking bed and breakfast at your hotel, so you are free to try different places for lunch and dinner.

Food, drinks and live music in Jambiani and Paje

Most hotels have a restaurant that welcomes non-residents for a drink or a good meal. Hotels also regularly put on live music, happy hours, barbecues, parties and more to keep their guests entertained.

Activities in Jambiani

Deep sea fishing
We can organise a deep sea fishing trip for a half or a full day. The Indian Ocean is well known for superb game fishing, with a wide range of species including tuna, marlin, sailfish, barracuda and dorado. Head out into deep water and try to land some impressive big fish, which can easily weigh as much as 60 kg.

Scuba diving
The lovely reefs here are home to some of the finest corals in Africa. You can choose from several dive sites, and the range of marine life is remarkable. We team up with the Nakupenda diving school at The Loop, so you can see turtles, seahorses, octopuses, stingrays, frogfish and Napoleon fish in their natural habitat.

Kitesurfing
The steady side-shore winds make Jambiani a perfect place for windsurfing or kitesurfing. The beaches are calm, and the shallow water has few sea urchins. You can of course leave the kite behind and simply surf the waves on a board.

Activity just outside Jambiani

Visit a local village
Get to know everyday life by calling in at a school, a tailor or a boat builder. You can also watch how villagers turn seaweed into soap or cream, and see the many products they make from coconuts.

Snorkelling at the Blue Lagoon
Begin your day with a 15-minute drive to Dongwe, home of the Blue Lagoon. Thanks to its lovely coral and tropical fish, it ranks among the best places to snorkel in Zanzibar. Visibility is good, and with a depth of 1 to 4 metres it suits snorkelling perfectly. Afterwards, treat yourself to good food and drinks at The Rock, a restaurant built on a rock out in the ocean and a great spot to take in the view. Finish the day at Kae Funk in Michamvi, where you can watch the most beautiful sunset in Zanzibar.
