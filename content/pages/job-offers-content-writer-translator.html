Content writers and translators

As a content writer/ translator, you write about national parks in South Africa, gorilla trekkings in Uganda, that beautiful 5-star hotel in Kenya’s Masai Mara, or that fun biking excursion on Zanzibar. Even when sitting at your desk at home all day, you feel like you are on holiday!

English, Norwegian or Spanish

All our texts are written in English and then translated to other languages. As a native speaker, you will mostly translate English texts to your language. But you are also able to write texts in proper English.

Who are we looking for ?

  • A native speaker (Norwegian, English or Spanish) with excellent writing skills (non-Ai)
  • Able to translate from English.
  • Able to write in English when necessary. 
  • Extensive experience in writing and translating/relevant education.
  • Able to write and translate longer and shorter texts, formal and non-formal, in all sorts of formats.
  • Able to play with language and use a playful tone of voice when needed.
  • Able to deliver a message in short sentences when writing or translating; know how to select the details that matter, without losing yourself in details.
  • The ability to translate from Dutch/English to French, German and/or Spanish (non-Ai) is a plus.
  • Knowledge of SEO is a plus.
  • 20-30 hours per week and available during working hours (EAT).
  • Able to work in a team; not too shy to ask questions, not too proud to receive (or give!) feedback.
  • Able to show initiative, understand things quickly, and learn new skills and systems.
  • Designer skills are a plus.
  • Creativity is a big fat plus!
  • All ages are welcome to apply

 

This is what you get:

  • You work within an informal setting with a great international team.
  • You work in a flexible environment – some weeks are busier than others.
  • You work online – you can do this job from anywhere (as long as you have a decent internet connection), but preferably you live in one of the countries we offer (Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda, South Africa… or even around like Botswana and Namibia).
  • You work as a freelancer.
  • You get a good remuneration.
  • You make people happy.
