The safari runs every month. June to October is dry, with the easiest game viewing; late December to March adds Ndutu and the calving season; April and May are green and quiet. Pick the departure that fits your calendar and we tell you what to expect in that month.

Day 1

Tarangire National Park

Silver

Pick-up at your hotel in Arusha in the morning, then about two hours to Tarangire. The park is elephant country: in the dry season herds of a hundred or more line the Tarangire River, the only water for miles, among giant baobabs. Lions, giraffes and zebras are regular sightings. After the game drive you drive on to your lodge for the night.

Accommodations:

  • Day 1: Tarangire National Park
  • Day 2: Serengeti National Park (central), with Ndutu from late December to March, when the migration herds give birth there
  • Day 3: Serengeti National Park (central)
  • Day 4: Ngorongoro Conservation Area and the crater
  • Day 5: Back to Arusha, end of the safari
Silver

Day 2 - 3

Serengeti National Park (two days)

Silver

Over the Ngorongoro highlands and down into the Serengeti. The central park around Seronera has wildlife all year: lions on the kopjes, leopards in the trees along the river, cheetahs on the plains. The second day starts early, when the light is soft and the cats are still active.

From late December to March the group also visits Ndutu, in the south of the Serengeti, where the migration herds gather to give birth on the short grass plains. Newborn wildebeest and zebras, and the predators that follow them, make for a busy day.

Activities:

Between late December and March, the Ndutu region forms the heart of the Great Migration, with more than 2 million animals arriving to give birth. Ndutu spans the southern Serengeti and the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. Thanks to the short grass, packed with nutrients, it is a safe place for newborn wildebeests and zebras, and at the same time an excellent spot for predators. This seasonal gathering means close-up sightings and nonstop activity on the plains.

Accommodations:

  • Silver Golden Safari Camp
Silver

Day 4

Ngorongoro Conservation Area (incl. Crater)

Silver

An early departure for the Ngorongoro Crater, the floor of a volcano that collapsed around two million years ago. Inside the crater walls live lions, elephants, buffalo, hyenas and a small number of black rhinos. Picnic lunch at the hippo pool, then up to the rim and on to your lodge.

Accommodations:

  • Silver Inoga Lodge
Silver
