With a honeymoon in Tanzania and Zanzibar, you say “I do” to romance and the adventure of your lifetime. Everything is possible: our private honeymoon safaris are tailored to your wishes. Though you may have a lot of questions… here you’ll find the answer to the most frequently asked questions from our travellers about their honeymoon.

We always make sure that your accommodations know you are on a honeymoon, and many times this means you get special treatment. Think of the following (sometimes complimentary) services: a fruit basket upon arrival, a room decorated in honeymoon style, a bottle of wine or champagne upon arrival, or a special cake. But don’t be afraid to tell everyone and anyone, as everyone loves honeymooners and are happy to go the extra mile to make your stay even more perfect.

Of course. We love surprises and would do anything to make it happen! Whether you want to surprise your love with a cake or champagne in a certain lodge, or plan an incredible ride in a hot-air balloon, please inform your travel consultant. They will provide you with more information and prices or (complimentary) possibilities in the parks and lodges on your itinerary.

If you want to make your trip even more romantic, there are some special activities we can arrange for you. Think of a cosy bush dinner for two, a romantic hot-air balloon ride above the savannah or a sunset cruise on the Indian Ocean. Or… renew your vows during a Maasai wedding ceremony… truly a once-in-a-lifetime experience! You’ll find our top activities for honeymooners on this page.

It all depends on your wishes and budget. You can choose from comfortable and budget-friendly lodges (our Silver category), more luxurious hotels with cosy rooms, swimming pools and delicious food (our Gold category) and the most high-end accommodations pampering you with private villas, exquisite dining and romance galore (our Platinum category). If you want to stay at the crème de la crème, our Diamond Luxury resorts are perfect for you. These hotels are the country’s highest standard and make you feel like you are in Seventh Heaven.

A day to remember: Wedding Ceremony with the Maasai

Extend your wedding you had at home or renew your vows in a traditional Maasai wedding ceremony in the middle of the savannah! The women of the tribe dress and adorn the bride with traditional clothing and jewellery and take her into a wedding boma. The warriors take the groom to meet the bride, after which an elder blesses you. The entire tribe welcomes you as a married couple – including singing, dancing and eating goat meat, the last step of a Maasai wedding. Ready to get married (again)?
