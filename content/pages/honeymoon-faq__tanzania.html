A day to cherish: a wedding ceremony with the Maasai

Carry on the wedding you celebrated at home, or renew your vows, with a traditional Maasai wedding ceremony right in the heart of the savannah! The women of the tribe dress the bride in traditional clothing and jewellery and lead her into a wedding boma. The warriors then bring the groom to meet his bride, and an elder gives you his blessing. The whole tribe welcomes you as a married couple, with singing, dancing and a meal of goat meat as the final step of a Maasai wedding. Ready to tie the knot (again)?
