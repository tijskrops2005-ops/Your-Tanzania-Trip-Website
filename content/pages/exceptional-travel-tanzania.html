Round off your exceptional safari with time in Zanzibar

After days full of wildlife, adventure and new discoveries, nothing beats winding down on the white-sand beaches of Zanzibar. This idyllic island just off the Tanzanian coast is the ideal finale to your exceptional safari – with luxury beachfront resorts, private villas and crystal-clear water to explore.

Snorkel over coral reefs, sail into the sunset aboard a traditional dhow or simply lie back under the palms: Zanzibar brings a little tropical magic to your trip. Between the rich culture of Stone Town and the calm of hidden beaches, it is the perfect place to reflect, recharge and enjoy barefoot luxury.
