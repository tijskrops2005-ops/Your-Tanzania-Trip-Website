Travel Insurance

A safari in Tanzania is the kind of journey you remember for the rest of your life. Just don’t forget the most important item for your luggage: travel insurance! Whether you’re watching a leopard slip through the grass or bouncing along a rough track, insurance means that a medical problem, an unplanned change of route or a literal bump in the road won’t ruin your adventure.

A solid travel insurance policy gives you peace of mind, as it covers medical emergencies, cancelled trips and other events you cannot plan for. That safety net matters even more in remote places, where hospitals and other services may be far away. With comprehensive cover in place you can concentrate on the excitement of the safari, confident that you are protected if something unexpected happens.

Zanzibar Mandatory Travel Insurance

Anyone travelling to Zanzibar from 1 October 2024 onwards is required to take out the Zanzibar Mandatory Travel Insurance, whatever insurance they already hold. Every traveller must apply for it before departure. Once you have paid USD 44,- per person, you receive a QR code that you show on arrival.
The basic cover of this compulsory policy includes emergency medical costs, repatriation (medical and of remains), personal accidents, delayed, stolen or lost baggage, legal expenses and personal liability. You can apply for Zanzibar’s Mandatory Travel Insurance at https://visitzanzibar.go.tz/.
