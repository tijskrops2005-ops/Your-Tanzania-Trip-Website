Get Your Heart Racing: The Thrills of Tanzania Adventure Safaris

So, what makes Tanzania adventure safaris stand out from your run-of-the-mill safari? First, you obviously have all the benefits of a standard safari experience.

Witness wildlife in their natural habitat

Tanzania has an almost overwhelming amount of wildlife, and sometimes you don’t know where to look first. The elephant herds gathering at the waterhole, the cheetah outrunning everything and everyone, or the humpback whales performing a very special ballet. All of this makes a safari in Tanzania is an adventure in itself – no matter if on land or at sea.

Experience unique and unusual cultures

One of the great things about going on adventures in Tanzania is that there are so many unusual cultures to experience and learn about. For instance, you can explore the vibrant sights and sounds of the city of Arusha, visit the Kondoa rock paintings or experience the hospitality of the Iraqw.

But, these are things you can do on almost any kind of safari… So, what sets Tanzania adventure tours apart? Keep scrolling and find out.

Interactivity

A Tanzania adventure tour usually requires far more involvement from you than a standard safari tour would. For instance, instead of sitting in the car, you can take a two-day hike from Ngorongoro to Lake Natron or get up close with dolphins or snakes.

Alternative Transportation

Who wants to be stuck in a car all day when there are so many other options to try on your Tanzania adventure? You can zip around in a Tuk-Tuk, go horseback riding in Arusha, or explore the crystal clear oceans in a semi-submarine.

Customisable Adventure

Not everyone’s idea of adventure is the same. For some, adventure is as simple as a yacht cruise. Others need to feel the heart pumping. The trick to the best Tanzania adventure tour is to pick the activities that make you go “wow” and experience them in an order which doesn’t make you lose valuable time.
