Together with Pemba Island and a host of smaller islands, Zanzibar forms the Zanzibar Archipelago. On Zanzibar Island you get relaxed beach living alongside a wide choice of activities, a vibrant culture and a rich history. Join a spice tour, or head out to the stunning coral reefs and swim among tropical reef fish. Everything you need for a holiday to remember is here on Zanzibar Island. Below you'll find our own top three places to stay, plus our favourite Zanzibar island itineraries.

This is how you stay!

Beaches and resorts can be found on every side of Zanzibar island, north, south, east and west. Our own favourite, though, is the east coast, chiefly for its beautiful, tranquil beaches, the wide range of activities and the many restaurants serving all kinds of cuisine, enough to please even the fussiest eater. In the view of our travel experts, this part of the island gives you the best all-round Zanzibar experience. With so many resorts and lodges to choose from, we have narrowed our three personal favourites down to three price categories.
In the Silver category our pick is Fun Beach hotel – 42 rooms with pool or beach view and modern amenities for maximum comfort. In the Gold category we point you to Next Paradise – a boutique resort facing the ocean, with 25 elegantly furnished rooms and excellent food. The rooms are divided into several categories, each with its own special touches.
Finally, in the Platinum category (drumroll….. ) – Qambani Luxury Resort. With flawless service across its six individually themed villas, this resort offers the most beautiful and indulgent stay on Zanzibar. Thanks to the refined, elegant touches in the interiors and artwork, every villa tells a story of its own while still feeling like a home away from home. Qambani is luxury and comfort at their very best!

A rich culture and history

The Zanzibar islands are known as the Spice Islands, a name earned through their long history of spice trading and their many spice plantations. Historic Stone Town is a fascinating blend of cultures, where food lovers and culture enthusiasts can explore spots such as the Sultan's Palace, the House of Wonders and the Darajani and Forodhani food markets. There really is no end to what you can do here!

Excursions and activities Zanzibar

  • Spice tour: walk with a guide between plantations where pepper, cinnamon, ginger, cloves, nutmeg and fruit are grown.
  • Kitesurfing: on the beaches of Paje, Kiwengwa and Nungwi.
  • Mountainbike excursion
  • Stone Town excursion: wander the narrow lanes of this bustling town, taking in a few local markets and the former slave market.
  • Prison Island excursion: a 30 minute boat ride brings you to this island, once used to hold slaves. Today it serves as a nature reserve for giant tortoises, and you can also see the ruins of the old prison.
  • Safari Blue: an excursion to 3 islands with time to swim, snorkel and enjoy a tasty BBQ.
  • Jozani forest visit: a wonderful jungle walk through Zanzibar's last remaining native forest, home to plenty of monkeys, including the rare red colobus.
  • Scuba diving and snorkelling on any of the many superb reefs.
  • Cultural tour: this outing shows you how local people live their daily lives.
  • Mnemba Island tour: a snorkelling trip to Zanzibar's most popular snorkelling site.

Travel time to Zanzibar

The flight from Dar es Salaam to Zanzibar lasts a mere 20 minutes, and pairing Zanzibar with a mainland safari is easy too. Between Arusha, the safari capital, and Zanzibar lies a distance of roughly 420 kilometres.

  • Arusha to Zanzibar: € 285 (one way)
  • Dar Es Salaam to Zanzibar: € 90 (one way)
  • Kilimanjaro to Zanzibar: € 350 (one way)

Best time for kitesurfing in Zanzibar

For anyone keen on great kitesurfing, Zanzibar is an ideal spot. The top kitesurfing period is:

  • December till mid-March
  • June till October
