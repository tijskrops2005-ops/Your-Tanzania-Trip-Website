The Top 10 Lodges in Arusha

2 July 2024

When you’re out and about exploring, you want to come back to a cosy and welcoming lodge in Arusha. And lucky you: there are plenty of wow hotels which will become your home away from home. Here you can enjoy comfort, taste local delicacies, and recharge your batteries before embarking on your next adventure.

We have put together ten of the most scenic accommodations in Arusha, so that you only have to one thing: pick your favourite and start dreaming!

Gran Meliá Arusha

Situated among tea and coffee plantations, Gran Meliá is a modern and beautiful lodge in Arusha. From its terrace and lounge, it offers a clear view of Mount Meru and the city. And should you prefer some privacy, simply sip your favourite drink on your private balcony.

After a strenuous hike or exciting safari, this superb Arusha accommodation pampers you with amenities to help you relax. Kick back in the hotel spa, jump into the heated pool, or simply take a stroll through the stunning gardens with national park vibes.

Legendary Lodge

Legendary Lodge in Arusha is also surrounded by a beautiful coffee plantation, and it’s safe to say that this accommodation is the definition of African authenticity. Far from the buzzing tourist hotspots, it offers a peaceful yet luxurious retreat. Here you can enjoy a mix of privacy, comfort and majestic mountain views.

The lodge is a great place to embark on your safari and to do other activities like canoeing, birdwatching and hiking. At this cosy place you can come close to the wildlife and unite with nature.

The Safari House

The Safari House is a 10-bedroom boutique hotel with contemporary features, an outside pool, spacious rooms, and delicious food. No matter if you want to get on Tanzania time, recover before your next adventure, play golf or ride a horse – here you can do it all in style.

This Arusha lodge is ideal for couples and families as the rooms have private bathrooms and some even a kitchenette. Taking a stroll around the estate will make your eyes sparkle, since African wildlife like zebras and elands lives nearby.

Lake Duluti Lodge

Surrounded by coffee plantations, the charming Lake Duluti Lodge offers an oasis of peace and tranquillity. Enjoy a refreshing drink on the porch, take in the unmatched beauty around you, and gather strength for your next trip.

The 19 spacious rooms have private bathrooms and scenic views all around. The best part of staying here is dining at the lake shore – a perfect opportunity to spend time and cherish every second with your loved ones.

Ahadi Lodge

Ahadi Lodge is situated right in the heart of Arusha, comprises five individual cottages and enchants with lush gardens. After a long flight or a safari in Arusha National Park, the pool is the place to be. Take the plunge and enjoy first-class and authentic African cuisine in the cosy restaurant afterwards.

Siringit Villa

Siringit Villa is anything but hustle and bustle. Away from the noises of a busy city, you can enjoy silence and nature at its best  – and listen to some birds singing. This 6-bedroom villa unites impeccable service with great food and an unparalleled scenery. The perfect place to charge your batteries for the next highlight of your Tanzania holiday.

Arusha Planet Lodge

Another luxurious accommodation is Arusha Planet Lodge. The idyllic resort offers great food, outstanding service, and spacious rooms with comfortable beds. It is conveniently located near the Kilimanjaro Airport, and the palm and banana trees create a secluded oasis many travellers have fallen in love with.

Ngare Sero Mountain Lodge

If you want to get far away from the city, Ngare Sero Mountain Lodge is meant for you. Here you can experience African wildlife up close and treat yourself to food cooked from the lodge’s own garden. From walking in the area to taking a boat in the lagoon or simply marvel at Mount Kilimanjaro – we promise you that you won’t get bored here.

Arusha Villa

Arusha Villa welcomes travellers who seek peace, but don’t want to miss out on memorable experiences. Recharge on a sunbed at the pool, take a stroll in the lush garden, visit villages with a guide and observe Blue Monkeys playing in the trees. The cherry on the cake? Delicious meals, great views of Mount Meru and a good night’s sleep in a comfy bed.

Wildlife Residence

If you love animals and want to stay at an authentic lodge in Arusha, head over to the Wildlife Residence. This accommodation is timeless and wows you with breathtaking views of Mount Meru and Kilimanjaro. The highlight: breakfast by the pool, where wild animals tend to graze. So while you’re sipping your morning coffee, wildebeests, elands, kudus, zebras and Co. are having their breakfast right in front of you.

And that’s a wrap! From cosy and authentic, to contemporary and secluded – find the lodge in Arusha that perfectly matches your Tanzania plans. And if you need a little help, we’re here for you.
