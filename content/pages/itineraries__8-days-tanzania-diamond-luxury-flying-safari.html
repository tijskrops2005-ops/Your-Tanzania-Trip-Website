A question we hear a lot is when Tanzania is best visited. Honestly, there isn’t a single correct answer. It comes down to what you hope to see, where you’d like to travel, your budget and what interests you. You’ll find a detailed guide on the best time to travel to Tanzania here.

 

    • Dry Season (June to October & December to February): These months are ideal for safaris and beach visits due to the pleasant weather and excellent wildlife viewing opportunities. However, expect higher hotel rates and more crowded parks and attractions.

 

    • Mid Season (March & November): This period offers a balance of moderate hotel rates and fewer tourists. The weather can be variable, and some attractions might have limited availability (due to rains), but you still get great wildlife sightings and a more relaxed safari experience.

 

    • Wet Season (April & May): Perfect for budget-conscious travelers, the low season features the lowest hotel rates and the least crowded parks. The heavy rains make the scenery vibrant and lush, but travel plans can be affected by the weather.

Day 2

Discover Mto Wa Mbu in a Tuktuk

Diamond Luxury

Close to Lake Manyara National Park, the village of Mto wa Mbu forms a green oasis amid the dry savannah plains. A tuktuk holds up to 3 people, and touring this way gives you as much flexibility as possible. These vehicles fit through narrow lanes and can pull over wherever you want. The route takes you past the village, then into the forest and by several farms and plantations. Over one or two hours you stop at a brewery, a local artist and a local school.

Activities:

Get to know Tanzanian cooking right in the heart of a banana farm. Choose three local dishes and watch and learn how each one is made. You can learn to prepare pilau (spiced rice), makande (a tasty mix of corn and brown beans) or ndizi nyama (plantains with meat), and get a real taste of Tanzania!

Day 3

Lake Manyara National Park - luxury style!

Diamond Luxury

For a scenic safari, Lake Manyara National Park is ideal, with acacia woodland, jungle forest, mountain cliffs and grassland. Thanks to this biodiversity it is home to the remarkable tree-climbing lions, as well as many other species, including baboons, elephants, buffalos, warthogs, giraffes, wildebeest, zebras and plenty more.

Day 4

Gibb's Farm walk & lunch

Diamond Luxury

On the slopes of the Ngorongoro Highlands, Gibb's Farm keeps to the age-old rhythm of an African farm and coffee plantation. Stroll around this peaceful, tranquil sanctuary and see vegetables being picked, coffee roasted, cows milked and pigs fed. The vegetable gardens, fruit trees and herbs inspire the farm's chefs, who then surprise guests with a delicious lunch. A little slice of heaven!

Day 5

Ngorongoro Crater - luxury style!

Today you head down into the wondrous caldera of the well-known Ngorongoro Crater. This is the day to set off on your game drive early, while the animals are many and the tourists are few! As you descend into the crater you’ll feel the hairs on your arms stand up, struck by the clouds hanging overhead and the many animals around you. Jackals, hyenas, buffaloes, wildebeests, gazelles and plenty of other animals live here, and you are almost certain to see all of them.

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury The Craters Edge Lodge – Wilderness Collection

Day 6

Serengeti National Park luxury style - by plane

Diamond Luxury

You fly into the breathtakingly beautiful Serengeti National Park, the very essence of safari. Here you can see the famous Big Five in all their splendour and, in certain months, thousands of wildebeest racing past during the Great Migration. Even without the migration, this is a paradise for big cats, with lion prides on kopjes, cheetahs in the tall yellow grass and leopards up in the trees. You get there in time for a wonderful afternoon game drive into the sunset.

Accommodations:

  • Diamond Luxury Lemala Nanyukie Tented Lodge
Diamond Luxury

Day 7

Serengeti National Park - luxury style!

Diamond Luxury

Whatever comes to mind when you imagine a luxury safari, you’ll find it in Serengeti National Park. There are the Big Five, the famous Great Migration and lots of big cats, but also rarer animals such as the gerenuk and the wild dog, and then there are the more than 500 bird species. Along the way you’ll naturally see antelopes, giraffes, elephants, zebras, hippos and many other animals. Be sure to get up at sunrise for an epic balloon safari...!

Activities:

Taking a balloon safari over the iconic Serengeti National Park turns your Tanzania safari into an experience to remember. You go up at sunrise and drift softly across the endless Serengeti plains. The hot air balloon glides at treetop level over the baobabs and acacias, so the animals are close by. Looking down on the wildlife from the balloon is both captivating and calm, with stunning panoramic views all around.
