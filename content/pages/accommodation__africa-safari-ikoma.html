Located within the Game Controlled Area of the Tanzanian Wildlife Authority (TAWA), Africa Safari Serengeti Ikoma is a great location to explore the Serengeti ecosystem at only 300 meters from the national park boundary. This spacious tented camp offers comfortable rooms with ensuite bathrooms with toilet and hot shower (solar-powered) and a private patio with tables and chairs from where you can enjoy the views. In the common areas, you can enjoy a buffet or à la carte menu at the restaurant, a drink at the lounge bar or by the campfire, or choose to have a go at the pool table or other games. It’s a lovely place to unwind between your exciting safari trips, and a great spot to continue game viewing, as the lodge has a proverbial backyard full of them, thanks to its proximity to the Grumeti River.
