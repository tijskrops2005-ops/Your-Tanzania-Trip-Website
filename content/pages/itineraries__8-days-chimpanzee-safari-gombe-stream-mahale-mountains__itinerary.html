Day 3 - 4

Gombe Stream National Park

Gold

Have you long wished to see chimps in the wild? Then Gombe Stream National Park is the place to go, as this is where Jane Goodall once carried out her research into the behaviour of chimpanzee groups. The small park sits on Tanzania's western border along the eastern shore of Lake Tanganyika, and you can only get there by boat. Follow in Jane Goodall's footsteps as a guide leads you to groups of chimpanzees climbing trees, playing or caring for their young!

Accommodations:

  • Gold Mbali Mbali Gombe Lodge
Gold

Day 5 - 6

Drive & boat transfer from Kigoma to Mbali Mbali Lodge Mahale

Gold

Enjoy a real adventure as you travel by road and by boat from Kigoma to Mbali Mbali Lodge Mahale. Take in the beautiful scenery on a comfortable, easy journey. Let yourself be absorbed by the wonders of nature and the lovely views along the route. Our carefully planned itinerary looks after every detail of your trip, so everything runs smoothly and the journey stays with you.

Activities:

Even more chimpanzees are waiting for you in Mahale Mountains National Park. A 2.5-hour drive plus a boat trip from Gombe National Park, this park is slightly bigger and has around 900 wild chimpanzees. It differs from most parks in that there are no roads, so you explore on foot. Experienced guides lead you on a walk through the mountains to look for chimpanzee groups. When you find them, you have lots of time to watch these wonderful animals - a magical moment!

Accommodations:

  • Gold Mbali Mbali Mahale lodge
Gold
