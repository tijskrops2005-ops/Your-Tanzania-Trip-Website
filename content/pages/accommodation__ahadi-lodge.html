In the heart of Arusha, in a genuine residential neighbourhood, you find Ahadi Lodge, a small-scale lodge where you see something of daily life in Tanzania while still enjoying calm surroundings. The rooms are fairly small and lie in a lush tropical garden around the swimming pool. Each room has its own colour and theme and comes with double or twin beds, an ensuite bathroom with a shower, a TV and a safe. There is also a small restaurant with good food – and guests love the friendly staff. Ahadi does not accept cash, so you pay for meals and drinks by card.
