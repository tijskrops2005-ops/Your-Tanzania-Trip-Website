November is the month for travelers who love to go a little off the beaten path. While many assume the short rains will spoil a safari, the reality is quite different — showers are usually brief, arriving in the late afternoon or evening, leaving most of the day bright and clear for game drives. The rains also transform the landscapes almost overnight, turning the dusty plains into lush, green backdrops that make wildlife sightings even more dramatic.

Why November is a Great Time for Tanzania

Because November falls in Tanzania’s low season, you’ll find far fewer safari vehicles, more lodge availability, and excellent rates. It feels like the parks belong to you — just your vehicle, your guide, and the wildlife. Add to that the wildebeest herds moving back into the Serengeti from Kenya and the arrival of migratory birds, and you’ve got a safari experience that’s both unique and incredibly rewarding. November is for the traveler who wants space, value, and the thrill of seeing Tanzania in a different light.

Weather in Tanzania in November

November brings Tanzania’s short rainy season, but don’t worry — it’s nothing like the heavy rains of April and May. Expect warm days averaging 25–30°C (77–86°F), with refreshing showers typically arriving in the late afternoon or evening. Most of the day remains clear and perfect for game drives.

The rain has a magical effect: it transforms the dusty landscapes into lush green backdrops almost overnight. Photographers love this season for the dramatic skies and vibrant colors, and travelers often appreciate the cooler evenings and the freshness the rains bring.

Wildlife in November

November is a fascinating month for wildlife viewing.

  • Serengeti Migration: The wildebeest herds begin moving south again from Kenya, returning to the Serengeti in large numbers. This makes for thrilling game drives as predators follow closely behind. It is important to mention that the wildebeest migration has become more and more unpredictable due to climate change.

  • Big Cats: Lions, cheetahs, and leopards are easier to spot against the emerald-green plains. Their activity peaks as herds return.

  • Birdwatching: November is paradise for bird lovers. Migratory birds arrive from Europe and Asia, adding color and sound to Tanzania’s skies.

The combination of returning herds, active predators, and fresh greenery makes November one of the most rewarding times for wildlife enthusiasts.

Benefits of Traveling in November

What are the true benefits of traveling in November?

  • Low Crowds: National parks feel quieter, offering more intimate game drives.

  • Better Prices: Lodges and camps often offer discounted low-season rates.

  • Unique Scenery: The green landscapes create a different, more dramatic safari backdrop.

  • Exclusive Feel: With fewer travelers, your safari feels private and tailor-made.

For adventurous travelers looking for value and authenticity, November is one of the smartest choices.

Zanzibar Holidays in November

Pairing your safari with Zanzibar in November is a great way to round off your trip. Zanzibar is warm and tropical, with highs around 28–30°C (82–86°F). While you may experience occasional showers, they are short and usually followed by brilliant sunshine.

The upside? Resorts are quieter, the beaches feel more private, and you can often find excellent deals. It’s a great time to explore Stone Town’s spice-scented alleys, dive into the clear Indian Ocean, or simply unwind on a nearly empty beach with a cocktail in hand.

FAQs About Traveling to Tanzania in November

Here are our most commonly asked questions about traveling to Tanzania in November:

Will the rains ruin my safari?

No. Showers are short and often in the late afternoon, leaving most of the day dry and perfect for wildlife viewing.

Are prices lower in November?

Yes. Many lodges and safari camps offer excellent discounts, making it one of the most affordable months to travel.

Is November good for photography?

Absolutely. The green landscapes, dramatic skies, and fewer vehicles in the parks make for stunning shots.

Is Zanzibar worth visiting in November?

Definitely! You’ll enjoy warmer seas, quiet beaches, and lower prices compared to peak season.
