Hakuna Matata

No worries, just wonder

Flight delayed or cancelled? Vehicle trouble in the middle of your safari? Our Hakuna Matata Package ensures that nothing gets in the way of your holiday, so you can enjoy a worry-free African travel experience – at no extra cost. Here is a closer look at this exclusive Your Tanzania Trip offer.

What is the Hakuna Matata Package?

Your Tanzania Trip’s Hakuna Matata Package is a complimentary support package that comes with every tailor-made holiday. The services it covers help you before and during your trip: a clear payment schedule, support when something unexpected affects your safari, and assistance with changing your itinerary where needed. Extras such as travel insurance, VIP airport pickup and a local SIM or eSIM can be added for an additional fee.

Before your safari

Your holiday starts long before you step off the plane, and we want you to know exactly what lies ahead.







 

Ahead of your safari, we send you a detailed travel dossier with practical details about your route, accommodation and activities. Airport pickup is arranged too. You pay a deposit when you confirm your trip and the balance before departure; the amounts and dates are on your proposal. Life does not always go to plan, so should you need to cancel or postpone, our clear and fair cancellation and refund policies set out your options. They are listed in our terms and conditions under point 5.3.

Flight delay and cancellation support

If you book your flight with us, it is covered by your Hakuna Matata Package.

Is your flight delayed or cancelled? Then we are there for you.

When you book your international flight through Your Tanzania Trip, flight disruption support becomes part of your Hakuna Matata Package. If your flight is cancelled or significantly delayed, we help arrange a suitable alternative, necessary accommodation, and adjustments to your itinerary. We also contact the airline on your behalf and pass on any refunds.

What help can you count on during your Tanzania safari?

While you are out discovering the wonders of Tanzania, you may wonder, “What could possibly go wrong?” With us, nothing. And if something does, we handle it.







 

On safari you ride in a well-maintained 4×4, backed by local bush garages and replacement vehicles should they be needed. Your professionally trained private guide accompanies you for the whole journey, and our team can be reached 24/7 if something unexpected comes up.
