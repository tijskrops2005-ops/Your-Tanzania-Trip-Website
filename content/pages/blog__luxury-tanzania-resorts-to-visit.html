If you’re going to Tanzania for the majestic safaris or just for some sightseeing, you want those picturesque views and accommodations that suit the ambition you set out for the adventures ahead.

But what are the best luxury resorts in Tanzania that can accommodate your needs? How can you get the most out of the amenities that ensure you take in all the splendors of these lodges and the wider country?

In this article, we’ll show you 10 Tanzania luxury resorts that offer something different in case you’re looking for that once-in-a-lifetime opportunity. Read on to make your vacation that much more special.

10 Best Resorts To Visit On Your Trip

1. Four Seasons Serengeti

The amazing thing about this villa is that it gives you a choice of what guest rooms and suites you like. This means you can observe elephants at the watering holes or an expanse of the savannah – both truly magnificent sights.

You’ve also got the option to visit the local Maasai people, or if you feel like kicking back after a long day, you can relax by the expansive pools and spas that are on offer, so you have the best of comfort and adventure.

You also have in-room dining, housekeeping, a fitness center, and a retail shop. As such, you have ways of making your trip more personal to you. Four Seasons Serengeti would make a perfect honeymoon spot for those who like luxury and convenience with a hint of adventure.

2. Manyara Best View Lodge

This luxury resort in Tanzania overlooks Lake Manyara, where you’ll be able to see all sorts of wildlife, from flamingoes to gazelles. What makes this hotel truly stand out is the fact that it was designed with a lot of purity while respecting the Tanzanian culture and local customs.

You can also expect to find spacious rooms with a lovely infinity pool and amenities that are very homely, with a restaurant that has fresh ingredients from the surrounding villages.

Whatever your priorities, staying at the lodge with service-focused staff is always a great idea if you want to unwind in the evening and have an unforgettable stay.

3. Nimali Tarangire

Situated in the national park of Tarangire, NimaliTarangire is a lodge with an exclusive and intimate feel to it. Tucked away amongst the baobab trees, you have a full view of the surrounding wildlife and flora.

Take in all the great amenities available, including a sun terrace, a pool, and even fire-side dining.

This location is ideal for a family vacation, as you will find spacious tents and recreational spaces. When it comes to luxury resorts in Tanzania, this one will for sure allow you to create memories that you’re going to remember for a lifetime.

4. andBeyond Ngorongoro Crater Lodge

If you want your accommodation to be truly luxurious and high-end, you’ll be pressed to find a better alternative than this lodge. andBeyond Ngorongoro Crater Lodge is located on the rim of Ngorongoro’s ancient caldera, where you can overlook some of Africa’s most famous landscapes.

You’ll find spacious suites that are bathed in natural luxury with the comforts of an in-room massage, a wine store, and a signature shop to find some truly unique keepsakes.

For families, this lodge has welcoming suites, so you can get them excited about wildlife and are unlikely to be too bored with a Wi-Fi network that allows some flexibility. It’s definitely a location to consider when planning some time off with your loved ones.

5. Legendary Lodge

This lodge is located on the outskirts of the vibrant town of Arusha and gives you access to the beautiful northern safari circuit of Tanzania, and has cottages that offer you a tranquil and comfortable experience.

Being close to Arusha gives you a chance to travel to the town where you can see the local markets and sights like the Materuni waterfalls. Nearby, you’ll also find an authentic coffee plantation, so go ahead and try some new and bold flavors as you have a cup.

Back at the lodge, you have tropical gardens, large and comfortable beds, a well-being center, living areas, a library, air conditioning, a laundry service, and a mini-bar, so your stay can be more than a simple stop-off. If you want to relax, unwind, and experience authentic Tanzania, this luxury resort will be a great option for you to consider.

6. Chem Chem Lodge

Chem Chem Lodge is the ultimate getaway, as you will find this luxury Tanzania resort in the corridor that runs between Lake Manyara National Park and Tarangire National Park. This means you’ll see animals passing through the area all year round.

The surrounding bush gives you both privacy and immersion, with a relaxation space that allows you to see the views just as well. What’s more, you can experience the best in fine dining than you would in other Tanzania luxury resorts.

You’ll find the rustic elements of this lodge have a relaxing feel about them, with walkways that can take you all around the property. You won’t feel restricted by designated areas, and we’re sure that you can really make yourself at home.

7. Singita Faru Faru Lodge

This lodge is set in front of a busy watering hole alongside the Grumeti River in the heart of the Serengeti, which has open areas that allow natural light and breathtaking views to be seen wherever you are.

You can try out local wines, try out their fitness centers, and choose a room with a river view or a modern terrace that are designed down to the last detail.

With easy seating and housekeeping, you never have to compromise any views that can appear right in front of you. Consider booking your stay at Singita Faru Faru Lodge for a truly exclusive experience!

8. Singita Sasakwa

This lodge has a more classy and stately look to it that offers a light and airy atmosphere with panoramic views, with a veranda that overlooks the vast expanse of the Singita Grumeti reserve in the Serengeti.

Singita Sasakwa is a great spot to see some of the stages of the annual migration. If you want to see this spectacular event with your own eyes, go there between June and September – it’s the best moment to see the herds on the move.

If you’re looking to relax, you’ll find treatments that are tailor-made and can immerse you in the surroundings, and afterward, you’ll feel revitalized and ready to go on an adventure. Singita Sasakwa is a perfect choice if ou want to have a truly outback experience.

9. Tarangire Treetops by Elewana

If you want to experience utmost luxury and privacy, move into one of the spacious treehouses of the Tarangire Treetops lodge.

After a day of adventure spotting lions, elephants and the crème de la crème of African wildlife, simply relax at the lodges’s pool or get a massage in the spa. Of course, you can also enjoy some us-time on your private balcony, taking in the view of the unique nature and baobab trees around you.

Or you keep your safari going by marvelling at the park’s animals – from the comfort of your very own tree house.

10. Mwiba Lodge

When we say that this Tanzania luxury resort offers you many ways to live out the experience of your dreams, we truly mean it! Mwiba Lodge is located by a rocky gorge on the Arugusinyai river, which is in a huge expanse of a wildlife reserve that adjoins the southern Serengeti.

Amenities include a spa and gym, boutique, library, fly-camping, and so much more that you can plan for ahead of time that can keep you occupied. Additionally, you can have private dining areas if you just want to reflect on the adventures you’ve had that day.

This dining is provided by chefs who can create wonderful meals and you can learn more about the area through tours that make your experience as best as it can be and are really insightful into the local wildlife that inhabits these areas.

Some Tips For Your Stay

Now we’ve outlined some wonderful luxury resorts in Tanzania, your search for a place to stay can depend on many factors, like the group you are going with, whether you want to go out exploring, or if you want to take it easy by the pool.

Whatever you decide, there are things that you can do to ensure that your trip is both enjoyable and safe, and in environments like the Serengeti, there are things that could go as unexpected if not considered.

Below are a few tips for you to have the best experience possible.

Be Patient When On Game Drives

With this type of experience, there are many instances that are unpredictable, so in some of your drives, you might see a lot or little game activity.

As rewarding as a drive can be, some of these rides can be testing, with the heat and cold conditions going from mild to severe and uneven paths you cross.

This is especially true if you’re following a herd and they go off course, so you can follow advice and make it more comfortable for you.

Follow Safety Guidance

This one seems straightforward, but there is always the temptation to get a closer look, even if it puts you and others at risk. Some animals are masters of camouflage, so you won’t see them until you get really close to them.

Keep any equipment you have inside the vehicle and try not to approach animals too closely or quickly, as, in this setting, they can be pretty unpredictable and can be quite aggressive if provoked.

As part of your itinerary, you’ll find some guidance that may apply to the area you’re in. They are also designed to keep you safe in many different scenarios, so keep to them and ask a guide if there are any queries.

Tailor Your Safari Experience To Your Needs

When in Tanzania, you can make your own tweaks to your experience to get the most out of your trip. In certain national parks, you may have options such as customizing your drive games, walking safaris, or even enjoying a balloon ride.

Each of these tours can give you a more relaxing or active time during your trip to Tanzania.

You can decide that you want to try out a game drive, especially if you have some scope for where you can venture and what you can see. Still, be mindful that you might be restricted in some out-of-bound areas.

You could also request to set up a picnic in the middle of the savannah, which the whole family can appreciate and allows you to get a few pictures taken as well, so you can create some unforgettable memories.

This is very much going to depend on your interests and how much activity you want on your safari, so when booking your safari, see what options are available to you.

Conclusion

So now that you know the best of what a safari experience can bring you, the only thing you need to do is decide on which magnificent location you’re going to stay at. You might want to see a particular animal group, have a more active trip or relax – it’s up to you, and the Tanzania luxury resorts we mentioned will surely meet your needs.

You should also note that the time of year you plan to go on a safari will determine how much wildlife you see. As a rule of thumb, it’s best to travel sometime between June and October, as in this time, you can see some of the most iconic migrations.
