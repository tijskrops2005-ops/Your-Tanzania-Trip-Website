Whether you travel to Tanzania for its majestic safaris or simply to see the sights, you will want scenic views and places to stay that match the ambitions you have for the adventures to come.

So which luxury resorts in Tanzania are best placed to meet your needs? And how do you make the most of the facilities, so that you enjoy everything these lodges and the wider country have to offer?

Below we present 10 luxury resorts in Tanzania that each offer something different, in case you are after that once-in-a-lifetime experience. Keep reading to make your holiday that little bit more special.

10 Best Resorts To Visit On Your Trip

1. Four Seasons Serengeti

What makes this villa special is that you get to choose the guest rooms and suites you prefer. That way you can watch elephants at the watering holes or look out over the wide savannah – both of them truly magnificent sights.

You can also choose to visit the local Maasai people, or, when you would rather unwind after a long day, relax at the large pools and spas available, so you get the best of both comfort and adventure.

In-room dining, housekeeping, a fitness centre and a retail shop are also available, giving you ways to tailor your trip to yourself. For couples who enjoy luxury and convenience with a touch of adventure, Four Seasons Serengeti would be an ideal honeymoon spot.

2. Manyara Best View Lodge

Overlooking Lake Manyara, this luxury resort in Tanzania lets you spot all kinds of wildlife, from flamingoes to gazelles. What really sets the hotel apart is its pure design, which respects Tanzanian culture and local customs.

You can also look forward to spacious rooms, a lovely infinity pool and very homely facilities, plus a restaurant that uses fresh ingredients from the nearby villages.

Whatever matters most to you, a stay at this lodge with its service-minded staff is always a good choice if you want to relax in the evenings and enjoy a memorable stay.

3. Nimali Tarangire

NimaliTarangire lies within Tarangire national park and is a lodge with an exclusive, intimate atmosphere. Hidden among the baobab trees, it gives you a full view of the wildlife and plants around you.

Enjoy the excellent facilities on offer, such as a sun terrace, a pool and even dining by the fire.

With its spacious tents and recreational areas, this spot is perfect for a family holiday. Among the luxury resorts in Tanzania, this one is sure to help you make memories that will stay with you for a lifetime.

4. andBeyond Ngorongoro Crater Lodge

If you are looking for truly luxurious, high-end accommodation, you will struggle to find anything better than this lodge. andBeyond Ngorongoro Crater Lodge sits on the rim of Ngorongoro’s ancient caldera, with views over some of Africa’s most famous landscapes.

The spacious suites are steeped in natural luxury and come with comforts such as in-room massage, a wine store and a signature shop where you can find genuinely unique keepsakes.

Families will find welcoming suites at this lodge, so you can get the children excited about wildlife, and with a Wi-Fi network that offers some flexibility they are unlikely to get bored. It’s certainly a place to keep in mind when you plan a break with your loved ones.

5. Legendary Lodge

On the edge of the lively town of Arusha, this lodge gives you access to Tanzania’s beautiful northern safari circuit, and its cottages provide a calm and comfortable stay.

Because it is close to Arusha, you can head into town to explore the local markets and sights such as the Materuni waterfalls. There is also a genuine coffee plantation nearby, so why not try some new, bold flavours over a cup.

At the lodge itself you will find tropical gardens, large comfortable beds, a well-being centre, living areas, a library, air conditioning, a laundry service and a mini-bar, which makes your stay more than just a stopover. If you want to rest, unwind and get a feel for authentic Tanzania, this luxury resort is well worth considering.

6. Chem Chem Lodge

Chem Chem Lodge makes for the ultimate escape: this luxury Tanzania resort lies in the corridor between Lake Manyara National Park and Tarangire National Park, so animals pass through the area all year round.

The bush around the lodge offers both privacy and a sense of immersion, and the relaxation area gives you just as good a view. On top of that, the fine dining here is better than you would find at other Tanzania luxury resorts.

The rustic touches of the lodge create a relaxing atmosphere, and walkways lead you all around the grounds. Designated areas won’t make you feel hemmed in, and we’re confident you will truly feel at home.

7. Singita Faru Faru Lodge

In the heart of the Serengeti, this lodge faces a busy watering hole beside the Grumeti River, and its open spaces let in natural light and offer breathtaking views wherever you are.

You can sample local wines, make use of the fitness centres, and pick a room with a view of the river or a modern terrace, each designed down to the smallest detail.

Comfortable seating and housekeeping mean you never have to miss any of the views that may unfold right in front of you. For a truly exclusive experience, think about booking your stay at Singita Faru Faru Lodge!

8. Singita Sasakwa

With a more elegant, stately look, this lodge has a light and airy feel and panoramic views, including a veranda that looks out over the vast Singita Grumeti reserve in the Serengeti.

Singita Sasakwa is a good place to witness some of the stages of the annual migration. To see this spectacular event for yourself, go between June and September – it’s the best time to watch the herds on the move.

When you want to relax, there are tailor-made treatments that immerse you in your surroundings, leaving you refreshed and ready for the next adventure. If you are after a true outback experience, Singita Sasakwa is an ideal choice.

9. Tarangire Treetops by Elewana

For the ultimate in luxury and privacy, settle into one of the spacious treehouses at the Tarangire Treetops lodge.

After an adventurous day looking for lions, elephants and the very best of African wildlife, you can simply unwind by the lodge’s pool or book a massage in the spa. You can of course also spend some time together on your private balcony, enjoying the view of the unique nature and the baobab trees all around you.

Or carry on with your safari by watching the park’s animals – without leaving the comfort of your own tree house.

10. Mwiba Lodge

We really mean it when we say this Tanzania luxury resort gives you plenty of ways to live the experience you have dreamt of! Mwiba Lodge sits beside a rocky gorge on the Arugusinyai river, within a vast wildlife reserve bordering the southern Serengeti.

Facilities include a spa and gym, a boutique, a library, fly-camping and much more that you can arrange in advance to keep you busy. You can also dine in private areas if you simply want to look back on the day’s adventures.

The meals are prepared by chefs who create wonderful dishes, and tours help you learn more about the area, making your experience as good as it can be and giving you real insight into the wildlife that lives here.

Some Tips For Your Stay

With these wonderful luxury resorts in Tanzania in mind, where you choose to stay can depend on many things, such as who you are travelling with, whether you want to go out and explore, or whether you prefer to take it easy by the pool.

Whatever you choose, there are steps you can take to make sure your trip is both enjoyable and safe, because in places like the Serengeti some things may not go as expected if you have not thought about them.

Here are a few tips to help you get the most out of your experience.

Be Patient When On Game Drives

A trip like this involves a lot that cannot be predicted, so on some drives you may see plenty of game activity and on others very little.

However rewarding a drive can be, some of them are demanding, with heat and cold ranging from mild to severe and uneven tracks to cross.

That is especially the case when you are following a herd that changes direction, so take advice on board to keep things more comfortable for you.

Follow Safety Guidance

This may sound obvious, but it is always tempting to get a closer look, even when that puts you and others in danger. Some animals are experts at camouflage, so you will not notice them until you are very close.

Keep all your equipment inside the vehicle and avoid approaching animals too closely or too quickly, because in this environment they can be quite unpredictable and may become aggressive if provoked.

Your itinerary will include guidance that may apply to the area you are visiting. It is there to keep you safe in a wide range of situations, so follow it and ask a guide if you have any questions.

Shape your safari experience around your needs

In Tanzania you can adjust your experience to make the most of your trip. Some national parks let you choose options such as customising your drive games, going on walking safaris or even taking a balloon ride.

Each of these tours can make your time in Tanzania either more relaxed or more active.

You might decide to try a game drive, particularly if there is some freedom in where you can go and what you can see. Do keep in mind, though, that some out-of-bound areas may be off limits.

Another option is to ask for a picnic to be set up in the middle of the savannah, something the whole family can enjoy, and a chance to take a few photos and make some unforgettable memories.

This will very much depend on what interests you and how active you want your safari to be, so check which options are available when you book.

Conclusion

Now that you know the best a safari experience can offer, all that is left is to choose which magnificent place you will stay at. Perhaps you want to see a certain group of animals, have a more active trip or simply relax – it’s your choice, and the Tanzania luxury resorts we have listed will certainly meet your needs.

Bear in mind too that the time of year you go on safari affects how much wildlife you see. In general, it’s best to travel at some point between June and October, as that is when you can witness some of the most iconic migrations.
