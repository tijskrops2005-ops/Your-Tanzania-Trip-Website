In the south of Tanzania lies Nyerere National Park, a vast and lovely park with a river, jungle and plenty of animals, including lions, leopards, hippos, crocodiles, Maasai giraffes and even some black rhinos and African wild dogs. You fly from Dar Es Salaam to this remarkable park, where safaris are possible by car, by boat or on foot. Make sure you see the Rufiji River, which runs through the whole reserve, and the enormous Stiegler Gorge!

Day 3

Full day boat safari in Nyerere National Park

Silver
Gold
Platinum

Going on a boat safari on the Rufiji River in Nyerere National Park is relaxing and adventurous at the same time! You glide along the river and on to lakes nearby, with good chances of seeing the many animals that gather at the water’s edge. Giraffes, impalas, waterbucks, kudus and warthogs are seen often. Now and then elephants or crocodiles are bathing too, and the calls of the many bird species are always in the air. A picnic lunch on the shore of one of the lakes is part of the trip.

This morning a small plane takes you to Ruaha National Park. As the flight is short, you can go on your first game drive as soon as you arrive! Ruaha National Park

Ruaha National Park ranks as the third largest national park in Tanzania. Set along the Great Ruaha River, it is famous for its many elephants and, with more than 570 species, it is a real haven for birdwatchers. You may also see lions, leopards, giraffes, zebras, crocodiles, elands, sable antelopes, jackals or the rare African wild dog. Game drives feel special here, as you travel through an unspoilt ecosystem in a fairly quiet yet beautiful park!
