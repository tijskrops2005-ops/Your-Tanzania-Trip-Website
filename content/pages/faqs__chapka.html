Why Choose Chapka?

Are you planning a safari with Your Tanzania Trip? Together with Chapka we offer travel insurance designed for adventurous trips throughout Africa. Whether your journey takes you to Tanzania or Zanzibar, Chapka covers you for unexpected medical costs, trip cancellations, lost baggage and more.

Here you’ll find answers to the questions people ask most often about Chapka Travel Insurance.

Want to insure your safari? Request your Chapka quote here.

Chapka provides travel insurance designed for people travelling internationally. Its policies cover unforeseen medical costs, trip cancellations, lost luggage and other unexpected travel problems, so you can enjoy your safari with more peace of mind.

We at Your Tanzania Trip recommend Chapka for its broad cover for adventurous travel, safaris included, together with round-the-clock emergency assistance.

For safari holidays the Cap Explorer policy is a good match, covering key situations such as medical evacuation, trip cancellation and lost or delayed baggage.

For most safari destinations in Africa, travel insurance is not compulsory. Even so, it’s strongly advised and some lodges or tour operators may ask for it. Medical facilities in remote safari areas tend to be limited, so an emergency medical evacuation can quickly cost tens of thousands of euros. If you lack suitable insurance, you would have to pay these costs yourself.

This is why we consider comprehensive travel insurance an essential part of any safari holiday.

Under the Cap Explorer policy, trips lasting up to 60 consecutive days are covered. Cancellation cover begins the moment you take out the policy and lasts until you depart. Every other type of cover, including medical expenses, assistance, baggage and personal liability, runs from the first day of your trip until the return date shown in your travel booking.

If you drive to the airport, assistance cover also begins up to 48 hours before you depart.

Medical Coverage & Expenses

On safari you often travel deep into Africa’s remote wilderness, a long way from major medical facilities. Suitable travel insurance lets you travel with more confidence, because you know you’ll get help if something unexpected happens on your trip.

If you fall seriously ill or are badly injured during your trip, Chapka covers medical and surgical expenses. This covers:

  • Appointments with doctors and medical specialists;
  • Prescribed medication;
  • Hospitalization and nursing care;
  • Urgent dental treatment up to 300 EUR (approximately 345 USD);
  • Any further treatment that is medically necessary for your condition.

Should you fall ill or get injured on safari, Chapka helps pay for necessary medical treatment, hospital care, prescribed medicines and other eligible healthcare costs. On trips to Africa, medical expenses are covered up to 200,000 EUR (approximately 230,000 USD per person). Each claim carries a 30 EUR (approximately 35 USD) excess.

Yes. If you need hospital treatment after a serious illness or injury on safari, it is covered, within the medical expenses limits described above.

If you’re taken into hospital, you need to call the Chapka assistance team within 24 hours of being admitted. Should you have to stay in hospital for more than three days in a row, Chapka can also arrange for a close relative or friend to travel out to you.

As a rule, pre-existing medical conditions are not covered. That said, if an existing condition suddenly causes an unexpected and unforeseen acute complication on your trip, the medical costs and assistance that follow may still fall under the policy.

For cancellation cover, any illness or injury diagnosed, treated or requiring hospital admission between the day you booked your trip and the day you bought your policy is excluded. If you’re not sure how a particular medical condition might affect your cover, we suggest checking with Chapka before you take out the policy.

Cancellation Coverage

Because safaris are usually booked months ahead, it’s sensible to protect what you spend on your trip right from the beginning. Cancellation cover helps limit the financial consequences of unforeseen events before departure and offers useful protection if your plans change without warning.

Should you need to cancel your safari for a covered reason, Chapka can refund your cancellation costs up to 10,000 EUR (approximately 11,500 USD) per person and 40,000 EUR (approximately 46,000 USD) per insured event. Let your travel provider know as soon as the event that stops you travelling happens, and inform Chapka within five business days of that.

The refund is calculated on the cancellation charges in force on the day the covered event happens. That’s why you should cancel your trip as quickly as possible instead of waiting.

Cover extends to 37 different cancellation scenarios. Among the reasons that matter most to safari travelers are:

  • Serious illness or injury to you, the person you travel with or a member of your immediate family;
  • The death of a close relative or family member;
  • Redundancy, as long as the process had not begun before you bought the policy;
  • Unforeseen pregnancy complications or a miscarriage;
  • Refusal of a tourist visa, as long as you applied in time and have never been refused before;
  • Essential travel documents being stolen in the 48 hours before you leave;
  • Serious damage to your home that requires you to be there;
  • The airline cancelling your outbound flight with no suitable alternative offered within 24 hours;
  • A terrorist attack or a natural disaster at your destination in the 30 days before you leave;
  • A government travel ban or a WHO travel restriction brought in after you made your booking;
  • Mandatory quarantine before departure.

Every insured person pays an excess. Depending on why you cancel, it ranges from 50 EUR (approximately 58 USD) to 20% of the total amount claimed.

The examples listed above are the cancellation reasons that matter most to safari travelers. For the full list of covered events, exclusions and excesses, please see the policy wording.

When your scheduled flight is delayed by more than three hours (or more than six hours for a charter flight), Chapka can refund reasonable extra costs such as meals, accommodation and local transport, up to 152 EUR (approximately 175 USD) per person.

And if an event outside your control makes you miss your flight, the policy can also pay for a replacement ticket to the same destination.

Safari Activities

Whether you go out on game drives, take part in guided bush walks, or try other safari experiences, it’s worth knowing what your travel insurance covers. Below you’ll find more on the activities included and any conditions attached.

The Chapka Cap Explorer policy covers most standard safari activities.

For more specialised activities, such as walking safaris, domestic bush flights and hot air balloon safaris, we suggest confirming your cover with Chapka directly before you go, because the standard policy wording does not name these activities specifically.

The sports and leisure third-party liability section of the policy covers most recreational sports and adventure activities, with cover of up to 150,000 EUR (approximately 173,000 USD) per insured event.

Hunting, professional sports and competitive training are not covered. If you plan to take part in specialised or riskier activities beyond a standard safari itinerary, we advise reading the policy conditions before you travel.

Baggage Protection

Go on safari with confidence, knowing that your personal belongings are covered. Learn what cover applies if your luggage is delayed, lost, damaged or stolen while you’re travelling.

Yes. Chapka covers loss, theft and accidental damage to your personal belongings up to 2,000 EUR (approximately 2,300 USD) per person, per trip. That includes valuable items people often bring on safari, such as cameras, binoculars, laptops, photography equipment and mobile phones. Each claim carries a 30 EUR (approximately 35 USD) excess.

Bear in mind that theft from a vehicle is covered only if the vehicle was locked and properly secured and the theft happened between 7 am and 9 pm local time.

Should the airline lose your baggage or should it be stolen on your trip, take the following steps:

  • Report what happened straight away to the relevant authority: the airline or carrier when your baggage is lost, or the local police within 24 hours when it has been stolen. For theft claims, only items named in the official police report can be taken into account;
  • When your luggage arrives more than 24 hours late, Chapka refunds the cost of essential replacement items such as clothing and toiletries, up to 152 EUR (approximately 175 USD) per person;
  • File your claim online at chapkadirect.com/sinistre within five business days of getting home, and be sure to include all the required documents and original receipts.

Yes. The policy treats cameras, photography and recording equipment, laptops, mobile phones and other electronic devices as valuables. They fall within the overall baggage limit of 2,000 EUR (approximately 2,300 USD), of which no more than 50% (1,000 EUR, approximately 1,150 USD) can go towards valuables.

Jewellery, watches and precious stones count as ‘precious objects.’ They’re covered against theft only while you’re wearing them or when they were kept in your room safe or the hotel’s safe, backed up by a receipt.

The baggage section of the policy does not cover passports, credit cards, cash or travel tickets.

Claims Process

Should something unexpected happen on your trip, claiming is simple once you know which steps to take. Learn how the claims process works, what documents you’ll need and the deadlines for submitting a claim.

In a medical emergency or any situation where you need assistance, call the emergency assistance team first on +33 1 70 79 07 69. All other claims, including trip cancellation, baggage, flight delays and personal liability, should be filed online at chapkadirect.com/sinistre.

You’ll need to:

  • Sign in using your policy details;
  • Explain what happened and what you’re claiming for;
  • Upload every required supporting document and the original receipts.

The claims department can also be reached by phone on +44 20 3808 7722.

Which documents you need depends on the kind of claim, but in most cases you should supply:

  • Your policy number and insurance certificate;
  • Original receipts for every expense you’re claiming;
  • For medical claims: prescriptions, medical certificates stating the diagnosis, the date and nature of the illness or injury, and hospital admission records, if relevant;
  • For cancellation claims: a medical certificate from your doctor (when the claim relates to illness), along with proof of the cancellation charges from your travel provider;
  • For stolen baggage: a police report filed within 24 hours of the incident;
  • For flight delays or cancellations: written confirmation from the airline or carrier stating why the disruption happened and how long the delay was.

Report your claim as soon as you can after the incident. How long you have depends on the type of claim:

  • Cancellation claims: let Chapka know within five business days of telling your travel provider about the cancellation;
  • Baggage claims: file your claim within five business days of getting home;
  • Medical assistance: call the assistance team straight away, or as soon as you can if an emergency stopped you calling first.

Filing your claim within the deadlines and supplying every supporting document helps prevent needless delays in handling it.

AXA Partners aims to acknowledge your claim within ten working days and to give a final answer within two months at most. More complicated claims may take longer to assess.

Sending all the required documents at the beginning can speed up your claim. Missing details or incomplete paperwork are among the most frequent causes of delay.
