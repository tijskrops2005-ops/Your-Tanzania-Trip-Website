The ultimate wildlife adventure: finding the Big Five

One of the most exciting parts of an adventure trip in Tanzania is searching for the Big Five: lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and rhino. Don't expect to sit back and simply watch, though. It is a thrilling journey across some of the best-known landscapes in Africa, where any movement in the grass might turn into an encounter you will never forget.

Perhaps you are rattling across the Serengeti in a 4×4 with an open roof, walking with a ranger in Ngorongoro or searching the horizon at dawn in Ruaha: the search itself is part of the magic. Wildlife viewing does not get more raw, immersive or exciting than this, and every moment belongs to the adventure.

Extend Your Adventure in Zanzibar

Once your exciting safari or tough trek is over, an adventure of another sort is the ideal way to unwind: the island paradise of Zanzibar. With everything from snorkelling and diving in turquoise water to kitesurfing along the east coast, Zanzibar gives active travellers plenty of choice, as well as excellent opportunities to relax.

Wander through the spice markets of Stone Town, sail into the sunset aboard a traditional dhow or just enjoy the sun on soft, powdery beaches once your safari is done. It combines tropical discovery with barefoot luxury perfectly. A fitting reward after your adventure trip.
