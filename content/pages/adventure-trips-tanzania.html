The Ultimate Wildlife Adventure: Spot the Big Five

Tracking the Big Five: lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and rhino is one of the most thrilling aspects of an adventure trip in Tanzania. But this isn’t a passive game-viewing experience. It’s an exhilarating journey through some of Africa’s most iconic landscapes, where every rustle in the grass could be your next unforgettable encounter.

Whether you’re bouncing through the Serengeti in an open-roof 4×4, exploring on foot with a ranger in Ngorongoro, or scanning the horizon at sunrise in Ruaha, the chase is part of the magic. This is wildlife viewing at its most raw, immersive, and exciting. And it’s all part of the adventure.

Extend Your Adventure in Zanzibar

After a thrilling safari or a challenging trek, the perfect way to wind down is with an adventure of a different kind: the island paradise of Zanzibar. From snorkeling and diving in turquoise waters to kite surfing off the eastern coast, Zanzibar offers endless options for active travelers — along with world-class relaxation.

Explore Stone Town’s spice markets, sail into the sunset on a traditional dhow, or simply soak up the sun on powdery beaches after your safari. It’s the ideal blend of tropical discovery and barefoot luxury. And the perfect reward after your adventure trip.
