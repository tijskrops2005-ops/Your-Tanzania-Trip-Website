Day 2 - 3

Tarangire National Park

Platinum

Large groups of elephants and a great many giant baobab trees are what Tarangire National Park is known for. The baobabs can live for hundreds of years, and in the dry season elephants actually use them as a water source, drawing water from them with a special technique. In the dry season the Tarangire River is also the only source of water, so at that time of year you can see groups of up to 250 elephants in Tarangire, along with many other animals!

Activities:

While you stay at Tarangire Safari Lodge, you can enjoy the excitement of a night game drive in Tarangire National Park. When dusk falls, you head out into the wild to see the lively nightlife of the African savanna. Led by expert guides, you discover special nocturnal animals and the captivating sounds of nature. This memorable outing shows you Tarangire’s varied ecosystems in a different light. Take the chance to explore Tanzania under the stars and book your night game drive now!

Day 4

Lake Manyara National Park

Platinum

Between Tarangire National Park and the Ngorongoro Crater, right beside the 400 meters deep Great Rift Valley, you find Lake Manyara National Park. Its range of landscapes draws many different animals. You can, for instance, visit the lake that takes up 75 percent of the park, travel through dense rainforest full of hundreds of monkeys and birds, and get close to elephants in the woodlands.

Activities:

Near Lake Manyara National Park, the village of Mto wa Mbu forms a green oasis between the dry savannah plains. A tuktuk seats up to 3 people, and touring by tuktuk gives you as much flexibility as possible. They fit through narrow streets and can stop wherever you want. The route takes you past the village, then into the forest and along several farms and plantations. Over one or two hours you stop at a brewery, a local artist and a local school.

Day 5

Visit Lake Eyasi and the Hadzabe tribe

Platinum

A day trip to Lake Eyasi and the Hadzabe tribe who live there is a fascinating experience. South of the Ngorongoro Highlands, Lake Eyasi is a shallow, seasonal salt lake and a paradise for bird lovers. Look for hippos in the shallow water, then pick up your binoculars to find species such as the African spoonbill, flamingos, gray headed gulls, great white pelicans and pied avocets. After that you meet the Hadzabe bushmen, masters of bow hunting.

  • Platinum Ngorongoro O'ldeani Mountain Lodge by Wellworth

Day 6 - 7

Ngorongoro Conservation Area (incl. Crater)

Platinum

The well-known Ngorongoro Crater is counted among Africa's seven natural wonders. It was created two million years ago when a huge volcano erupted. The fertile lava produced a unique ecosystem that is now home to the highest concentration of animals anywhere in Africa! Inside the crater you are surrounded by thousands of animals, including lions, zebras, buffalos and black rhinos, and the views are so striking you won’t know where to look first!

Activities:

Step into the culture, colours and traditions of the Maasai, travel back in time and see how people can be content with the simplest way of living. Find out about their survival skills and what daily life is like in and around the homes of their village. A special experience not to miss on your Tanzania trip!

Accommodations:

  • Platinum Ngorongoro Lodge by Melia

Day 8 - 10

Serengeti National Park (central west/east)

Platinum

Serengeti National Park is famous as the place for a safari, largely because of the Great Migration that moves through it. The huge stampeding herds are impressive, and so is the number of predators living here: more than four thousand lions, more than 200 cheetahs, a thousand leopards, 3500 hyenas and hundreds of wild dogs! There are of course many other beautiful animals too, and your guide will gladly look for them with you. Are you ready?

Activities:

Taking a balloon safari over the iconic Serengeti National Park turns your Tanzania safari into an experience to remember. You go up at sunrise and drift slowly across the vast Serengeti plains. The balloon glides at treetop height over the baobabs and acacias, so you can see the animals close by. Looking down on the wildlife from the balloon is a captivating and calm experience, with stunning views all around.

Day 11 - 12

Ndutu (only from end of December to March during the calving season of the Great Migration)

Platinum
Platinum

Between late December and March, the Ndutu region is the centre of the Great Migration, when more than 2 million animals come here to give birth. The region spans the southern Serengeti and the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. Thanks to the short, nutritious grass, the area is a safe place for newborn zebras and wildebeests, and also an ideal hunting ground for predators. This seasonal gathering means close wildlife encounters and non-stop activity on the plains.
