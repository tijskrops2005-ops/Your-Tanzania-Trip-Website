Discover Africa’s greatest national parks on this 7 day Tanzania Safari! You begin in Arusha and then enjoy a week of remarkable safari experiences, including visits to two of Africa’s Seven Natural Wonders!

This Tanzania safari takes you to the famous plains of the vast Serengeti. The Serengeti is full of wildlife, and its biggest attraction is without doubt the unforgettable Great Wildebeest Migration. You end the trip at the unique Ngorongoro Crater, a natural wonder with the highest concentration of wildlife in Africa.

Your professional safari guide drives you in your private 4×4, so on this private 7 day Tanzania safari you are free to travel at the pace you prefer, just the way you want. Every hotel and safari lodge on this trip has been chosen by hand by our team of Tanzania travel experts.

A question we hear a lot is when you should travel to Tanzania. Honestly, there is no single correct answer. It comes down to what you hope to see, the places you want to visit, your budget and what interests you. You can read our detailed guide on the best time to travel to Tanzania here.

 

    • Dry Season (June to October & December to February): These months are ideal for safaris and beach visits due to the pleasant weather and excellent wildlife viewing opportunities. However, expect higher hotel rates and more crowded parks and attractions.

 

    • Mid Season (March & November): This period offers a balance of moderate hotel rates and fewer tourists. The weather can be variable, and some attractions might have limited availability (due to rains), but you still get great wildlife sightings and a more relaxed safari experience.

 

    • Wet Season (April & May): Perfect for budget-conscious travelers, the low season features the lowest hotel rates and the least crowded parks. The heavy rains make the scenery vibrant and lush, but travel plans can be affected by the weather.

On a safari with us you get the chance to discover Tanzania’s remarkable wildlife and beautiful scenery. Private safaris are our speciality, so the 4×4 Landcruiser or jeep is yours alone. The well-known Ngorongoro Crater is counted among Africa's seven natural wonders. It was created two million years ago when a huge volcano erupted. The fertile lava produced a unique ecosystem that is now home to the highest concentration of animals anywhere in Africa! Inside the crater you are surrounded by thousands of animals, including lions, zebras, buffalos and black rhinos, and the views are so striking you won’t know where to look first!
