Summit Kilimanjaro on the successful 8-day Lemosho Route

Among the routes to the summit, the Lemosho route stands out as one of the most scenic and distinctive. Starting on the mountain's western flank, it sees fewer climbers, so the trek feels more secluded, and it is known for spectacular views all the way.

You need a reasonable level of fitness for this route, and it is one of the pricier options. It also takes a little longer, which works in your favour: you get the time to acclimatise well, and that is the reason it has the highest success rate.

So get ready for the trek of a lifetime and for the summit photo in which you’ll be wearing your widest smile 🙂

Getting ready for your climb: Before the climb starts you meet your guide for a briefing, with advice on clothing, packing and other useful tips to keep you as comfortable as possible on the mountain.

Day 2

Lemosho Route (1/8) | Lemosho Gate (2,100 m/6,889 ft) - Big Tree Camp (2,780 m/9,120 ft)

In the morning our guide collects you from your hotel and drives you to Londorossi Gate on the west side, the starting point of your memorable Kilimanjaro climb! Once the registration and the required climbing permit formalities are done, you set off for the route's first camp: Big Tree Camp. Make sure your rain jacket is at hand, because part of the way leads through rainforest. After lunch you go on an acclimatisation walk to Shira 1 Camp, climbing 300 metres, and then return to Big Tree Camp for dinner.

Day 3

Lemosho Route (2/8) | Big Tree Camp (2,780 m/9,120 ft) - Shira 1 Camp (3,510 m/11,515 ft)

After an early wake-up and breakfast, you climb gradually to 3,505 metres and get a stunning first look at the Shira Plateau and Kibo Volcano. The walk to the high mountain camp of Shira 1 is fairly easy and takes around 5-6 hours, which helps with acclimatisation, an essential part of a successful trek. Lunch is served when you reach camp, and afterwards you can rest in your tent or explore the area on foot.

Day 4

Lemosho Route (3/8) | Shira 1 Camp (3,510 m/11,515 ft) - Shira 2 Camp (3,890 m/12,762 ft)

Today's trek takes you from Shira 1 Camp to Shira 2 Camp. It is not a hard walk, and as you gain height you can take in the wonderful scenery and the distinctive plant life around Kilimanjaro. When the sky is clear, Shira 2 Camp is an ideal spot for views of Mount Meru (Africa's fifth peak). After lunch you take an acclimatisation walk towards Lava Tower Camp (200m higher), which improves your chances of reaching the summit and lowers the risk of altitude sickness.

Day 5

Lemosho Route (4/8) | Shira 2 Camp (3,890 m/12,762 ft) - Lava Tower (4,630 m/15,190 ft) - Barranco Camp (3,950 m/12,959 ft)

Today the Lemosho route merges with the Machame route. From Shira Camp 2 you walk to the striking Lava Tower at 4,630 m (15,190 ft). This section goes up and down a lot, yet rewards you with superb views. It is a long day, and the altitude may start to make you feel a little unwell. That is why finding the right pace matters, so your body can get used to the height. After lunch you go down to Barranco Camp, at the base of Barranco Wall.

Day 6

Lemosho Route (5/8) | Barranco Camp (3,950 m/12,959 ft) - Karanga Camp (4,040 m/13,254 ft)

Following breakfast you leave Barranco Camp and head for the Barranco Wall. Many climbers find this the best part of the whole trek. From the top of the Barranco Wall the views of the Kibo Volcano and Mount Meru are spectacular.

Next comes a tougher walk to Karanga Camp. The trail climbs and drops repeatedly, so keep a steady pace. Lunch is at the camp, and after some rest you can go on an acclimatisation walk towards Barafu Camp.

Day 7

Lemosho Route (6/8) | Karanga Camp (4,040 m/13,254 ft) - Barafu Camp (4,645 m/15,239 ft)

After breakfast (naturally!) you hike to Barafu Camp today. From this camp you set out for Uhuru Peak - the summit of Kilimanjaro! You reach camp at about lunchtime. Take a rest, and once you feel stronger, do an acclimatisation walk to Kosovo Summit Camp (4,870 m) and back. Take in the stunning views of the peaks around you and the excitement of camping at high altitude while you get ready for the final summit push at around midnight.

Day 8

Lemosho Route (7/8) | Barafu Camp (4,645 m/15,239 ft) - Uhuru Peak (5,895 m/19,340 ft) - Millenium Camp (3,820 m/12,532 ft)

At last, the big day has arrived! At around midnight you start climbing from Barafu Camp towards Uhuru Peak. The paths are good, and the hardest part is the high altitude. At the summit you can watch a beautiful sunrise, which is truly impressive at this height! After a short celebration on top (after all, you just conquered Kilimanjaro!), you begin a slow descent to the scenic Millennium Camp.

Day 9

Lemosho Route (8/8) | Millennium Camp (3,820 m/12,532 ft) - Mweka Gate (1,640 m/5,380 ft) - Hotel

Silver

The Millenium camp lies in a tropical rainforest. Waking up today, you feel relieved at the lower altitude and satisfied at having reached the summit. After breakfast, an easy walk brings you to Mweka Gate, where you leave Kilimanjaro National Park.

Your driver then takes you back to the hotel, where you can rest after all your effort. Unforgettable memories, stunning views and a real feeling of achievement are yours to keep for the rest of your life!
