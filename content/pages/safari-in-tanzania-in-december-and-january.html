What weather can you expect in Tanzania in December and January?

Asking yourself, “Is December a good time to visit Tanzania?” It certainly is! The weather in Tanzania in December and January suits a safari perfectly. Conditions are hot and dry, with temperatures between 20°C and 30°C (68°F to 86°F). Because it is the dry season, animals gather around water sources and the vegetation thins out, which makes wildlife easier to spot.

For travellers the warmth is just right: not overly hot, yet comfortable for game drives and relaxing alike. The golden sunlight also gives the landscapes a beautiful glow, which means spectacular photos. Win-win!

Why travel to Tanzania in December and January?

Besides the favourable weather, Tanzania in December and January brings unique wildlife moments and cultural events that only happen at this time of year. Here is why these months count among the best times to visit.

Great Migration in Ndutu

Between December and March, the Great Migration settles in the Ndutu region of the Southern Serengeti. This is calving season, when thousands of wildebeest and zebras bring their young into the world. With herds spread across the plains and predators prowling close by, the drama makes for a breathtaking scene. Witnessing the Great Migration in Ndutu is a highlight you will never forget on a Tanzania safari in December or January.

Whale Sharks in Mafia and Zanzibar

If you love the ocean, December ranks among the best times to see whale sharks near Mafia Island and Zanzibar. Between October and March these gentle giants are regular visitors to these waters, which makes for incredible snorkelling and diving. A Tanzania safari is easy to pair with a beach holiday, so you get wildlife and ocean adventures in one trip. The perfect combination!

Sauti za Busara Festival

Visiting Tanzania in February? Then the Sauti za Busara Festival in Zanzibar is not to be missed. This vibrant celebration of East African music and culture gathers performers from all over the region for several days of music, dancing and festivities against the historic backdrop of Stone Town. It is the ideal cultural finale to your Tanzania winter holiday.

Stunning Coastal Beaches

Once the excitement of safari is over, relax on Zanzibar’s stunning coastal beaches. Picture yourself stretched out on pristine white sand, with turquoise water and swaying palms all around. December and January temperatures are perfect for sunbathing, and the warm Indian Ocean waters are perfect for water sports like snorkeling, diving, and kite surfing. It’s the ideal way to combine adventure with relaxation for a well-rounded Tanzanian trip.

Walking Safaris

Would you like to get closer to nature? Tanzania offers unforgettable walking safaris. On foot you cross the varied landscapes of Tarangire National Park or the iconic Serengeti, where you can immerse yourself in the natural surroundings and observe wildlife up close. Intimate and authentic, this experience shows you the finer details of the Tanzanian wilderness: following animal tracks, and learning from expert guides about the local flora and fauna.
